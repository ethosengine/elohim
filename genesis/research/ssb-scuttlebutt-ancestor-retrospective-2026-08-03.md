# Secure Scuttlebutt — Ancestor Retrospective

*Not a peer confrontation (Freenet is a live peer; SSB is not) and not an ordinary cross-pollination survey. SSB is an **ancestor**: a ten-year, completed natural experiment in offline-first, subjective, gossip-replicated social infrastructure — the lineage every local-first successor (p2panda, Earthstar, Willow, PPPPP, in part Nostr and AT Protocol) explicitly descends from or reacts against. Its own architects wrote the post-mortem and left. That gives us something Freenet cannot: **outcome data** on bets we have inverted, and on a few bets we share.*

**Method:** five ssbc repos cloned and read directly (`scuttlebutt-protocol-guide`, `ssb-db2`, `ssb-server`, `ssb-ebt`, `ssb2-discussion-forum` — all registered in `research-manifest.json`), the ssbc org and successor ecosystem surveyed from the web, and a ten-axis Elohim-side grounding pass with `file:line` evidence, spot-verified against the tree. Two Sonnet researchers + one Opus architect fanned out; synthesis by the orchestrating session.

**Verification key:** ✅ verified in source (theirs or ours) · ◐ single-source, plausible · ⚠ UNVERIFIED / web-only.

---

## 1. The bet SSB made

SSB's bet is **subjective locality**. An identity is a bare Ed25519 keypair — *"An identity is an Ed25519 key pair and typically represents a person, a device, a server or a bot"* ✅ (protocol guide). Each identity publishes exactly one append-only signed hash chain; truth is per-feed; replication is the follow graph; there is deliberately **no DHT, no global namespace, no shared adjudication**. Everything hard — recovery, moderation, economics, deletion, multi-device — is pushed out of the protocol into an unwritten social layer.

Ours is the inverse. One global validating DHT namespace, identity as a socially-backstopped position rather than a key, and the social layer pulled *into* the substrate as notarized entry types — governance acts, REA commitments, stewardship grants, recovery authorities. **SSB buys simplicity and pays in un-modelled humanity; we buy modelled humanity and pay in substrate weight.**

The frame that organizes everything below: **SSB's refusals — no recovery, no economics, no governance, no global truth, no delete — are, item for item, our feature list.** SSB is the control group for our whole design. And it ran to completion.

## 2. The protocol, compressed

What SSB actually ships (all ✅ from the protocol guide and cloned source unless marked):

- **Feeds.** Per-key append-only sigchain: `previous`/`author`/`sequence`/`timestamp`/`hash`/`content`/`signature`, message ID = sha256 of the canonical JSON *including* signature. Canonicalization is defined by ECMA-262 `JSON.stringify` with `content` key order *preserved as received* — signing is bound to V8 object-enumeration semantics. An external audit called it *"literally what you will find if you google 'How Not To Sign JSON'"* ◐ (derctuo.github.io); it is the root cause of SSB's JS-implementation lock-in, and two successive binary formats (buttwoo, then minibutt's bipf+blake3) were designed to escape it.
- **Forks are terminal.** Two messages at the same sequence under one key — two devices, or old and new database both writing — fork the feed irrecoverably; peers holding one branch ignore the other forever. `ssb-db2`'s migration doc treats this as a first-class operational hazard: *"carefully apply them to avoid forking feeds into an irrecoverable state… One of them, not both, has to be considered the source of truth"* ✅. The design response is purely social discipline (one writer per key), not protocol.
- **Replication: EBT** (epidemic broadcast trees, Plumtree-derived). Peers exchange live **vector clocks** ("notes": one signed int packing replicate-flag + receive-flag + sequence), diff them, and stream missing messages. Two bandwidth disciplines worth naming: **request skipping** (persist the remote's last clock; omit feeds known to be current) and **clock partitioning** (request each feed from only one connected peer at a time, timeout to an alternate) ✅ (`ssb-ebt`). Blocking has protocol teeth at exactly one point: `ssb.ebt.block(alice, bob, true)` suppresses forwarding alice's data to bob mesh-wide ✅.
- **Hops are a client heuristic, not protocol.** Patchwork showed 2 hops, replicated 3 *"to help keep them available for others"* ✅. Any peer may replicate any feed; the horizon is a UI/scheduler policy.
- **Pubs and rooms.** A pub is an ordinary always-on peer that follows-back invite redeemers — explicitly *not* privileged infrastructure, intended to decay as members cross-follow (*"after joining a pub… the pub is no longer needed for feed visibility"* ✅). Rooms (successor) are pure encrypted-tunnel rendezvous — no feed hosting, the portal cannot read or tamper ✅.
- **Secret Handshake + box stream.** 4-message mutually-authenticating key exchange; the **network key** (caps key) partitions networks cryptographically — a different 32-byte constant is simply a different network that cannot complete a handshake with mainnet ✅. Structurally our DNA-hash namespace partition, done at the transport layer.
- **Private messages.** Envelope encryption to ≤7 recipients; recipients are concealed, so every client **trial-decrypts every private message it ever sees** ✅ — O(all traffic) curve operations as the price of metadata privacy. Private groups (box2) ship as a pluggable `ssb-encryption-format` with a `reindexEncrypted()` hook for keys added at runtime ✅ (`ssb-db2/indexes/private.js`).
- **Blobs.** Content-addressed (`&sha256`), fetched via hop-bounded **want/have flood-fill** (wants at −1/−2 forwarded, −3 dropped) ✅ — content discovery beyond direct peers without any DHT.
- **What does not exist** ✅: DHT, global namespace, delete (db2's `del()` is local-only and *breaks replication* for that feed), key rotation/recovery (*"generate a new identity and tell people"*), economics of any kind, moderation beyond follow/block, multi-device (Fusion Identity: designed, implemented, never shipped), partial replication (metafeeds: funded, specified, archived unadopted — the guide itself now files the whole apparatus under **"Archived material"**: *"the proposal never gained adoption… the SSB development community at the time had already started to scatter"* ✅).

## 3. The outcome data

Timeline in one paragraph: born 2014 (Dominic Tarr, offline-first by lived necessity); Patchwork era ~2015-2018; Manyverse (Staltz) 2018; ~8,000+ users late 2018 ◐, "estimated 30,000" at peak ⚠ (Forbes, undated); ACM ICN paper 2019; EU NGI Pointer grant funds the metafeeds/partial-replication/Fusion-Identity push 2020-21 — team disbands when the grant ends; Patchwork deprecated ~2021; the community's own successor venue (`ssb2-discussion-forum`) produces **one design doc and stalls** ✅ (the clone contains a one-line README and `minibutt.md`, nothing else — the sparseness is itself the data point); Staltz exits April 2024 (*"Seven years of donation-based FOSS income proved financially unsustainable"* ◐) right after his successor protocol PPPPP hit first proof-of-concept; Manyverse pulled from Google Play January 2025 ◐; today the org is maintained protocol-plumbing for a small community (protocol guide updated March 2026; Tilde Friends the most credibly alive client ◐).

The causes, from their own mouths (`minibutt.md`, by Anders Rune Jensen with Staltz/cblgh feedback — insider testimony, not outside criticism) ✅:

1. **Fork-fatal identity.** *"Most commonly, when restoring an identity the latest message was not properly fetched before writing new messages"* — ordinary users bricked their accounts by restoring a backup. Minibutt's first move is linear feed → DAG.
2. **The official fix died on UX, not crypto.** Fusion Identity: *"there exists an implementation. It has never been put into production, because the implementation is the easy part, the UI is a lot trickier."*
3. **The funded fix died on complexity.** Metafeeds — the NGI-Pointer-funded, formally-specified answer to multi-device and partial replication — is dismissed by its own community two years later: *"we remove a lot of complexity that [meta feeds] has."*
4. **Onboarding weight.** Initial sync *"up to an hour and a couple of gigabytes"* ◐ before a usable feed; pubs *"were never intended for discovery and onboarding of strangers"*; growth stayed invite-gated.
5. **Sustainability.** Grant-pulse funding (one NGI infusion, then dispersal) plus donation-income burnout for the load-bearing maintainer. SSB had no economic layer *in the protocol* and no durable economics *around* it — the same absence at two scales.

And what everyone — including every successor — still credits it for: offline-first that actually worked (sneakernet included), the subjective non-global social graph (*"the very concept of welcoming is subjective"*), **moderation as topology** (a public block prunes the blocker as a bridge, shrinking the blocked account's replication reach structurally, with no admin anywhere), cryptographic identity as root of trust (visibly propagated into Bluesky's DIDs, Nostr's keys, Willow's capabilities), and the solarpunk communitarian culture. The successors' reversals are just as informative: Willow reverses to *"mutability and multi-writer support over the immutability and single-writer focus of ssb"* ◐; Nostr reverses completeness itself (accepts never-knowing-if-you-have-everything for simplicity); minibutt reverses JSON, linearity, and pure-append (first-class edit/delete via tangles).

## 4. Axis by axis

| Axis | SSB | Elohim | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity | key **is** identity; no recovery, no multi-device | key is one field of a notarized social record; 5 graduated recovery authorities (`intimate→community→governance→network`, `cryptographic` orthogonal) ✅ `recovery_v2.rs:16-31`; `AgentPeerBinding` per device ✅ | **INVERSE** — deepest divergence |
| Append-only log | feed; fork is fatal and local | source chain; fork is third-party-detected — `ChainStatus::Forked` + gossiped `Warrant` ✅; integrity zomes validate domain content, not just signatures | **CONVERGENT primitive, INVERSE failure semantics** |
| Replication | follow-graph EBT; hops as client policy; no DHT | DHT (full-arc today) **plus** reach-scoped fanout (`channels_for_reach` ✅ `fanout.rs:86-93`: Private/Intimate→DirectOnly … Commons→Kad+Gossip) + REA-commitment-gated placement ◐ | **PARTIAL** — both scope replication socially; their currency is *follows*, ours is *declared reach + economic commitment* |
| Edge | pubs (always-on ordinary peers, intended to decay); rooms (blind tunnels) | doorway: projects from DHT, owns nothing, users switch freely ◐ (`infrastructure_integrity/src/lib.rs:83-86`); T1-T4 participation tracks | **CONVERGENT function, INVERSE ownership** — and their trap is our live risk (§6) |
| Visibility | subjective hop distance | declared 8-level notarized reach ordinal, bilateral-consent gates, `openness()` not derived `Ord` ✅ `epr/src/reach.rs` | **INVERSE** — no hops analog exists in our tree |
| Moderation/governance | unilateral, public, permanent follow/block; no governance layer | no peer-blocklist primitive at all ◐; private TTL'd `AttentionTending` + k-anonymous aggregates; a whole notarized mishpat DNA (precedent, challenge, appeal, consent tallies) ◐ | **INVERSE** on every dimension |
| Economy | none — the defining refusal | full VF/REA entry types incl. infra-native actions `custody-blob`/`serve-blob`/`operate-doorway` ◐; hREA DNA reserved-not-installed, VF bridge fixtures ✅-honest | **INVERSE** — but theirs is shipped-absence, ours is partially-inert presence |
| Private content | envelope encryption + box2 groups, in daily use for a decade | **unbuilt** — Envelope signs, never encrypts ✅; reach is a SQL ACL; blobs plaintext at rest ◐; own docs: *"entire encryption layer unbuilt"* ◐ | **SSB AHEAD, decisively** |
| Offline | delay-tolerant to the point of sneakernet; shipped | Automerge CRDT plane, Tauri sidecar, household-nodes floor ◐; **zero sneakernet analog** (grep-verified ✅); minutes-to-hours degraded-mode time constants | **CONVERGENT intent, SSB ahead on execution** |
| Content addressing | sigchain + blobs (2 namespaces) | DNA hash / action hash / EPR CID / bytes (4 namespaces); CID excludes `proof`/`supersededBy` ✅ | **CONVERGENT split**, ours one tier deeper |

Three axes deserve prose.

**Identity is where the experiment's verdict is clearest.** SSB's single-keypair apex produced, in production, exactly the failures our imagodei design predicts: bricked accounts from device restores, an unshippable multi-device retrofit, and *"tell people to use their new one instead"* as the entire recovery story. Deeper: an apex-keypair model has nowhere to put the child, the ward, or the coerced spouse — SSB doesn't fail that test, it never takes it. Our `INALIENABLE_FEATURES` (six capabilities no coordinator can disable ✅ `stewardship.rs:110-118`) and authority-basis/appeal lifecycle are precisely the un-modelled humanity SSB priced out. The ten-year outcome data says the price was the network.

**The fork lesson generalizes past identity.** Every per-agent-chain system has this failure mode; SSB shows it is the *civilization-ending* one, because it strikes exactly when a user behaves reasonably (restores a backup, adds a device). We hold the two structural answers SSB lacked — third-party fork detection with warrants, and notarized device bindings — but our "exactly one writer per chain" discipline deserves the same paranoia ssb-db2's migration doc models, and a regression scenario (§5).

**Replication-follows-relationship is a genuine convergence with a different denominator.** EBT scoping by follows and our reach-scoped fanout + commitment-gated placement are the same instinct — *don't replicate the world, replicate your relationships* — but their edge is affective (who you follow) and ours is contractual (what reach the author declared × what custody a peer has committed via REA). The affective version demonstrably worked at small scale and demonstrably capped growth (onboarding needed a pub to synthesize relationships). The contractual version is our bet that the same scoping can price custody honestly — noting honestly that key parts (salvage capacity flag, earned-reach escalation) are built-but-inert today ◐.

## 5. What we take (ranked by leverage)

1. **Envelope encryption's existence proof — and box2's integration shape.** The sharpest finding of the whole engagement: the thing SSB shipped that we have not is exactly the thing they could make simple. Our own assessment says *"entire encryption layer unbuilt"*; our reach gate is an ACL over SQLite; and the P2P reach classifier **fails open** on DB-pool errors — verified verbatim, `reach_authorization.rs` returns `true` with a warning at Stage 1 ✅. Two concrete imports: (a) treat fail-closed as the classifier's contract the way `epr_service.rs`'s HTTP path already does; (b) when we build `KeyEnvelope`, steal `ssb-db2`'s *pluggable encryption-format + reindex-on-new-key* interface (`installEncryptionFormat` / `reindexEncrypted()` ✅) — it cleanly separates "which messages can I decrypt *now*" from the log itself, which is exactly the shape a late-added reader-key substrate needs.
2. **EBT's bandwidth disciplines for the dataplane.** Request skipping (persist the remote's last vector clock; omit currently-synced heads) and clock partitioning (ask one peer per head, timeout to alternates) are directly applicable to our carried-bytes/evidence-supply work — the same problem Freenet's 53.7%-anti-entropy finding names, solved with two small mechanisms a decade ago. Our head-announcement gossip currently has no request-skipping analog ◐.
3. **The UX-kills-crypto law.** Fusion Identity: implemented, never shipped, *"the UI is a lot trickier."* Our social-threshold recovery is the same class of ceremony — multi-party, emotionally loaded, rare enough that no one rehearses it. The take: the recovery flows' a2o scenarios must specify the *ceremony* (who taps what, what the custodian sees, what the panicked user sees), not just the share math; and the unbuilt `shamir_combine` leg ✅-honest in `recovery/mod.rs` is the *easy* part of that work.
4. **Sneakernet as a held capability.** SSB actually moved logs on USB sticks; we have zero offline-transfer story (grep-verified ✅) despite a household-nodes floor doctrine that is *thematically* sneakernet-adjacent. A held/backlog candidate: offline export/import bundle at the storage layer (heads + bytes + provenance), testable entirely on household-nodes.
5. **Blob want/have flood-fill.** Hop-bounded want propagation (−1/−2 forwarded, −3 dropped) is a cheap content-discovery relay for the household mesh — lighter than a Kad lookup, bounded by construction, and consonant with "replication follows relationship." Candidate for the T3-spoke tier where full Kad is too heavy.
6. **"Free listening" and "near moderation" as vocabulary.** Their names for subjective horizon and moderation-as-topology are better than ours; the concepts validate reach/standing over admin takedown. Import the words.
7. **Canonicalization as negative example.** Signing bytes defined by a runtime's object-enumeration order locked SSB into one language for a decade. Our EPR CID discipline (dag-cbor canonical bytes, mutable fields excluded from the hash ✅) is the correct inversion — this survey is the evidence to cite when someone proposes "just sign the JSON."

## 6. What we watch (their failure modes, live in our tree)

- **The pub trap is our doorway trap.** Pubs were *designed* to decay and became de-facto infrastructure anyway, because intent-to-decay was never measured. Our doorways provably don't own data ◐, but the alpha fabric routes browsers to `doorway.elohim.host`, doorway reads pin fixed PRIMARY conductors, and the genesis bootstrap pair is operator-run. The SSB lesson: add a **decay probe** — what fraction of participation would survive doorway loss? — rather than trusting the architecture's intent. Structurally escapable is not escaped.
- **Onboarding weight is the same disease as full-arc.** SSB's hour-and-gigabytes initial sync capped organic growth at tens of thousands. Our RAM-∝-corpus full-arc conductors (arc factor hard-clamped to {0,1} upstream ✅-cited) and hours-scale divergent-anchor catch-up class are the same ceiling wearing different clothes. SSB is the empirical answer to "how far does a network get when joining is expensive."
- **Funded-spec-nobody-adopts is our built-but-inert.** Metafeeds (funded, specified, archived) rhymes uncomfortably with our own `salvage_capacity_enabled: false` in zero manifests, earned-reach with one caller, hREA reserved-not-installed — and with Freenet's `enable_metering: false`. Three projects, same pathology: **capability that exists in text but not in behavior reads as shipped and isn't.** The flag-with-no-reader lint from the Freenet engagement covers this class; SSB adds the sociological variant — a spec whose *community* never arrives is equally dead.
- **Sustainability is not a feature request.** SSB died of un-modelled economics twice: none in the protocol, none around the maintainers. We are the project whose thesis says the economic layer is load-bearing — which makes shefa's current fixture-state (VF bridge returning canned data ✅) an existential gap, not a backlog item. The control group's result is in.

## 7. What we leave behind, and why

- **Key-is-identity.** Ten years of outcome data: bricked accounts, no recovery, no place for dependents or coercion. Our five-layer recovery + inalienable-features design is the direct inversion; SSB is its justification.
- **Fork-fatal linearity without third-party detection.** Keep the append-only primitive (we share it); refuse the "fork = silent death" semantics. Ours is detected, warranted, adjudicable.
- **Trial-decryption private messaging.** Take the *existence* of envelope encryption (§5.1), leave the mechanism: O(all-traffic) trial decryption and a 7-recipient cap don't survive scale, and our reach-scoped fanout means private content should never ride broadcast channels at all (`Private|Intimate → DirectOnly` ✅).
- **No-delete absolutism.** The sickness-diary testimony ◐ (a user un-publishing their own health posts in fear) is what permanence-as-virtue costs real people. Our TTL'd `AttentionTending` ("*prevent permanent moats*"), reach tiers, and supersedeBy pointers are the deliberate softening; SSB shows why the hard version harms.
- **Unilateral public permanent block.** Public blocks worked as topology at SSB's scale but are a permanent social wound with no appeal, no expiry, no restoration path — the punitive shape our Mishpat framing rejects. We keep the *structural* insight (moderation reshapes replication, not a takedown queue) in reach/standing form.
- **Hops as visibility metric.** Subjective graph distance can't express "commons," can't be consented to bilaterally, and made moderation and replication policy inseparable from UI heuristics. The declared reach ordinal is strictly more expressive.
- **Runtime-defined canonical bytes and single-implementation coupling** (§5.7).

## 8. Verdict

SSB is the most valuable kind of ancestor: one that ran the inverse of our bet to completion and published the failure analysis. Where it failed for reasons *intrinsic to the bet* — key-is-identity, fork-fatality, un-modelled economics, no recovery — our inversions are validated by its outcome, not just argued. Where it failed for *extrinsic* reasons — funding pulses, JS lock-in, UX-starved crypto — the lessons transfer as process discipline. And on the two axes where it beat us — shipped envelope encryption and shipped sneakernet — it beat us precisely where simplicity was achievable, which is the most actionable criticism this survey produces: our encryption layer and offline-transfer story are not blocked on hard problems; they are blocked on being scheduled.
