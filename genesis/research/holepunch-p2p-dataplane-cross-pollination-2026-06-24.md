# Holepunch Cross-Pollination — June 2026

**[Holepunch](https://github.com/holepunchto)** (the company behind the **Hypercore** protocol stack and the **[Pear](https://docs.pears.com/)** runtime) is the most mature fully-P2P application stack in the JS ecosystem — append-only logs, a holepunching DHT, multi-writer convergence, blob/FS composition, a reliable-UDP transport, and a real shipped app ([Keet](https://keet.io/)) built entirely on it, with **no servers**. It is the closest external mirror the protocol has for its **p2p substrate** — the data plane (atlas §3.10), the NAT-traversal/transport layer, and the confidentiality plane (§3.13). Where the [Hypha-DAO survey](hypha-dao-autonomous-collectives-cross-pollination-2026-06-24.md) mirrored the **collective**, and the [Distributed Press survey](hypha-distributed-press-cross-pollination-2026-06-23.md) mirrored the **doorway**, Holepunch mirrors **the substrate itself** — how bytes move, how peers find each other across NATs, and how content stays available without leaking read access.

This survey is a green-team / red-team adjudicated against the protocol's **current build-state** (grounded from source on 2026-06-24, not from the durable spec) and its design gates ([p2p-design-gate](../../.claude/skills/p2p-design-gate/SKILL.md), the [truth-vs-data-plane line](../docs/content/elohim-protocol/architecture/2026-06-21-elohim-seam-map-concern-routing.md), [P1 reconciliation-controller](../../.claude/memory/project_principle_p1_reconciliation_controller.md), [identity-sovereignty guard](../../.claude/memory/feedback-identity-sovereignty-ontology-guard.md), the [p2p-vs-federation layer split](../../.claude/memory/feedback_p2p_vs_federation_layer_vocabulary.md)).

The one-line verdict: **Holepunch is the most architecturally-aligned external P2P stack we have surveyed — it independently rebuilt the protocol's entire data plane — and that is *exactly* why the integrity-vs-validity line must be drawn most carefully here. Alignment is the seduction, not the safety.** Borrow its transport and its blind-hosting patterns; the trust layer is the protocol's own. Said as one sentence for outreach and design: **Holepunch trusts the writer's key; Elohim trusts the network's validation — integrity is what a Hypercore proves, validity is what only the DHT can.**

This survey **advances past** the two existing Holepunch notes rather than repeating them: the [`hypercore-holepunch-prior-art.md`](../../steward/node/research/hypercore-holepunch-prior-art.md) module note is a single-primitive brainstorm capture (Hypercore = integrity-only source-chain), and the Distributed Press survey saw Hypercore through the *fediverse/publishing* lens. This one is **org-wide, gate-adjudicated, and seam-routed** — the whole Holepunch column run through the source-of-truth classifier against grounded build-state.

## Subjects surveyed (org-wide, confidence-graded)

Each was web-cited against primary sources (holepunchto repos, docs.pears.com, the BEP44 spec, the Pears engineering blog) with confidence grades; load-bearing claims were adversarially verified, including at the `lib/persistent.js` / `MESSAGE.md` source level. Corrections the verification pass caught are kept inline, not laundered.

1. **Hypercore** — a secure append-only signed log; per-entry **Blake2b Merkle tree** enables *verified-block streaming* (verify one block mid-stream against the signed root without the whole log). **Sparse replication** ("download only the blocks you need" = "replication follows relationship"). Three **separable credentials**: public key (verify/identify) ≠ `discoveryKey` (a hash of the public key — announce/find peers *without leaking read capability*) ≠ block-encryption key (at-rest). ⚠️ *Correction:* not strictly single-writer — Hypercore manifests support **multisig `signers` + `quorum`** ([hyper-multisig](https://github.com/holepunchto/hyper-multisig)); but quorum is **threshold authorization of named keys, not network validation of content**. *(HIGH; the Holochain contrast is architectural synthesis, MEDIUM.)*
2. **HyperDHT + Hyperswarm** — a Kademlia DHT (`dht-rpc`, BitTorrent-mainline family) with **immutable/mutable records** (BEP44-style: ed25519 signature + monotonic `seq` only — *no semantic validation*; source-confirmed at `lib/persistent.js`). Discovery by 32-byte **topic** (`discoveryKey`). **UDP holepunching is the namesake**: *every DHT node is a potential introducer* — it observed both peers' external IP:port and relays the punch metadata, then both fire UDP simultaneously to prime NAT mappings; un-holepunchable peers fall back to ≤3 relay addresses. Pears reports **~95% direct / ~5% relayed** on consumer networks (above libp2p's ~70% measured). *(HIGH; "mainline BEP44 lineage" framing MEDIUM.)*
3. **Autobase** — combines *multiple* single-writer cores into one logical multi-writer structure **with no global consensus**. ⚠️ *Load-bearing distinction:* it is a **deterministic LINEARIZATION of a causal DAG** (each node references its causal heads; a linearizer produces a total order, ties broken by input key; an `apply` reducer materializes a *view*), **NOT a commutative CRDT**. Forks heal by **truncate-and-replay** above a quorum-signed `signedLength` checkpoint. *Indexer-quorum signatures bind to **order**, never to data-type validity; no BFT claimed.* `apply` must be pure-deterministic or peers diverge. *(HIGH.)*
4. **The composition stack** — "**everything is a hypercore**": **Corestore** (factory: deterministic `name → keypair` from one master key; manages/replicates many cores), **Hyperbee** (append-only B-tree KV → free `version`/`checkout`/`createDiffStream` time-travel), **Hyperdrive** (a POSIX FS = a Hyperbee of metadata + a Hyperblobs of content), **Hyperblobs** (byte-range-addressable blobs — seek without full pull). One uniform log substrate; replication/discovery/encryption written **once** at the core layer, inherited upward. *(HIGH.)*
5. **Transport + crypto** — **UDX** ("reliable, multiplexed, congestion-controlled streams over UDP… *no handshakes, no encryption, no features* — good for P2P"): a pure reliability layer riding an *already-punched* path (zero in-transport handshake RTT because auth/discovery happened in the DHT above). **secret-stream** (Noise **XX** handshake → libsodium secretstream AEAD; exposes `handshakeHash` for channel-binding). **protomux** (named-protocol demux; ⚠️ *correction:* protomux does **NOT** itself preserve message boundaries — framing comes from secret-stream's length-prefixing). *(HIGH; "UDX-beats-QUIC" advantage architecturally clear but unbenchmarked, MEDIUM.)*
6. **Blind peering / blind mirroring** — an always-on node keeps a core **available** by replicating + serving its **ciphertext blocks** while holding only the `discoveryKey`, **not the read/encryption key**. Untrusted infra (a VPS, a hosting service) provides **availability without read access**. Shipped as `blind-peer` / `blind-peering` (XOR-distance mirror selection) / `blind-encryption-sodium`; productized in Keet. Gives availability + confidentiality-from-host; gives **NOT** validity, quorum, or censorship-resistance (a host can withhold by `discoveryKey`) — and no durability guarantee (best-effort mirror). *(HIGH on the credential model; the "stores ciphertext, cannot decrypt" guarantee is partly synthesis from the credential separation, flagged MEDIUM.)*
7. **Pear · Bare · Keet** — **Pear** ships *apps themselves over Hypercore* (`pear stage/seed/release/run`; versioned `<fork>.<length>.<key>`; auto-propagating updates; no app store). **Bare** is Holepunch's minimal embeddable JS runtime (Node alternative; tier-1 iOS/Android prebuilds). **Keet** is the worked proof — accountless, serverless, E2E-encrypted chat/video with Autobase-backed rooms and holepunched media. *(HIGH.)*

## Grounded Elohim reality (what the borrows are adjudicated against)

This is the survey's distinguishing move — verdicts are routed against *current build-state*, not the spec:

- **Peer-hoster CRDT (§3.10) — LIVE-WIRED.** The engine is **Automerge** (commutative op-log, sled-backed at `/data/sync.sled`), exchanging `have_heads`-filtered op-deltas over a libp2p request-response loop (`doc_store.rs`, `p2p/mod.rs`). ⚠️ The grounding's "`sync_manager` is `None`, no first writer" claim was **refuted by verification**: `SyncManager` *is* constructed (`main.rs:1996` / `p2p/mod.rs:1706`) and a write route exists (`POST /sync/v1/{app}/docs/{doc}/changes`, `http.rs:3294`). The *real* gap is narrower: **no frontend Automerge client drives writes**, and **the doorway intentionally excludes `/sync/*`** from its client-facing route manifest. The substrate is live; the client leg is absent.
- **Transport + NAT — built but pinned.** libp2p **0.54.1** (storage: no QUIC feature; `steward/node`: +QUIC). **relay / DCUtR / AutoNAT compiled but DORMANT** — no relay-reservation dial wired, `P2P_BOOTSTRAP_NODES` pinned to `svc.cluster.local`, no `ANNOUNCE_ADDRS`, mDNS off in prod. **iroh 0.92 present but DORMANT** via an env-name drift (`TRANSPORT_BACKEND` vs `ELOHIM_TRANSPORT_BACKEND`) + invalid `"dual-stack"` variant → silently stays libp2p. **The one WAN-operational P2P plane is the Holochain conductor's** kitsune2/tx5/go-pion stack (peerCount ~13) — but it traverses NAT through a **single dedicated SBD signal relay** (`wss://signal.doorway-alpha.elohim.host`) = a SPOF.
- **Confidentiality (§3.13) — almost entirely SPEC-ONLY.** `BlobStore::store → fs::write` is plaintext; no encryption-at-rest in any production path. `services/private_replica.rs` is a **proof-of-concept** (encrypt-then-shard: random DEK → XSalsa20-Poly1305 → RS(4,7); per-reader DEK sealed to an **X25519** key via `crypto_box_seal` = the future `KeyEnvelope` stand-in). The **blind-hosting property is mathematically proven in tests** (`reader_with_envelope_recovers_custodian_cannot`) — but unbuilt in production. **Blocker:** Holochain agent keys are **ed25519, not X25519**; the key-conversion substrate does not exist. Gated to weave **Wave C**.
- **Blob / quilt (§3.10/§3.1) — full-copy, not erasure.** RS(4,7) encoder exists + unit-tested, but `shard_manifest_backfill` hardcodes `parity_shard_count: 0` → production is single-copy replication. `TierController` absent (tier always `None`). Byte-mobility = gossipsub **inventory** (metadata) + `blob_fetch::race_fetch` (on-demand, sha256-verified pull). **Live operational fact:** "*inventory count=3430 / blob bytes=0 after 36h*" — inventory without bytes; the fan-out is dead because **placement policy is unbuilt**.
- **Steward desktop (§3.2/3.3) — conventional packaging, no auto-update.** Tauri 2.x `.deb`/AppImage via GitHub Release; **no `tauri-plugin-updater`**, manual release-only. Conductor embedded + storage sidecar :8090; no P2P app delivery. The [hub-optional floor](../../.claude/memory/project_hub_optional_floor.md) holds (a laptop is a full DHT peer).

## What we'd lift (green team)

Per the [weave-epic "compose-don't-fork" finding](../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-06-20-weave-epic-arc-design.md), **none of these mints a new DHT entry type** — they are transport/discovery patterns, projection-mechanics, or recognition-fold kernels. Each names its **Elohim home · seam · source-of-truth class**.

### The TOP-3 highest-leverage (ranked — each targets a *live* gap)

1. **Distributed-introducer signaling** *(from HyperDHT)* — Holepunch's "every DHT node is a potential introducer" is the exact distributed-signaling model that could retire the protocol's single dedicated **SBD signal relay SPOF**. Harvest any already-connected swarm peer's AutoNAT address-observation as the DCUtR rendezvous instead of waiting on dedicated relays. **Home:** `libp2p-discovery` skill + the WAN-NAT three-plane backlog (Gap A: relay/DCUtR built-but-unwired). **Seam:** transport-nat. **Class C** (peer-observed addresses, nothing notarized). **No new entry type.**
2. **Per-block verified streaming + byte-range fetch** *(from Hypercore + Hyperblobs)* — `blob_fetch::race_fetch` today verifies **whole-blob** sha256 only; there is no per-block Merkle proof for the 16–64 MB chunked path or RS shards, and no byte-range (seek-without-full-pull) fetch idiom. Lift Hypercore's `proof()`/`verifyFullyRemote()` per-block verification + Hyperblobs' `{byteOffset, blockOffset, blockLength, byteLength}` addressing. **Home:** `elohim/elohim-storage/src/p2p/blob_fetch.rs` + `sharding.rs`. **Seam:** peer-hoster / blob-quilt. **Class C** (verification is a transport property; the CID stays the address). **No new entry type.**
3. **XOR-distance mirror-selection as a placement policy** *(from blind-peering)* — this is not a nicety: it is the **missing placement policy** that would make inventory gossip actually move bytes (the dead `parity_shard_count:0` fan-out). Select multiple deterministic-distance always-on holders to spread custody. **Home:** `elohim/elohim-storage/src/reconcile/custody.rs` (the reconciler that already emits `placement-gap`). **Seam:** blob-quilt. **Class:** placement *intent* is the already-existing **`custody-blob` Mishpat commitment (class A)**, the byte copy is C; see [REA compute-commitment primitive](../../.claude/memory/project_rea_compute_commitment_primitive.md). **No new entry type.**

### The rest

4. **Three-way credential separation (verify-key ≠ locate-token ≠ at-rest-key)** *(Hypercore / blind-peering)* — the strongest *confidentiality* borrow. Today inventory gossip ships the **bare hash**, so holding the locate-token implies you can request the bytes. Hypercore's `discoveryKey` (derived from the public key, reveals no read capability) is the pattern for "find a private blob's custodians without exposing read access." Maps onto the protocol's split: CID/sha256 (verify+address) · inventory-topic membership (locate) · per-reader sealed DEK (read). **Home:** `services/private_replica.rs` (the future `KeyEnvelope`) + `p2p/inventory_gossip.rs`. **Seam:** confidentiality §3.13. **Class B2** (agent-scoped read-capability + attestation). ⚠️ **No mint *today*** — but the `KeyEnvelope` is *named-but-undesigned*; when built it **must pass p2p-design-gate** (likely B2). This does **not** pre-clear the future entry.
5. **Blind-hosting as independent confirmation of "encrypt-then-erasure-code."** Holepunch's blind peer (ciphertext blocks + `discoveryKey`, no read key) validates the protocol's *already-chosen* `private_replica.rs` ordering (encrypt → RS-shard → custody). External corroboration that "availability delegated, confidentiality + verification retained client-side" is convergent, not idiosyncratic. **Home:** `private_replica.rs` + [tiered-quilt-stewardship](../docs/content/elohim-protocol/architecture/2026-05-11-tiered-quilt-stewardship-design.md). **Seam:** §3.13. (DEFER on *operational* readiness — see red-team.)
6. **Channel-binding via handshake-hash** *(secret-stream)* — `secret-stream` exposes a per-session `handshakeHash` for binding application messages to the transport session. The protocol's `identity_handshake.rs` has **no** channel binding (its anti-replay is a ±5 min timestamp window); binding the signed `AgentPeerBinding` to *this* Noise session would harden the `STAGE1_SIGNATURE_SENTINEL` → real cross-signature path. **Home:** the [`agent-peer-binding-cross-signed-proof`](../data/timeline/backlog/agent-peer-binding-cross-signed-proof.md) backlog (Gap D). **Seam:** transport-nat. **Class C.** **No new entry type.**
7. **Causal-linearization discipline for op-log *projections*** *(Autobase)* — the Automerge plane is commutative (no ordering needed), but the **projection** layer (storage SQLite, EPR nav views) re-derives ordered views with no formal "truncate-suffix-and-replay above a stable checkpoint" contract. Autobase's "nodes never precede their references; reorg only above the stable point" + "`apply` must be a pure deterministic reducer — non-determinism = divergence" + **fast-forward** (jump a lagging peer to a signed checkpoint instead of replaying every node) are portable disciplines. **Home:** [P1 reconciliation-controller](../../.claude/memory/project_principle_p1_reconciliation_controller.md) + `reconcile/controller.rs`; `automerge-sync` skill. **Seam:** peer-hoster-crdt. **Class C.** **No new entry type.**
8. **App-delivery over the substrate as a versioned content-addressed log** *(Pear)* — the steward's clearest unfilled gap is **no auto-update at all**. A steward release is already a content-addressed blob-set (`bafkrei…` is the live wire address via `BlobStore`/`race_fetch`); version-pin it and fetch peer-to-peer with sha256-verification. Channel-keyed links (dev vs production) map onto the protocol's own [per-host deploy-lag reality](../../.claude/memory/project_prod_main_lag_vs_alpha_dev.md). **Home:** `steward/device/Jenkinsfile` + the [prior-art note's open "self-update leg"](../../steward/node/research/hypercore-holepunch-prior-art.md). **Seam:** OS/packaging §3.3. **Class C** (a release is a pinned blob-set, not notarized state). **No new entry type.**
9. **Layer-separation validation + `atomize` atomic batch** *(transport-crypto / Hypercore 11)* — Holepunch's UDX/secret-stream/protomux three-layer split *validates the bet the protocol already made* (`noise::Config` + `yamux` are decoupled from TCP/QUIC transport — which is *why* the `p2p`/`p2p-iroh` dual-stack parity tests are sound). And Hypercore 11's `atomize`/`atom.flush()` cross-core atomic batch is a pattern for the known consistency hazard between the Automerge sled store and the SQLite projection. **Home:** `p2p_iroh/README.md`; `doc_store.rs` + reconcile controllers. **Seam:** transport-nat / peer-hoster. **Class C.** **No new entry type.**

## Where our paths diverge (red team)

### REJECT (lines the protocol won't cross)

1. **Any Hypercore / HyperDHT / Autobase artifact as a truth primitive — REJECT (the cardinal line).** Guard: **truth-vs-data-plane + no-global-validation.** Source-verified across the column: a Hypercore block is valid iff the writer signed it (Blake2b Merkle + signature; content-semantic validation "left to higher-level applications"); a HyperDHT record passes only BEP44 ed25519+seq checks (`lib/persistent.js` — nothing inspects `value`'s meaning); an Autobase `signedLength` quorum binds to **order**, never type, with **no BFT**; even Hypercore's multisig quorum is *threshold authorization, not network validation*. Single-writer-signed (or quorum-authorized) ≠ network-validated. **A Holochain entry's validity is a network property enforced by every validating authority; nothing in Holepunch is.** These move bytes beside iroh/libp2p — never notarize.
2. **The keypair-as-global-address / "no accounts, your device = your identity" model as identity apex — REJECT.** Guard: **identity-sovereignty.** HyperDHT's ed25519-pubkey-as-address and Keet's accountless model are the exact self-sovereign apex framing the protocol subordinates to community/institutional governance (imago-dei backstop). Accept keypairs only as **transport-layer key material (Category C)**, downstream of and evictable relative to the notarized `agent_cid`.
3. **Tether/token-economy gravity — REJECT the framing.** Guard: **DHT-is-notary-not-bank.** Holepunch's P2P-plus-crypto-payment worldview imports fungible-balance / metered-global-state assumptions the protocol forecloses. Borrow the transport; never the economic frame. (Sibling line to Hypha's transferable-token reject.)
4. **Porting any Holepunch module as code — REJECT.** Guard: **JS-vs-Rust-port-cost.** The substrate is Rust — Automerge, libp2p 0.54.1, iroh 0.92, in-tree `noise`/`yamux`/QUIC. Adopting `hypercore`/`hyperdht`/`autobase`/UDX means a second runtime (Node/Bare) in the dataplane that buys nothing the live stack lacks. Borrow patterns, reject codebases. *(This is what makes the survey safe: the alignment is at the pattern level, where it can be lifted without the runtime.)*
5. **HyperDHT / blind-peering's `discoveryKey` rendezvous as a doorway/federation primitive — REJECT.** Guard: **p2p-vs-federation vocabulary.** "Announce-without-read" is a fine *data-plane* locate token; it must never become how doorways find each other or how content authenticity is judged. The doorway is not a P2P participant and must never be a blind peer.

### DEFER (genuine, but post-v0 or substrate-blocked)

6. **Operational blind hosting end-to-end — DEFER (not BORROW-ready).** The math is proven in tests, but no live custodian-dispatch path exists, `parity_shard_count:0` is hardcoded, and the whole encryption layer is **blocked on the ed25519→X25519 key-conversion substrate** (R6). **Unblocks when:** that substrate lands + the `KeyEnvelope` entry passes p2p-design-gate + RS(4,7) leaves test-only. Gated to weave Wave C. *(Kill any framing that reads as shippable.)*
7. **UDX as a transport — DEFER (likely permanent).** Genuinely elegant (drives the 95% number via zero-handshake-RTT on an already-punched path), but the protocol already carries QUIC in `steward/node` and iroh-QUIC as the long-term blob plane. **Unblocks only if** measured iroh-QUIC rates underperform UDX on real consumer NAT *after* the iroh cutover (Gap B env-drift) — and the answer is more likely "tune iroh-QUIC," not "port UDX."
8. **Bare as a mobile runtime — DEFER (bordering reject).** Tier-1 iOS/Android prebuilds are attractive (the protocol has no mobile target wired), but the steward is Tauri+Rust+`tauri-plugin-holochain`; **Tauri mobile is the route**, not a JS runtime that would require reimplementing the substrate.
9. **Autobase multi-writer linearization — DEFER (redundant).** The protocol *already* has commutative-CRDT multi-writer convergence (Automerge, live-wired, reachable on the libp2p request-response path) — no causal-graph linearizer or LWW tie-break needed. Autobase's `apply` reducer is interesting only if a future *non-commutative ordering* workload appears that Automerge can't express; and even then, **governed membership and finality belong in the DHT-notary plane**, not in the data structure.

## The one hard rule (the integrity-boundary invariant)

If any Holepunch piece is ever adopted, it crosses exactly one boundary — and this is the consolidated form of every REJECT above:

> **Any Holepunch piece may MOVE or LOCATE bytes (data plane) but may never DECIDE whether bytes are TRUE (truth plane). Adopt the transport, never the attestation.** Every adoption must classify as Class C (operational) or B/B2 (agent-scoped projection) and mint **no new DHT entry type**. If a borrow would let a signed-by-writer (or quorum-authorized) artifact stand in for a network-validated entry, it has crossed the line and becomes a REJECT — Autobase's quorum, HyperDHT's BEP44 records, and Hypercore's multisig all fail this test and stay strictly data-plane.

This is the analog of the Hypha survey's lossy-bridge invariant: the safety property is that the *seductive* part (a "DHT," a "signed log," a "quorum checkpoint") is exactly the part that must be dropped at the boundary.

## The sharpest convergence, and the sharpest divergence

**Convergence (why this survey was worth doing):** Holepunch is a non-Holochain, decade-deep, production-shipping stack that *independently rebuilt the protocol's entire data plane* — append-only signed logs, sparse "replication-follows-relationship," holepunching NAT traversal, multi-writer convergence, content-addressed blob composition, and encrypted blind hosting. When an outsider reaches the same substrate under different constraints, the substrate is structural, not idiosyncratic. Its single most valuable contribution lands precisely on the protocol's two *live operational* wounds: the **single SBD signal-relay SPOF** (→ distributed-introducer signaling) and the **dead inventory fan-out** (→ XOR-distance placement). Neither prior Holepunch note touched either.

**Divergence (the rhyme that names the whole bet):** both stacks ship a "secure append-only signed log" and a "DHT." But Holepunch's DHT is a **discovery/holepunch rendezvous with BEP44 integrity**, and its log is **trust-the-writer's-key**; the protocol's DHT is a **validating graph DHT** where correctness is enforced by every authority. **Same primitives, opposite trust root.** *Holepunch trusts the writer's key; Elohim trusts the network's validation — integrity is what a Hypercore proves, validity is what only the DHT can.* That contrast — and the fact that the *closer* the architectural alignment, the *more* carefully the line must be drawn — is the single most useful sentence this survey produces.
