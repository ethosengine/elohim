# p2panda Cross-Pollination — August 2026

**[p2panda](https://github.com/p2panda)** is the SSB-lineage project that took the ancestor's post-mortem seriously and rebuilt as **data-type-agnostic Rust building blocks over iroh** — ten composable crates (core, store, sync, net, discovery, auth, encryption, spaces, stream, blobs) plus a facade, bring-your-own-CRDT, and a real shipped app ([Reflection](https://github.com/p2panda/reflection), a GNOME collaborative editor). Where [Holepunch](holepunch-p2p-dataplane-cross-pollination-2026-06-24.md) mirrors our substrate and [Freenet](epr:freenet-peer-confrontation-2026-07-27) confronts our bet, p2panda is the **sibling craftsman**: materially the closest stack to ours in the whole field — Rust, iroh, CBOR, Ed25519, BLAKE3, SQLite, append-only logs, tokio — which makes it the one project whose *engineering discipline* transfers almost without translation. That discipline is the survey's special axis, at the operator's direction: we struggle with a modularity that would compose well and play nicely with others; p2panda has it; this survey extracts it as adoptable standards, adjudicated against a grounded audit of our own workspace.

The one-line verdict: **p2panda's highest-value export to us is not a protocol primitive but a proof: a three-person team dissolved their own monolithic node (aquadoggo, now deprecated by its authors) into building blocks, and the blocks outlived the framework. Our `elohim-storage` (243k LoC, 23 concerns, one 38-field struct) is our aquadoggo — and unlike them, we already have the pivot half-built: fifty-plus small crates, a live private registry, and a two-day-old exemplar (`seam-contracts`) that out-disciplines most of p2panda. We made the building blocks and kept writing the services around them instead of out of them; p2panda made every unit a building block.**

Method: `p2panda`, `aquadoggo`, and `reflection` cloned and read directly (registered in `research-manifest.json`); web research on history/funding/standards; a full Elohim-side workspace-discipline audit with `file:line` evidence. Two Sonnet researchers + one Opus architect fanned out; synthesis by the orchestrating session. **Verification key:** ✅ verified in source · ◐ single-source/plausible · ⚠ web-only/unverified.

## ⚠️ Org / lineage guard (read first)

- **`aquadoggo` is DEPRECATED by its own authors** ✅ (README badge "Maintained: no" + redirect to p2panda.org). The active center is the `p2panda/p2panda` monorepo (1,140 commits, pushed same-day at survey time, all crates at lock-step **0.7.0**, pre-1.0 and 🚧-bannered). When citing "p2panda," cite the crate collection, not the node.
- **The 2024 pivot is on record and load-bearing** ✅: *"Such a toolkit shouldn't come at the cost of a highly abstract 'monolithic' API or framework"* — developers should *"pick what they need and integrate it with minimal friction"* so the work *"contributes the most to a wider, interoperable p2p ecosystem which outlives 'framework lock-in'"* ([2024-12-06 release post](https://p2panda.org/2024/12/06/p2panda-release.html)). Bamboo logs, the GraphQL/SQL/schema node, and the proprietary CRDT were all dropped in the same move.
- **Grant-pulse funded, SSB's risk shape hedged not solved**: NGI-POINTER → NGI-ASSURE → NGI0-ENTRUST → Innovate UK → Prototype Fund, ~3 core maintainers (adz, sam, glyph) ✅. Diversified across five programs plus institutional partners (GNOME; HIRO's `rhio` in production edge micro-datacenters) — but no revenue model; the [SSB retrospective's](epr:ssb-scuttlebutt-ancestor-retrospective-2026-08-03) funding-cliff pathology remains live for them. Cite capabilities, not longevity.
- **Two invented-vs-adopted tensions, flag before citing**: (1) UCAN appears in their standards-reuse marketing, but shipped `p2panda-auth` is a **homegrown ACL-CRDT** (Pull/Read/Write/Manage over a signed membership DAG; they considered and rejected Willow's Meadowcap capabilities) ✅; (2) the `p2panda-encryption` security audit (Radically Open Security) was **announced Feb 2025 but never confirmed published** ⚠ — treat the crate as research-grounded (DCGKA, 2SM, Double Ratchet, X3DH, XChaCha20-Poly1305) but unaudited until shown otherwise.

## Subjects surveyed (compressed; full anatomy in the cloned repos)

1. **The workspace** ✅ — 10 crates + facade, **65,226 LoC total**, a clean acyclic DAG rooted in an **I/O-free `p2panda-core`** (zero tokio, zero networking, zero DB — pure data types + crypto + CBOR). Extension seams are explicit traits: `sync::Protocol`/`Manager` (transport-agnostic over generic Sink/Stream), `auth::Resolver` (pluggable conflict resolution), trait-per-domain stores (11 separate `traits.rs` storage contracts with SQLite impls behind feature gates).
2. **The discipline machinery** ✅ — workspace-level `[workspace.lints.clippy]` with every crate opting in via `[lints] workspace = true`; uniform `rust-version = "1.96"` and `edition = "2024"` across all 11 manifests; `cargo-deny` (license allowlist + justified advisory ignores) and `cargo-hack --feature-powerset` in CI; Keep-a-Changelog with per-crate prefixes; a 13-step `RELEASE.md` runbook publishing the whole workspace lock-step to crates.io; SPDX headers on 98% of files; dual MIT/Apache-2.0; **one guarded `unsafe` block in 65k LoC**; `thiserror` per-module error enums everywhere (`anyhow` appears only in the one broken crate — the idiom decays exactly where the code does).
3. **`p2panda-net`** ✅ — the only crate touching iroh (**iroh 1.0.0** + iroh-gossip 0.101; we pin `=0.92`); adds above iroh: persistent SQLite `AddressBook`, **PSI-based confidential topic discovery** (topics are secrets; discovery never leaks topic identity to unrelated peers), log-sync with catch-up semantics over fire-and-forget gossip, and **ractor actor supervision** (Erlang-style restart trees per subsystem). iroh is wrapped at a module boundary, *not* behind a transport trait — their "future mesh/radio transports" line is aspiration, and our dual libp2p/iroh stack is actually further along on having two backends.
4. **`p2panda-encryption`** ✅ — I/O-free group crypto: `data_scheme` (group symmetric encryption) + `message_scheme` (forward-secure double-ratchet messaging), 2SM key agreement (O(n) group healing), `zeroize` on secrets, fuzz-tested. MLS considered and set aside as premature for fully-decentralized p2p ✅.
5. **Composition proof — Reflection** ✅ — 3-crate layered app (`app → doc → node`); `reflection-node` depends on **the facade only** (`p2panda = "0.7.0"`), never reaching iroh/gossip/sync internals; whole node bring-up is one builder chain. CRDT is **Loro** (swapped from Automerge for docs + native undo/redo ◐). The pattern worth naming: **ephemeral gossip for presence (cursors), durable sync for content (deltas + snapshot-on-reconnect)** — two consistency tiers, one topic API.
6. **History** — Berlin festival-organizing origins (BLATT 3000 → HOFFNUNG 3000 → festival-tool, 36C3 talk 2019 ✅), SSB/DAT/Cabal/CCC founding milieu ◐, Bamboo era, aquadoggo era (GraphQL node + `fishy` schemas + materialized views ✅), 2024 pivot. The aquadoggo-era formal spec site was not carried forward — the new stack's spec is blog prose + rustdoc + source ◐.

## Grounded Elohim reality (what the verdicts adjudicate against)

Audited 2026-08-04 from source, not memory. The full report lives with the session; the load-bearing facts:

- **No root workspace.** Eight first-party workspace roots + three giant standalone services carrying **336,565 of our ~400k first-party Rust LoC** outside every shared mechanism: `elohim-storage` **243,011 LoC / 496 files / 23 distinct concerns** behind a 38-field `Option<Arc<…>>` god-struct with 27 `with_*` builders (`src/http.rs` is 15,701 lines — 2.2× our own `source-file-loc-ceiling@1` hard ceiling); `doorway-service` 80,648 (better directory-per-concern shape, still one crate, `server/http.rs` 7,365); `steward/node` 12,906 (genuinely modest).
- **The reuse-and-duplication paradox, in one dependency**: `steward/node` *already consumes elohim-storage as a library* (compiling 243k LoC incl. diesel, Shamir, CozoDB) **and then builds its own libp2p swarm anyway** — two independent `#[derive(NetworkBehaviour)]`s (`elohim-storage/src/p2p/behaviour.rs:70`, `steward/node/src/p2p/transport.rs:42`). The reuse unit is so large that reuse and duplication happen simultaneously. Health endpoints ×3, config loading ×3.
- **The transport abstraction is half-built**: 7 clean `*Backend` traits exist on the iroh side (`p2p_iroh/{epr,sync,shard,auth,view_fed}.rs`); the libp2p side implements none of them — parallel implementations with a trait seam on only one side.
- **The disciplined corner is real**: 36 crates inherit `[workspace.package]`; Nexus `cargo-internal` registry live with `elohim-epr 0.1.0` published and `brit`/`rakia` consuming cross-repo; `elohim-views`/`elohim-facings` extractions landed with byte-identical golden tests; **`crates/seam-contracts` (2026-08-02) out-disciplines p2panda** — `default = []` pulling nothing, `#![warn(missing_docs)]`, and a lockfile-reading boundary test that *fails the build* if a heavy dep enters the tree (p2panda has no equivalent).
- **The outward-facing gaps**: zero CHANGELOGs and zero release tags first-party (41 crates frozen at 0.1.0 — publish scripts idempotently no-op with nothing to publish); no `[workspace.lints]` anywhere (the `-D warnings` contract lives in a 59KB pre-push bash script instead of the manifests); **license incoherence** — AGPL-3.0 / CAL-1.0 / Apache-2.0 / none across 20 unlicensed crates, with `steward/node` (Apache) consuming `elohim-storage` (AGPL) consuming `elohim-epr` (CAL-1.0); `anyhow::Result` in `elohim-bitswap`'s public `ContentStore` trait (`behaviour.rs:59-65`); dependency-pin rationale essays that are excellent but live in one manifest each.

## What we adopt NOW (the discipline program — the operator's named axis)

None of these mints a DHT entry type; all are Class C engineering practice. Ranked by leverage-per-effort:

1. **`[workspace.lints]` + `[lints] workspace = true`** — move the lint contract from the 59KB pre-push bash into the manifests where an outside contributor (or a fresh agent) can read it. p2panda does it in 3 lines. Apply to every first-party workspace root; the hook keeps enforcing, the manifest starts *declaring*. **Home:** all 8 workspace roots + the three services. *(Hours.)*
2. **License coherence decision** — one deliberate policy: `MIT OR Apache-2.0` for outward building blocks (what the Rust ecosystem will actually consume), the copyfarleft stance (CAL-1.0/AGPL) *stated* where intentional, and the 20 unlicensed crates closed. Today our one published crate carries the license almost no consumer has evaluated — it reads as accident, not stance. **Home:** operator decision + a sweep. *(One decision + hours.)*
3. **Versioning that means something** — adopt Keep-a-Changelog (per-crate prefixes, p2panda's shape) + version bumps on publish + git tags, starting with the already-published crates (`elohim-epr`, eprfs quintet, `seam-contracts`). A consumer of `elohim-epr 0.1.0` today cannot tell January's code from August's — they differ. **Home:** `RELEASE.md` runbook at repo root modeled on p2panda's 13-step; wire into the existing idempotent publish scripts. *(A day.)*
4. **`cargo-deny` + `cargo-hack --feature-powerset` as CI gates** — we have feature-bearing crates (`seam-contracts`, `elohim-cache-core`, `elohim-peer-fabric`, storage's `p2p`/`graph-native`) and no gate proving feature combinations compile; and no license/advisory allowlist at all. Both are drop-in workflows. **Home:** the Genesis/Edge pipelines' Rust stages. *(A day.)*
5. **The extraction program — services out of building blocks** — the Opus audit's ranked seam list, ordered by measured coupling (zero diesel/libp2p/holochain imports = free): **`elohim-blob`** (blob_store + sharding + dag_store + content_server, ~1,900 LoC, extractable now) → **`elohim-govern`** (7 voting mechanisms + opinion clustering, ~2,400 LoC, row-type param only) → **`elohim-admission`** (shed/backoff/advertiser-health policy, ~1,000 LoC) → **`elohim-transport`** (finish the half-built seam: retrofit libp2p onto the 7 existing iroh-side `*Backend` traits — sequence after the `p2p/mod.rs` LoC-ceiling decomposition) → **`elohim-conductor`** (hc client + hot-swap, invert the projection callback first) → **`elohim-reconcile`** (the P1 controller pattern, highest architectural value, 61 diesel refs to invert). Method already proven in-tree by `elohim-facings` (byte-identical golden) and enforced by `seam-contracts`' lockfile boundary test. **The crate-level discipline our `source-file-loc-ceiling` policy cannot see**: that policy makes monoliths tidier, not smaller — extractions are the missing remedy class, and each should land as a backlog-driven bounded work item, not a mid-edit refactor.
6. **The two-tier consistency pattern as a named idiom** *(from Reflection)* — ephemeral gossip for presence, durable sync for content, one topic API. We have both planes (gossipsub inventory; Automerge sync) but no shared idiom naming which plane a new feature rides. **Home:** `automerge-sync` skill + dataplane docs. *(Documentation.)*
7. **I/O-free core as a stated invariant** — `p2panda-core`'s zero-tokio/zero-IO property is what makes everything above it composable; our `elohim-epr` and `seam-contracts` already have it *de facto*. State it (a boundary test like seam-contracts' lockfile check) so it survives contributors. **Home:** `elohim/epr`, `crates/seam-contracts`, future extractions.

## What we study seriously (candidate adoptions, gated)

8. **`p2panda-encryption` as a candidate for our unbuilt encryption layer** — the sharpest *protocol-level* finding. The SSB retrospective concluded our encryption layer is "blocked on being scheduled, not on hard problems"; p2panda ships an I/O-free, MIT/Apache, fuzz-tested, research-grounded (DCGKA/2SM/Double-Ratchet) group-encryption crate in our own language with `zeroize` hygiene. Consuming the *crate* (not the protocol stack) could collapse the `KeyEnvelope` build-out substantially. **Gates:** the unpublished-audit flag ⚠; the ed25519→X25519 key-conversion substrate (same blocker the Holepunch survey named); and the future `KeyEnvelope` entry must still pass p2p-design-gate (likely B2). Study-then-decide, not adopt-blind.
9. **PSI-based confidential topic discovery** — discovery that never leaks topic identity to unrelated peers is a *reach-shaped* discovery primitive: our inventory gossip ships bare hashes (holding the locate-token implies fetch rights — the same gap Holepunch's `discoveryKey` exposed). p2panda's PSI approach is the more private end of that design space. **Home:** the confidentiality-plane backlog (§3.13), beside the three-way credential split. Class C.
10. **Actor supervision for the swarm event loops** — `p2panda-net`'s ractor restart-trees vs our monolithic `p2p/mod.rs` event loop (8,873 lines). Study when the transport extraction lands; Erlang-style per-subsystem restart is the shape our churn-tolerant dataplane wants. Not a library adoption decision today.

## What we leave behind, and why

- **Writer-signed truth (the cardinal line, third survey running).** Everything in p2panda is valid iff the author signed it; the ACL-CRDT governs *who may write*, never *whether content is true*. Same adjudication as Holepunch and SSB: **p2panda may move or locate bytes; only the validating DHT decides truth.** Their auth CRDT must never stand in for Mishpat — group membership resolved by a `Resolver` trait is authorization plumbing, not notarized governance with precedent, challenge, and appeal.
- **Key-as-identity apex.** Ed25519-keypair-as-author, again. The SSB retrospective already carries the ten-year outcome data; p2panda softens it (multi-device via spaces/groups) but does not invert it — no socially-backstopped recovery, nowhere to put the ward or the coerced spouse. Keys stay Category C transport material downstream of the notarized agent.
- **Topic-secret-as-namespace as the *only* scoping.** A secret hash as symmetric namespace key is elegant for private groups and structurally similar to SSB's network-caps key — but it cannot express our 8-level declared-reach ordinal, bilateral consent gates, or commons visibility. We take the PSI *mechanism* (§9) into the discovery layer; we leave the "topic secrecy is the access model" ontology.
- **ACL-over-capabilities as *our* choice.** Their careful ACL-vs-Meadowcap analysis is worth reading, but our answer is neither: authority in Elohim is DHT-notarized, Mishpat-adjudicated, and REA-accounted. Import the analysis, not the conclusion.
- **Lock-step 0.x versioning across the whole workspace.** Right for their 10-crate single-team pre-1.0 moment; wrong for a polyglot monorepo where the DNA hash, the schema contract, and the crates move on different clocks. We need per-crate semver (adoption #3), not their lock-step.
- **Bring-your-own-CRDT as a *substrate* stance.** Correct for their toolkit mission; our substrate already chose Automerge and made it a reconciliation-controller concern (P1). Generic-over-CRDT at the storage layer would re-open a decision we closed for integrity reasons — though Reflection's Loro swap ◐ is a data point to keep on file for the client leg.

## What we watch (their failure modes, live in our tree)

- **`p2panda-blobs` is the flag-with-no-reader pathology in crate form** ✅ — a README presenting a working blob store while `lib.rs` is 5 lines with no `mod` declarations; 560 LoC of real implementation orphaned on disk, `Cargo.toml` declaring zero dependencies, `cargo check` passing trivially on an empty crate. **Fourth sighting of the same disease across four projects**: Freenet's `enable_metering: false`, our `enable_eviction`-with-zero-readers, SSB's funded-specified-archived metafeeds, now this. The Freenet lint ("constant or flag with no reader") extends: *a documented module with no `mod` line, a README claim with no compiled symbol*. Worth adding to the drift toolkit.
- **Discipline decays at the leading edge, uniformly-shaped**: their TODO density and doc gaps concentrate in the newest crates (spaces/net/blobs) while core/discovery sit at zero — the same gradient our tree shows between `seam-contracts` and `elohim-storage`. The lesson is not "they're clean, we're not"; it is that **discipline is a ratchet applied at birth, not a cleanup applied later** — which is exactly the `.epr-meta` born-governed bet, aimed at the crate tier.
- **The spec went missing in the pivot** ◐ — aquadoggo-era p2panda had a formal specification site; the building-block era's spec is blog posts + rustdoc. For a protocol claiming interoperability, code-as-spec is a regression we should not copy as we extract crates: our view schemas + a2o scenarios are the spec discipline to keep.
- **Their sustainability is our outreach question.** Same solarpunk-communitarian culture, same Berlin-CCC lineage as the SSB milieu, grant-funded, three maintainers, GNOME partnership. A genuine fellow-traveler with no token gravity (contrast Holepunch) — arguably the *most* values-adjacent substrate team surveyed to date. Whether that warrants a thread is the operator's call; the technical basis (we share iroh, CBOR, Ed25519, BLAKE3, Rust, and now possibly their encryption crate) is the strongest of any survey so far.

## The one hard rule

> **Adopt the workshop, audit the tools, refuse the trust root.** p2panda's discipline — I/O-free core, trait seams, workspace lints, feature-matrix CI, changelog/release hygiene — transfers wholesale and should. Its crates transfer selectively, each through p2p-design-gate where it touches a notarized concern (`p2panda-encryption` → future `KeyEnvelope`, B2-gated). Its trust root — writer-signed validity, key-as-identity, ACL-as-authority — transfers never, for the same reason Holepunch's and SSB's did not: integrity is what a signed log proves; validity is what only the validating DHT can.

## Verdict

p2panda is the survey subject that finally makes the discipline argument concrete, because it removes every excuse: same language, same dependency neighborhood, smaller team, less funding — and every unit composable, documented at birth, gated by manifest-declared lints, released by runbook. The confrontation is not shaming; it is *permission*: their 2024 pivot proves a team can deprecate its own monolith and come out stronger, and our own tree already contains both the method (`elohim-facings`' golden-test extraction) and the exemplar (`seam-contracts`). The seven-item adoption program above is the transfer. Where they are ahead of us on protocol substance it is narrow and specific — a shipped, research-grounded group-encryption crate against our unbuilt layer — and where we are ahead of them it is structural: a truth plane they don't attempt, a dual-transport reality their aspiration hasn't reached, and governance machinery (lockfile boundary tests, compose-gates, policy pins designed for external consumers) that out-reaches anything in their workshop. Take the workshop; keep the cathedral.
