invariant: >-
  A build result reaches people beyond its builder only by earning it, in three layers. Reach:
  its reach is derived at each read from the records that attest it, is never a field its builder
  sets, and only narrows on the record itself. Attestation: what counts toward a wider rung is
  principals with standing, so one human's devices, agents and runners together count once;
  widening past `intimate` is one governed act that cites its policy, the accepted records and a
  standing proof for each counted principal, and no builder graduates their own work alone; each
  counted record is a fulfilled commitment, so a result later shown wrong is attributable to the
  principals who signed it and is repaired by them. Notary: the act mints one attestation per
  converged (derivation, output) on the DNA, so the provenance of a graduated result is held by
  the network and by no device, forge or registry. The saving is read, never asserted: a peer
  inside a result's earned reach records a `Use`, a peer outside it a `Produce`, and a rung with
  no recorded difference reads unmeasured.
