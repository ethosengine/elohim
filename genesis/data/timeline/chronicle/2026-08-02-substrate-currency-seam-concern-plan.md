## Wisdom worth carrying forward

A concern canon is itself a decision surface: the ceremony's own C7 (a registry row
naming a validator it doesn't have) and C0 (three taxonomies sharing the word
"plane") appeared *inside the plan that defines them* and were caught only by the
fresh-context lens. The two-home split (enforcement rows vs Precedent/governor rows)
is the durable answer for canonizing guarantee-classes that carry no evaluable
predicate — cite governors.yaml's header as the precedent, and give every
non-enforcement row a named consumer or it is silently-unloaded by another name.
