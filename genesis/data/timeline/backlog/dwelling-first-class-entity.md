# Dwelling as first-class entity (place axis, structure)

D2 canon deferred "place-as-first-class" to v2; this is v2's design session, held as a
thesis on the household lattice (umbrella §6). Operator framing (2026-06-04): dwellings
are the real grounding principle — connected to place/land/location/network/supply-chain;
imagodei-like characteristics; **on-protocol dwelling vs "dwelling presence"** mirroring
human vs contributor-presence; a global directory grounded in the real world (the
Nextdoor shape); hard-verifiable anchor of the collective sensemaking commons; place
feeding ecological/system floors, tiers, friction, limits.

**Less greenfield than it sounds — the substrate is mostly shipped:**
- Place / SpatialContext (polymorphic, can attach geo to ANY entity today — nothing
  populates it for households) / H3→governance/reach mapping / CarryingCapacity
  enforcement (the ecological-floors bridge) — the Cybersyn stack.
- ContributorPresence (UNCLAIMED→STEWARDED→CLAIMED + claim-transfer) is the duality
  donor, applied to place.
- The witnessed-records reach flywheel (see `witnessed-records-reach-flywheel.md`) is
  the directory's activation engine: a neighbor witnesses a dwelling into the
  directory, disclaims authority, stewards until inhabitants claim.

**Genuinely missing:** the Dwelling entity decision itself (Place subtype vs Collective
role vs new entry — needs its own p2p-design-gate; NOTE it contradicts the current
"hub is a role, not a notarized entity" posture, hub-boundaries Q1/Q2), dwelling
presence lifecycle, spatial_context population for households, the directory
projection/geo-discovery query, hard place-verification/geo-attestation (deferred by
the dwelling-hub spec), place→donut/reach wiring at dwelling scale.

Do NOT absorb into the formation spine. Reach-enum reconciliation (roadmap #13) is a
standing precondition.
