## 1. Thesis

**Where the cost is.** One closure is paid three times, and two payments are for inputs that did not change. (1) On the device, the hook claims `dev-integration` before any check and holds it through the whole serial gate loop (`.husky/pre-push.bash:255-327` claims, `:194-227` renews at ttl/3, `:1072` → `gate-runner.mjs:624`), so the second device waits the first device's whole gate. Fold of this device's berth ledger, `dev-integration` rows 2026-10-24→29, pairing each `claim` with the next `release` of the same session, renews ignored: 44 holds, p50 **233 s**, p90 **3,321 s**, max 7,164 s, 15 of 44 ≥ 1,500 s, 11 refusals (all `reason: owner`, one 14-minute burst on 10-27, holder shem's device); and at the time of writing shem's device has held `dev-integration` since 02:16:47Z. (2) On the forge, the edge job rebuilds storage and doorway on every commit because the image tag is commit-derived (`elohim/holochain/Jenkinsfile:2660-2690`, `scripts/ci/build-storage-image.sh:78` `CACHE_BUST=${GIT_COMMIT_HASH}`), although the deploy already proves the inputs unchanged and holds the live image (`storage-workload-image.sh:69-110`, `inputs unchanged … holding` on every unchanged-storage run); and the conductor roll fires whenever the hApp's `bundle:` roll key moves, which it does on every DNA-selected wave whose coordinator hot-swap did not report SUCCESS (`happ-roll-key.sh`: `dna-set:` only on SUCCESS, else `bundle:` over the `.happ` bytes; `conductor-happ-stamp.sh` → `conductor ROLLS`). (3) Jenkins re-gates what the device gated. The third payment is the one we keep: Jenkins is a witness and one builder among many, never a second policy engine.

**What this design does.** It keys all three on the closure. The device gates holding nothing and claims `dev-integration` only for a 600 s compare-and-swap window; a silent sibling is overridden by rule under A3's timing (the override row is the evidence floor 5 of the spec asks for), so one device alone always lands. The edge images and the DNA pack are tagged by input closure and skipped on a registry hit whose pinned-digest annotation names that closure; an unchanged DNA is re-tagged, never repacked, so its roll key is identical by construction and the conductors hold. Attestation moves to the device: the SessionStart harvest writes brit `ci:<pipeline>` notes from Jenkins's archived build graph and runs `jenkins-bridge observe` under its governed offer; Jenkins writes no notes, reads no lease, and no daemon is added. The sibling verdict (A8) and, last, a carried gate over eprfs-peer with the carrier's own guard lines as the acceptor are how two devices become one steward's builders: rakia Stage 2 at n=2 without a scheduler, a queue server, a DHT entry or a second Jenkins.

**What it does not claim.** The witness is not a condition on landing; until `epr peer serve` is supervised (A4), fleet serialization across devices rests on Jenkins's `dev` branch job and on whichever session watches its deploy holding `alpha-fleet`, and that is printed, not hidden. The landing intent is a real `Intent` atom in the device's own flow store, but eprfs-peer today folds only `FlowEvent` rows (`eprfs-peer/src/fold.rs:439`), so what crosses at S1 is a `Cite` announcing the atom; the atom itself crosses at A12. The sprint-1 receipt on the edge job is read at stage level because the job verdict is hostage to four crash-looping shem storage containers that nothing here moves.

**Two numbers bound everything below.** Level 0 of a wave is `max(DNA 77–83 min M, eprfs 24–33, edge)`, so "218 → ~85–107 min" is reachable only when DNA is unselected *or* closure-skipped (S2c). Offload on a push pays at most `min(carried gate seconds, rest of the serial loop)`: storage 642–2,613 s against eprfs 1,518 + steward-node 534 + orchestrator 87 ≈ 2,140 s cold.

**Tomorrow, in order.** Day zero (§4): shed the 11.8 GB of unmoored residents, reap the 803-h `push` lease and 273 stale moorings, isolate the hanging `prepush-berth` test case, put the shem crash-loop's first probe and the DIMM steps on the operator list. Then sprint 1's three lanes (§5): α the CI closure skip on a build-only PR first; β the Phase V/L hook with override-by-rule and base-recipe re-exec; γ the sensors. The habits moved are `push-delivers-within-budget` (active red), `sprint-lands-each-closure-once` (red, inactive, upstream, D1) and `push-gate-pays-once` (green, sensor).

**Parallel Jenkins branches, answered from the evidence.** Build-only N=2 is measured safe inside intel-nuc's envelope (PR-215/PR-216 edge builds overlapped on 2026-10-28, 53.5 and 107 min, no crash; 2 × 5.85 + 2 ≈ 13.7 GiB < 15.23). Deploy-bearing N ≥ 2 is unsafe without a lock, and no lock is built: off `dev`/`main` a wave builds and deploys nothing by rule **and pushes no tag** (the DNA job's `Push to Harbor` and `pushHappCandidate` gain the same `dev|main` rule, closing the path by which a `claude/*` run seeded `dev-latest` or a closure tag); `dev`'s own branch job serializes itself; across devices, during a wave, `alpha-fleet` is held by the watching session until the supervised witness exists. Jenkins never reads a lease. The 79-minute orchestrator queue (#2174, lock-bound) shrinks only with shorter waves, which is why the closure-keyed build is in sprint 1 even though it is CI-side.

**Native distribution, answered.** Yes, within one steward's grant and in three rungs: a sibling's green stands in for a gate it already paid (A8, sprint 2; gain on *this* device ≈ 0 until shem pays a closure first, which its floor discourages); a gate neither device paid is *carried* by the device whose own guard lines admit it, with the result crossing as the existing `gate-verdict@1` (sprint 3), the requester waiting **0 s when no admitted device has a head younger than `freshness_s` 90** and at most `confirm_wait_s` 10 s otherwise; measure-class runs stay on `just measure` (rung H today, rung A when adam is provisioned) and are never carried. shem's host (94.7 % 7d CPU peak, MemAvailable min 6.45 GB, 213 OOM kills) carries nothing until its own ram-guard/io-guard lines read clear, as the outcome of the claim, not a policy line.
