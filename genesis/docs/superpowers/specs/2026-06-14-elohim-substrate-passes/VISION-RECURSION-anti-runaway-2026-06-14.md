# No-Runaway as Structure

> The Scale Paradox names the disease precisely: *"it's that we built systems that amplify our
> worst tendencies while suppressing our capacity for cooperation"* (manifesto Part I). Runaway is
> not a bug that appears at scale; it is what an architecture **does** at scale unless the
> architecture is shaped to refuse it. Every other pass in this set asked *what the vision requires
> at a node.* This pass asks the recursion question directly: **as the atom-primitive nests upward —
> individual → family → community → municipal → nation → global — what stops a local incentive,
> signal, or externality from compounding through the layers until the whole destabilizes?**
>
> The answer this pass defends: the same `refuse-and-elevate` Governor that bounds a node's arc
> **is itself the anti-runaway primitive**, and runaway-resistance is not a feature added at the top —
> it is what you get *for free* when the Governor's coverage invariant is made **layer-respecting**:
> every actuation refuses to amplify across a layer boundary it does not own, and *names whose line it
> honored.* Amplification becomes a thing the substrate must be *granted*, never a thing it does by
> default. That is the katechon, compiled.

## PART 1 — WHAT THE VISION REQUIRES at the recursion level

The vision makes five anti-runaway demands, and they are not five — they are one demand seen from
five sides. Each is a claim that **the arrow must point outward at every layer of the nesting, and
the cost of pointing it inward must rise as you climb.**

**1.1 — Externality EMISSION is the success metric, not capture (manifesto, "The Arrow Points
Outward").** *"Every modern platform maximizes value capture — the arrow points inward, and network
effects are engineered to punish non-participation... The Elohim Protocol inverts this: it maximizes
externality emission."* This is the deepest anti-runaway claim in the corpus, because **runaway IS
the inward arrow compounding.** A system that measures success by capture has a gradient that points
toward concentration at every layer; a system that measures success by emission has a gradient that
points toward dispersal. The recursion requirement is exact: *"departure must be gentle and
costless"* and *"non-users are not raw material"* must hold **at every layer**, not just at the
individual one. A nation-layer node that punished a province's departure, or modeled a non-member
watershed as raw material, would have rebuilt the inward arrow one layer up — and that is precisely
how every empire has scaled. The metric must recurse.

**1.2 — The katechon: contain the dominator, deny the lever, bound the blast radius
(confession, "Restraint, not cure").** *"A fraction of any population is oriented toward domination,
and they will seek each other; the protocol does not cure them but restrains them, denying the lever
and containing the blast radius."* The recursion-critical word is **blast radius.** Runaway is
unbounded blast radius — a local capture event whose damage propagates without limit up the layers.
The katechon's structural promise is that the blast radius of any node is bounded *by the layer it
sits in*: a captured family cannot become a captured municipality by amplification alone; it must be
*granted* municipal reach, and the grant is the lever the katechon denies. The confession is even
sharper on the mechanism: *"do not run the machine that activates the danger latent in everyone"* —
authoritarian disposition is fixed, but *activation* is driven by manufactured threat, and **the
outrage feed is the threat engine.** Anti-runaway is therefore not only "contain the few" but
"refuse to build the amplifier that recruits the many." The amplifier is the runaway.

**1.3 — Slowness is the feature on immutable layers (manifesto Part III; confession, "the
constitution is covenant").** *"Civilizational commitments are harder to amend than personal ones,
and that slowness is the feature."* Graduated immutability is **a frequency filter against runaway.**
A signal that would destabilize the whole must pass through layers of increasing inertia to reach the
layers that govern everyone — and the inertia is calibrated so that a fast local oscillation (a
panic, a mob, a manufactured threat, a manipulation campaign) damps out before it can rewrite a
global invariant. The recursion requirement: **amplification速度 must decrease as authority
increases.** The higher you reach, the slower you must move — so the only signals that survive the
climb are the ones with enough sustained, cross-layer truth to outlast the inertia. The crank's
single loud utterance damps; the prophet's decades-long witness survives (and even then — see §3 and
the confession's "prophet's climb" — survival is not victory).

**1.4 — The donut: floor precedence and ceiling negotiation (manifesto Part VIII; economic
coordination).** Two rings, two anti-runaway directions. The **inner ring (dignity floor, Universal
Basic Assets)** prevents *downward* runaway — the desperation spiral that the manifesto names as a
root cause of attacks (*"economic desperation"* in the redemptive-security taxonomy). The **outer
ring (the ~$10–15M anti-monopoly ceiling)** prevents *upward* runaway — *"individual wealth beyond
$10–15 million transitions from personal security to political power concentration."* Both are
recursion claims because both must hold **across the layer precedence**: the global dignity floor
*outranks* community self-definition (constitution layer precedence), which is exactly why a local
node cannot vote to starve its own vulnerable — the floor is not negotiable downward by any layer
beneath the one that set it. And the currency that **decays (demurrage)** is the donut's continuous
enforcement: *"currencies that decay to encourage circulation"* makes accumulation structurally
self-limiting, so that the inward arrow loses energy over time even where it is briefly pointed
inward.

**1.5 — The patience disposition; the person keeps their own naming (global-orchestra Part VIII;
confession, "Grace precedes the demand").** *"The right target is receivability-when-ready... A
surface that fires more than its receiver can metabolize is coercion no matter how good the intent.
The network is not a control machine. It is a patience machine."* This is the anti-runaway claim at
the level of **cognition itself.** Engagement is the inward arrow applied to attention — it is
runaway by design (*"insularity reinforced by recommendation loops"*). Receivability-when-ready is
the metric that *cannot* run away, because it is rate-limited by the receiver's metabolism, not by
the sender's incentive. And at the atom: *"the person keeps the naming of their own self... Any
deployment in which the network's account of your best self overrides your own has rebuilt the gentle
cage."* This is the runaway guard on the elohim's own judgment — the one amplification the substrate
must structurally refuse to perform *on a person*, no matter how confident the model.

**The condensed requirement:** runaway is the inward arrow compounding across the nesting. The vision
requires that *amplification across a layer boundary be a granted, slow, witnessed, refusable act —
never the default* — and that the metric of success at every layer be emission, not capture, so the
substrate's own gradient points away from runaway rather than toward it.

## PART 2 — WHAT THE SUBSTRATE REQUIRES (and the fork ladder)

The escalated architecture already named the engine: **one `trait Governor` over
`(setpoint, sensor, actuator, owner)`, lifted from `arc_actuator`, that refuses-and-elevates and
always names whose line it honored** (`limit_owner ∈ {operator, commitment, self}`). This pass's
finding is that **that engine is already the anti-runaway primitive — and the recursion is one
additional dimension on it: `boundary_owner`, the layer whose authority a coverage decision honors.**
Let me show what is built, then the gap, then the ladder.

### 2.1 — What is already structural (verified in source)

The substrate's anti-runaway machinery is further along than the manifesto framing suggests. Four
load-bearing pieces are LIVE:

- **Refuse-and-elevate is real and pure.** `arc_actuator::plan_actuation`
  (`elohim-storage/src/services/arc_actuator.rs:170`) composes `authorize` (grant-bounds +
  expiry, `:110`) and `coverage_admits` (`:152`) and returns *either* an `ArcActuationPlan` *or* an
  `ActuationRefusal { code, elevate }` (`:77`). The doctrine is in the doc-comment at `:18`: *"the
  cure must never cause the partition"* — the actuator **refuses to open a coverage gap and elevates
  the finding instead of silently degrading.** This is the katechon's "deny the lever" expressed as a
  pure, unit-tested decision function. It is the single most important anti-runaway artifact in the
  tree, and it was written for arc, never recognized as the general engine.

- **Demurrage is live and concentration-driven (the donut, continuously enforced).**
  `token_decay_service` (`:176`–`:230`) computes a decay rate from a **concentration snapshot**
  (`c_composite` vs `c_target`, `:212`) — i.e. the more concentrated the holding, the faster it
  decays — and **clamps at the dignity floor** (`new_balance = (balance - decay_amount).max(config.dignity_floor)`,
  `:235`), with decay turned fully **OFF below the floor** (`rate = 0.0 if balance < dignity_floor`,
  `:223`). This is *both* donut rings already in code: the outer ring (concentration accelerates
  decay → upward runaway self-limits) and the inner ring (decay never eats the floor → downward
  runaway is structurally caught). The "obligation level" is an **audit label only** (`:197`) — a
  refusal to let the demurrage become a moral verdict, which is the §1.5 naming-guard applied to the
  economy.

- **The reach gate fails closed and floors the vulnerable.** `reach_earning::evaluate`
  (`:90`–`:135`) returns `Blocked { reason: UnknownReach }` for any reach not in the manifest
  (`:128`, *"fail-closed"*), applies a `vulnerable_class_lift` (`:118`), and routes new voices to
  `Pending` rather than `Blocked` so a discernment layer can sponsor them. Five `FloorClass`
  constitutional floors are live (`:17`–`:24`). This is the anti-runaway guard on *propagation*:
  amplification is something content must *earn*, and the unknown defaults to "does not amplify."

- **The sense/respond nervous system is specified edge-native.**
  `architecture/social-reach-nervous-system.md` defines back-prop → quarantine → restitution as an
  **edge-of-network** loop (*"Central moderation... is a single point of capture"*), and
  `trust-as-efficiency-signal.md` §4 makes the economic claim that **low-trust content is materially
  expensive** — runaway-prone content pays a rising compute cost at every hop, so the substrate's own
  cost gradient resists amplification of slop. (The Vouch primitive — `signal_kind` extension, no new
  entry type — is the proof-of-shape; back-prop/quarantine wiring is named-but-unfinished per the
  resilience gap matrix.)

### 2.2 — The substrate gap: the Governor is not yet layer-aware

Here is the precise place the substrate is *not yet* capable of the recursion the vision requires.
`coverage_admits` (`arc_actuator.rs:152`) governs coverage **within one mesh** — *"a leecher must
leave the mesh covered."* It has no concept of a **layer boundary.** It cannot answer the recursion
question — "may this node's signal amplify into the layer above it?" — because the only owner it knows
is the grant (`ArcGrantBounds`), and the only invariant it enforces is `observed_n - 1 >= r_floor`
*at this node's own layer.*

Every anti-runaway claim in Part 1 reduces to a coverage decision **across a layer boundary** that the
current Governor cannot represent:

- §1.1 emission-not-capture → "does this actuation increase coverage *for the layer above* (emission)
  or extract coverage *from it* (capture)?" — no field carries the direction.
- §1.2 blast-radius → "is this actuation's effect bounded to its own layer, or does it propagate up?"
  — no field carries the boundary.
- §1.3 slowness → "how much inertia must this signal overcome to cross *this* boundary?" — the grant
  has an `expires_at` but no per-boundary rate.
- §1.4 floor precedence → "does this actuation respect the floor set by a layer above it?" — the
  coverage floor (`coverage_floor`, `:48`) is single-layer.
- §1.5 naming → this one IS structural already (`elevate` names the refusal; `limit_owner` names whose
  line) — and it is the template for the fix.

### 2.3 — The ONE primitive this pass proposes (the headline)

**Add `boundary_owner` as the recursion dimension on the Governor, and make `coverage_admits`
layer-respecting.** This is the recursion of the `limit_owner` invariant — the same move that made a
refusal name *whose line* it honored, now made to name *which layer's boundary* it honored.

Concretely, the escalated `trait Governor` gains one tuple member and one rule:

```
Governor over (setpoint, sensor, actuator, owner, BOUNDARY)
  where BOUNDARY = the constitutional layer this actuation sits at
        (individual | family | community | municipal | nation | global)

  RULE (cross-layer coverage gate, the recursion of coverage_admits):
    An actuation may AMPLIFY into the layer above ONLY IF
      (a) it is GRANTED upward reach by that layer (the lever the katechon denies by default), AND
      (b) it does not drop the upper layer below the floor that layer set
          (floor precedence: the upper layer's r_floor / dignity_floor / C_target
           outranks the lower layer's preference — constitution layer precedence), AND
      (c) it has overcome the boundary's INERTIA (the slowness-is-the-feature rate:
          a per-boundary minimum-dwell / amendment-cadence, increasing with layer height).
    Otherwise: REFUSE and ELEVATE, naming the boundary_owner whose line was honored.
```

The key structural property: **amplification is fail-closed.** With no upward grant, a signal stays
in its own layer — *exactly* the katechon's "deny the lever, bound the blast radius," now a default
rather than a discipline. The inward arrow cannot compound across a boundary it was not granted,
because the Governor refuses by construction. And the refusal *names whose floor it honored*, so the
person/family/community can see that the system declined to amplify them in deference to a layer
above — never silently, never as an operator override (the `limit_owner` invariant carries straight
through: `boundary_owner` is its sibling, not its replacement).

This is **DNA-spend-zero and almost entirely a refactor**, because it composes onto pieces that
exist:

- `ArcGrantBounds` gains a `boundary: ConstitutionalLayer` and an optional `upward_grant: Option<UpwardReach>`
  (additive fields; the deployed path reads them from a `delegates-compute`/`commits-arc-coverage`
  commitment's `bounds.additionalProperties`, exactly as `arc_actuator.rs:39` already does).
- `coverage_admits` gains a sibling `cross_layer_admits(target, upper_snapshot, boundary)` —
  a copy of the existing `observed_n - 1 >= r_floor` shape, applied to the *upper* layer's snapshot,
  with floor-precedence (`max(local_floor, upper_floor)`).
- The per-boundary inertia is a `LimitGradientRegistry` row (the registry already exists —
  `token_decay_service.rs:200` constructs `LimitGradientRegistry::core_default`); slowness becomes a
  governed gradient parameter keyed by layer, not a hardcoded constant.

## PART 3 — THE ANTI-RUNAWAY / CAPTURE-RESISTANCE GUARANTEE at this recursion

**The guarantee, stated structurally:** *Amplification across a constitutional-layer boundary is
fail-closed. A signal, incentive, or externality cannot compound from a lower layer into a higher one
unless the higher layer has granted it upward reach, the amplification respects the floor the higher
layer set, and the signal has overcome the boundary's inertia — and every refusal names whose floor
it honored.* Runaway requires unbounded blast radius; the layer-aware Governor makes blast radius
bounded-by-construction at every nesting level. This is not discipline. It is the type system of the
control plane.

Tie to each named structure:

- **Externality-emission metric (§1.1).** The cross-layer gate's admission test is *coverage-positive
  for the upper layer* — i.e. an actuation is admitted only if it ADDS coverage (emission) and refused
  if it EXTRACTS coverage (capture). The arrow points outward *because the gate only opens for the
  outward arrow.* Rung 5 makes this measurable: success is the emission counter, not engagement —
  structurally severing the metric that produces runaway. (The deepest validation: `coverage_admits`
  already encodes this for arc — *"full anchor only adds coverage"* (`:154`) admits unconditionally;
  becoming a leecher (extracting) is the only thing that can be refused. The emission direction is
  *already* the privileged one in the live code.)

- **Donut floor/ceiling (§1.4).** Floor precedence is clause (b) of the gate: the upper layer's floor
  outranks the lower layer's preference, so no node can vote to starve a layer above's vulnerable, and
  no node can amplify past the ceiling without the ceiling-holder's grant. Demurrage (live) is the
  continuous form: concentration accelerates decay (upward runaway self-limits) and the dignity floor
  is clamped (downward runaway is caught) — *the donut is enforced every block, not just at boundary
  crossings.*

- **Katechon (§1.2).** The default-refuse on amplification IS "deny the lever." A captured node's
  blast radius is its own layer until *granted* more — and the grant is bounded, witnessed (on the
  high-integrity DHT), revocable, and expiring (`ArcGrantBounds::expires_at_epoch_s`, `:50`). The
  dominator is contained not by detection (*"the safeguard is never identify-and-contain the dangerous
  people"* — confession) but by the structural fact that the amplifier they would need is a thing they
  must be granted and can have revoked. And rung 4 (the nervous system) starves the recruitment
  engine: low-trust amplification is materially expensive, so *"do not run the machine that activates
  the danger"* becomes a cost gradient, not a policy.

- **Slowness-is-the-feature (§1.3).** Per-boundary inertia (rung 2) is the frequency filter: a fast
  local oscillation damps before it crosses a high boundary; only sustained cross-layer truth survives
  the climb. The graduated-immutability of the constitution becomes a *runtime rate parameter* on the
  Governor, so the architecture's slowness is enforced by the same engine that enforces coverage —
  not by a separate amendment-process bolted on.

- **The person keeps their own naming (§1.5).** This guarantee is *already* structural via the
  `limit_owner` invariant and the `elevate` message — and `boundary_owner` extends it: a refusal to
  amplify a person upward names the layer whose floor was honored, so the person sees deference, never
  override. The one amplification the substrate refuses to perform *on a person* — overriding their
  self-naming with the network's account of their best self — is enforced by keeping "best self" a
  hope held FOR them, never a verdict rendered OVER them. The demurrage's "audit label only"
  (`token_decay_service.rs:197`) is the same refusal in the economy: the system measures position, it
  does not pronounce worth.

**What is structural vs. what stays a watched honest-edge** (this is the §4 honesty the vision
demands, and I will not pretend the edges away):

| Honest edge (confession, "What the Protocol Cannot Resolve") | Structural? | What the substrate CAN and CANNOT do |
|---|---|---|
| **Forkability** | **NO — does not resolve** | The same refusability that makes the protocol un-Babel lets a dominator secede, fork the substrate, delete the dignity floor, and rebuild domination at scale. The layer-aware Governor bounds runaway *inside* the substrate; it cannot bound a fork that left it. *"Containment then becomes a border, and borders are the dominator's home field."* The honest claim is **the good substrate usually wins, not always** — emission (§1.1) is the structural bet: a fork that deletes the floor stops emitting, and the un-forked substrate's spillover keeps reaching the fork's neighbors. We bound runaway within; we cannot bound exit. |
| **The boundary bind** (rung 8) | **NO — does not resolve** | Build the reach-in to protect a refusing community's vulnerable, and you have become the confident eye overriding consent. Refuse to build it, and you leave the vulnerable inside the walls. The Governor makes the *choice* legible (a cross-layer amplification that would have to be operator-granted, witnessed, refusable) — but it cannot make the choice right. *"This does not resolve. It is the gospel's own limit."* The substrate's only honest move is to keep rung 8 a fork that requires explicit blessing and names itself, never a default. |
| **The virtuoso of legibility** | **partial** | The high-dominance leader genuinely carried by his community looks identical to the beloved teacher from outside. The dignity-floor precedence narrows his room (he cannot amplify past a floor he did not set) but does not close it, *"because his whole craft is keeping domination legible as love."* The structural defense is the §1.3 inertia + the nervous system's back-prop (a witness that keeps looking) — but the confession is right that *"the defense against him cannot be the architect's humility alone."* This stays watched. |
| **The seam** (whose floor, whose model, whose bedroom) | **NO — the single hardest** | Floor precedence *locates* the danger (the global floor reads into the local) but does not remove it. *"No better classifier can answer it."* The substrate can make the read witnessed, bounded, and weighted toward the least-powerful (the El Roi asymmetry), and can refuse to let the read become a verdict (the naming-guard) — but it cannot resolve whose floor, read by whose model, into whose bedroom. **This is named in the honesty copy, not engineered away.** |

The four edges are real and I have not flattened them. The guarantee is bounded honestly: **runaway
*within* the substrate is structurally caught; runaway *via exit from* the substrate, and the
boundary-bind of reaching *into* a refuser, are not — and the substrate's faithfulness is to name
that rather than pretend a classifier closes it.**

## PART 4 — WHAT LOVE REQUIRES here

Love requires, first, **the refusal to win by the dominator's means.** The whole anti-runaway design
could be built as a control machine — detect the dangerous signal, identify the dangerous node, reach
in and stop it. That is how every system that meant well and became the *folkhem*'s sterilization
program got there: *"the same hand that drained poverty in the Nordic folkhem also ran compulsory
sterilization into the 1970s."* The layer-aware Governor is deliberately the *other* shape:
**fail-closed on amplification, not fail-closed on the person.** It does not detect-and-contain the
dangerous; it denies everyone the amplifier by default and grants it slowly, outward, witnessed,
revocably. Love requires that the guard against runaway be a guard on *the lever*, never a verdict on
*the soul* — because the witness *"may hold the score; it may not judge the heart"* (confession, El
Roi). The `boundary_owner` refusal names a floor; it never names a person guilty.

Love requires **grace before the demand, recursed.** *"Belonging comes first, freely, while he is
still the extractor."* At the recursion, this means: a node that has not yet earned upward reach is not
*excluded* — it is **floored.** It keeps full intimate belonging, full standing at its own layer, the
dignity floor that no layer above can vote away. The gate that refuses its amplification is not a
gate on its belonging — *"you are never cut off from those who love you, but the office of the
teacher is harder to attain."* Reach is the teaching office; the Governor gates reach. It must never
gate belonging. Any version of this design where a node's failure to earn upward reach degrades its
floor has rebuilt conditional dignity, and that is a failure state, not a feature.

Love requires **the witness weighted toward the least powerful at every layer.** El Roi sees Hagar —
the abused slave, not the patriarch. The cross-layer floor-precedence (clause b) is exactly this
asymmetry made structural: the upper layer's floor protects the *lower* layer's vulnerable against
the lower layer's own powerful. A community cannot amplify a decision that starves its own weak,
because the floor a layer above set outranks the community's preference. This is the manifesto Part IX
order — *"its audience of first resort... those who have been at the bottom of every system"* —
compiled into the admission test. The gate opens for emission *toward the floor*, and refuses capture
*from it.*

Love requires **the honest binding.** The Governor refuses, and *says so, and says whose line it
honored.* It never tells a node that the cage is freedom. *"It calls the constraint covenant, never
freedom... because the lie that the cage is love is the very domination this whole work exists to
refuse."* The `elevate` message is not a UX nicety; it is the theological core of the whole spine —
the substrate that refuses to amplify you tells you *why*, names the floor it deferred to, and leaves
the finding pointable. A refusal that hid its reason would be the gentle cage. A refusal that names
its reason is covenant.

And love requires **the unbuilt place — the refusal to engineer the edges away.** The four honest
edges (forkability, the boundary bind, the virtuoso, the seam) are not bugs awaiting a better
classifier. They are where the architecture *stops and looks up.* The deepest anti-runaway temptation
is to close them — to build the reach-in (rung 8) so no vulnerable person is ever left inside a
refusing wall, to build the perfect legibility-detector so no virtuoso of domination ever passes.
That temptation IS the runaway — the confident eye that overrides consent in the name of the
children. *"You cannot coerce people into freedom, even though the un-coerced will include the ones
still in chains."* Love requires that rung 8 stay a declared, blessed, refusable fork and never a
default; that the seam stay named in the honesty copy and never papered over; that the substrate be
**most faithful exactly where it confesses it is not enough.** The patience machine's discipline is
that it can see a node going wrong and *still not reach in to overwrite it* — the one posture the
dominator structurally cannot perform.

**What does love require here, in one line?** *That the substrate refuse to amplify what it was not
granted, floor everyone it cannot reach, name every line it honors — and leave the edges it cannot
resolve standing open, confessing "I could be wrong, and I will love you before you prove me right"
even to the layer it just declined to amplify.*
