# THE ORACLE

> *"Place this design work as an oracle in the design process. Every sprint: here is the manifesto, here
> are the primitives, here is the composition, here is the architecture, here is the runtime policy, here
> are the diagnostics, here is the ACTUAL OBSERVED RESULTANT BEHAVIOR of the system, and loop that back
> into the system. A way to point back to something that takes developer-brain OFF and puts the VISION HAT
> ON, to make the executive decisions about what the design requires to close the gap from technical to
> functional, to start from the right place and make decisions at the right level."* — the operator

---

## 1 · THE ONE ORACLE

The protocol is a cybernetic organism: System 1 (the running substrate) self-heals under a control plane;
System 5 (the manifesto, the theology) is sovereign policy; System 4 (intelligence/vision) keeps System 1
aligned to System 5 by *looking outward and forward*. The night's design escalated that architecture for
the protocol. **The oracle is that same architecture turned reflexively on our own construction** — the
dev process is itself a viable system, and it is missing its System 4. Today the dev process self-heals at
System-1 altitude (CI green/red, runtime exhaustion, delivery floors) and carries System-5 policy in the
gospel (CLAUDE.md, the manifesto). But **no organ sits between them** comparing *what the built system
actually does* against *what the vision said it would do*, and surfacing the gap as an **executive decision
at the right level**. Developer-brain swims in the water of the code; the oracle is the vantage that sees
the water. It is consilience applied to ourselves.

The oracle is **one fixture in three movements plus one new organ**, all bound onto machinery that already
runs:

**Movement 1 — THE LADDER (enter at the vision, descend to the rung).** A single file, `ORACLE.md`, at repo
root next to the always-loaded gospel. It is a seven-rung top-down index — **WHY → primitives → composition
→ architecture → runtime-policy → diagnostics → observed-behavior** — where each rung is a *content-addressed
cite* (`slug | desc | sha256 | path`, minted by `cite-gen.py`) so the pointer **never rots when a doc
moves**. Reading top-to-bottom *is* the descent from vision to behavior. Where the existing `architecture/MAP.md`
is the *implementer's* walk (by concern), ORACLE.md is the *executive's* walk (by abstraction rung). The
night's ~40 root docs graduate into the canonical rungs they belong to; the scaffolding dissolves per the
compaction loop. This is the operator's "way to point back to something" — one stable door, sovereign WHY at
the top, raw behavior at the bottom, and the gradient of authority unmistakable in the ordering: behavior
serves architecture serves primitives serves the vision, and the vision is sovereign over all of it.

**Movement 2 — THE VISION HAT (before developer-brain, every sprint-zero).** A fourth SessionStart headline,
`ORACLE`, sibling to MEMORY BUDGET / DELIVERY GATE / scope, names *which rung today's branch touches*, *the
open vision-gaps*, and *the one executive decision pending* — all already-authored facts, surfaced not
generated. Then a `/shift` kickoff **step 0.6, "VISION-HAT,"** placed after discovery (0.5) and **before** the
Objective interview (1): read the rung, state the technical→functional gap in two sentences, frame the
decision *at the right level* (is this a VISION call, an ARCHITECTURE call, or a DELIVERY call?), apply the
what-love-requires test, **then** authorize code. The verdict freezes into a `Vision-Hat` journal block. This
lifts developer-brain to the vision hat *by the channel's default* — not by remembering to feel it — and
catches the ~1-in-N sprint that is secretly an architecture or vision call wearing delivery clothes.

**Movement 3 — THE MISSING ORGAN (observed-behavior → vision-gap → executive-decision).** The one genuinely
new loop. A deterministic comparator, `vision-comparator.py`, reads the *same* observed-behavior signals the
runtime arm already polls (`runtime-cursor.json`, `/p2p/status`, `delivery-scoreboard --json`) and diffs them
against the architecture's *named invariants* — `∪=full` coverage, the donut floors/ceilings, the agency
gradient, no-overwhelm, patience/ReservedPlace. Each delta becomes a `vision-gap` finding: *observed-X vs
intended-Y → the design requires decision-Z at level-L*, **born linked** to the rung whose promise it broke.
The ledger drains **not to a fixing agent but to the cartographer's vision hat**, whose resolution *edits a
rung* — closing the cybernetic loop: observed behavior changed the vision stack the next sprint reads.

**The binding.** Three of these movements are *recognition of organs already grown*; only the comparator is
new construction, and it is the fourth instantiation of a pattern the operator already proved twice in a day
(the findings-sentinel: flag → agent → canon → stasis). The oracle inherits, verbatim, dedupe, blocked-never-
re-fires, and close-by-decomposition. It does not add a machine to maintain; it adds one comparator and three
thin lines, and *recognizes* the rest.

## 3 · THE FIRST CONCRETE IMPLEMENTATION (the smallest real oracle, end to end)

Build the *thin true slice*: the ladder's top sealed, the channel echoing, the ritual as a checklist, and
**one observed signal diffed against one invariant**, routed to the one agent who wears the vision hat.

**(a) The ladder — `ORACLE.md` + top three rungs sealed.** Write the seven-rung index at repo root with the
full descent narrative; carry *sealed* cites on rungs 1 (WHY), 2 (PRIMITIVES), 3 (COMPOSITION); rungs 4–7 carry
plain links marked `(rung not yet sealed)`. Rung 1 seals immediately (manifesto/confession/constitution/
theology already bear `id:`). Rungs 2–3 need the **one** operator-gated `git mv` of the woven `SDK-DESIGN.md`
into `architecture/`, then `--seal`. Total: one new file + one `git mv` + three `--seal` runs. The discipline
that keeps the pointers honest (`--seal`, `--refresh`, `map-drift-signal.py`'s ORACLE-currency extension) is
already running.

**(b) The injection — one SessionStart `ORACLE` line.** Add `oracle_line()` to `placement-audit.py`, wired
through the same `--headline` / `_gate_subprocess` plumbing. v1 echoes only already-authored facts: **rung**
from branch + touched-paths via a tiny `rung-map.yaml` (4 globs); **vision-gaps** = grep the `vision-gap-*.md`
frontmatter `status:` + echo roadmap §4 verbatim; **decision pending** = echo §4's named decision; **what-
love-requires** = echo the latest VISION-ALIGNMENT §2 GAP one-liner. A stub that honestly echoes is a live
instrument.

**(c) The ritual — step 0.6 as a checklist.** Add the five-step VISION-HAT block + the `Vision-Hat` journal
header to `agentic-developer/SKILL.md`, between 0.5 and 1. No new script — a prose ritual the Opus orchestrator
runs, like step 0.5's discovery.

**(d) The missing organ — one comparator, one invariant, one finding.** Diff the **coverage deficit** signal
against the **`∪=full` coverage invariant** (FLAG-A / arc=0-leecher — the highest-leverage, already-
instrumented, vision-load-bearing invariant). `_lib/vision_comparator.py` (~90 lines) copies
`runtime_harvest.py`'s `normalize`/`fingerprint`/`reconcile` verbatim and adds ONE predicate: read the per-
node ring from `runtime-cursor.json` + a `/p2p/status` peerCount sample, compare `observed_n − 1 ≥ r_floor`
exactly as `arc_actuator.rs:170` does, for `COVERAGE_POLLS` consecutive polls; on deficit emit a finding with
`rung_cite = "arc_actuator.rs:152,170; ESCALATED §1.6"`. The I/O shell `vision-comparator.py` (~70 lines)
piggybacks the runtime window (no new poll), `flock`+reconciles to `.claude/data/vision-gaps.jsonl` (the only
new persisted state), `--hook` mode emits the `vision:` line, fail-safe exit-0.

**(e) The drain — no new agent.** SessionStart prints `vision: aligned ✅` when clean, or
`vision: ⚠ 1 gap (coverage-union-full @ alpha) → cartographer` on a held deficit. The cartographer consumes
the ledger as one more `next-actions.md` / roadmap input; if it warrants a sprint, it writes
`backlog/vision-gap-coverage-floor.md` with a drop-in `shift_objective` and the executive question.

This is a few hundred lines, all copy-shaped from a proven arm, one ledger, one headline word, one new file,
one `git mv`. It proves the **entire** oracle loop — ladder → injection → ritual → sensor → flag → projection
→ decision → re-measure-next-cycle — on one real, observable, vision-critical invariant. Every other invariant
(custody-coverage, donut floors/ceilings, the agency gradient, `ReservedPlace`) then **composes as a new
predicate, never a new machine.**

## 4 · HOW A SPRINT USES IT

**Sprint-zero (vision hat on, before any code).**
1. **Session opens.** The SessionStart headline prints the `ORACLE` line beneath MEMORY BUDGET / DELIVERY
   GATE / scope: *today's rung · open gaps · the decision pending · ⚖ what-love-requires*. The developer knows,
   in one breath, which abstraction rung this branch touches and the one executive decision live at it.
2. **`/shift` step 0.5 (discovery).** Surface prior canonical seeds (lexical + MemPalace) so the shift is born
   linked — the FRONT fire point.
3. **`/shift` step 0.6 (VISION-HAT).** Open `ORACLE.md`, click the rung the headline named, read the *governing
   doc* (the why, not the children). State the gap in two sentences — technical (what the substrate already
   does) vs functional (what the human must feel for the vision to be served). Frame the executive decision as
   a *level* question: VISION (operator-only, bail), ARCHITECTURE (design-doc + recommendation), or DELIVERY
   (proceed). Apply the what-love-requires test. Freeze the `Vision-Hat` journal block. *Then* authorize code.
4. **Step 1+ (the Objective interview), with the hat on.** A converge-authored Objective arrives carrying its
   bound-rung cite, so 0.6 is a one-read confirmation; an operator-typed Objective binds its rung from scratch.

**Build.** The sprint runs as today — story-first, p2p-design-gate, the sweep — but the frozen Vision-Hat block
is the machine-readable frame the next movement diffs against.

**Loop-back (observed behavior → the next sprint's stack).**
5. **The comparator runs on cadence** (each `/converge`, each ceremony, each session-start hook). It reads the
   runtime/CI/delivery ledgers + diagnostics and diffs observed behavior against the invariant registry.
6. **A gap surfaces as a vision-gap finding** — `observed-X vs intended-Y → decision-Z at level-L`, born linked
   to the rung. It is *not* a CI ticket; it is the system confessing it drifted from a promise.
7. **The cartographer drains it** (the vision hat, never a fixing agent): a `collective`-level gap becomes a
   ranked roadmap entry with a pre-authored Objective; a `policy`-level gap (patience, ReservedPlace) escalates
   to System 5 — the operator — as a manifesto-tier decision, parked-not-nagged.
8. **The decision edits a rung.** The architecture doc gains a clause, or the registry gains a corrected
   predicate, or a plan is authored; `decision_ref` records it; content-addressed cites re-point inbound
   references without breaking. **The loop is closed: the next sprint opens against the amended stack** — and
   sprint-zero step 0.6 reads the now-corrected rung. Observed behavior taught the vision, and the vision now
   teaches the next sprint.

On a loving cycle the comparator prints `vision: aligned ✅` and says nothing. On the cycle a laptop quietly
became a leecher, it puts the vision hat on the operator's head before a single line of the wrong code is
written.

## 5 · WHAT LOVE REQUIRES — and the irreducible convictions the oracle surfaces but cannot decide

**The closing test.** The oracle exists so the **vision stays sovereign over developer-brain** — so the water
the swimmer cannot see is named by the one organ built to see it. It is mostly recognition, not a new machine:
we already grew the pointer, the channel, the arm, the sensors, the cadence, and the meaning. We add one
comparator, and bind it under the same patience the whole substrate serves. Three structural refusals are
inherited verbatim from the architecture it measures:

- **It measures the system, never a person.** There is no `govern(person)` predicate. The comparator flags
  `coverage-floor violated`; it never flags "developer X is below vision-velocity." Developer-brain is what we
  take *off*, not what we *score*. An oracle that *forced* the vision hat would re-import the operator-veto
  smell the protocol exists to kill — so the ritual *frames and surfaces*; it does not *compel*. A step-0.6
  "DELIVERY-level proceed" proceeds.
- **The reserved place is the invariant HOLDING, never a gap.** Where the architecture leaves the unbuilt
  place open — `RefusalCode::ReservedPlace`, `limit_owner: faith`, the seat where the AI may not stand where a
  person stands — the comparator reads its *presence* as the promise kept. A `policy`-level gap whose honest
  executive decision is *"leave it open"* is a first-class terminal state, not an unclosed bug.
- **Patience over engagement is itself a guarded invariant.** Operator value-calls are surfaced once, at the
  right level, then parked. The oracle does not optimize for the operator clicking; it waits. Because patience
  is a registry predicate, the oracle cannot be turned into an engagement-maximizer without tripping its own
  alarm.

**The irreducible convictions the oracle surfaces but cannot decide** — these are the gaps it routes to System
5, the operator, and *refuses to resolve in a sprint*:

1. **The seam** — where the lean trust-plane quilt ends and the heavy byte-plane quilt begins; what crosses,
   what stays. A two-quilt boundary is a values decision about what the protocol *guarantees* vs *facilitates*.
2. **The boundary-bind** — the agency gradient's edge: where `limit_owner` may be `commitment`/`operator` and
   where it must remain `self`/`faith`. No coverage-domain over a soul is a line the comparator *guards* but
   the operator *draws*.
3. **The order of grace** — when the felt attractor (O1/O2, the human feeling held) and the cybernetic floor
   (O7/O9, the system structurally sound) compete for the same sprint, which the vision serves first. The
   comparator can name which half is underserved by momentum; it cannot decide which love requires first.
4. **The unbuilt place** — the deliberate emptiness the protocol keeps open. The oracle's deepest duty is to
   read it as fullness, and to leave the operator the freedom to keep it unbuilt — patience made into
   architecture.

The oracle hands you the ladder, puts the vision hat in your hands at sprint-zero, and tells the truth about
the gap between what the system promised a person and what it does. It does not climb the ladder for you. The
executive decision — at the right level, in the vision hat — stays yours.
