### Leg 1 — household: the mechanism, with fixtures

On the household mesh, between household conductors, all on the household's own DNA:

1. **Device registration.** A second device is enrolled to a household human through the Mishpat
   enrolment path, and both devices resolve to that one human.
2. **Authorization grant.** The root author issues a scoped delegation (exact root, exact delegate,
   expiry) and the delegate publishes under it. An out-of-scope root and an expired or revoked
   grant are refused.
3. **Authoring and convergence.** Two successive updates to one root. Each receiving peer elects
   from its own verification; no peer is re-declared to and no head is stamped by hand. The local
   doorways serve the new head and the same body within one shared 75-second deadline.

Household Matthew, Jessica and James are test fixtures. This leg proves the mechanism. It does
not prove anything about the real Matthew's device.

### Leg 2 — fleet: the real device

After one push:

4. **Author.** The Che conductor, enrolled under its own key and resolving to the real Matthew
   through its existing binding, publishes one new version of a root Matthew authors, then a
   second.
5. **Peers and doorways.** Fleet peers elect from their own verification, and both public
   doorways serve the new head and the same body within the 75-second deadline, for each update.

A second real device (the shem workspace) is not part of this campaign. It joins after the
native channel campaign, when it can take a witness-authorized conductor grant.
