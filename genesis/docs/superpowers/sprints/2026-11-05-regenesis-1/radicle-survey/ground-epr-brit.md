## 1. Why, and the guidestars in the authors' own words

Quotes:

1. "The protocol is designed so that content is reached through its record. A conforming peer checks the governance in an EPR's head before it serves the content bytes, and a delivery made through EPR resolution credits the people who steward the content. Bytes fetched by their content address alone, outside EPR resolution, carry none of this context." (genesis/docs/content/elohim-protocol/protocol-specification.md:25) ✅
2. "A CID tells you what the bytes are. An AT URI tells you who published them. An Elohim EPR tells you what they mean, who cares for them, and who said they could be here." (protocol-specification.md:29) ✅ An artifact store that stops at the CID is Radicle/IPFS, not this protocol.
3. "The EPR Head is the magnet link of the Elohim Protocol ... small enough to store in a record of Kademlia ... and it carries enough to render a preview card, assess access rights and fetch the bytes by their content address." (protocol-specification.md:118) ✅
4. Division of labour: "Holochain DNAs notarize structural commitments ... elohim-storage moves and reconciles bytes between peers over libp2p and over iroh ... Doorways project canonical content to the web; they are plural and replaceable, and none of them owns the content." (protocol-specification.md:44) ✅
5. Three planes: "Control / truth plane — the DHT-notary: identity, commitments, attestations, reach. Source of truth; small, cheap, witnessed. (P1: storage is a reconciliation controller over this plane — DHT is the manifest, libp2p the controller...)  Data plane — actual byte/record movement ... Where availability and durability live.  Projection plane — read-optimized renders of truth ... Never the source of truth." (architecture/2026-06-21-elohim-seam-map-concern-routing.md:535-542) ✅
6. "The recurring bug is treating a projection as truth — the Case-A all-zeros join and any 'SQLite is authoritative' drift are this mistake." (same, ~line 547) ✅
7. P1 mechanism: "DHT is the manifest, libp2p is the controller. A reconcile pass is one idempotent function called from any of three triggers" (gossip arrival, connection event, periodic sweep); for each `custody-blob` commitment where this peer is provider and the blob is missing, it fetches; where this peer is receiver and the provider has aged past grace, it emits a `placement-gap` REA event. (architecture/2026-05-02-blob-custody-reconciliation-design.md:96-110) ✅
8. Trust contract I1: "Verification terminates in the receiving peer's own conductor. No peer ever adopts a head from gossip, HTTP, or announcement payloads. Announcements are doorbells." I2: "Canonical channels alone move a DECLARED head ... heal/boot paths stamp GapFill (fill-only, never move)." (architecture/2026-07-12-substrate-trust-contract-runbook.md:44-45) ✅
9. "Reach ≠ head ≠ replication" (history/2026-08-07-version-dag-lives-at-l2-not-in-the-crdt-doc.md:228) ✅. Reach = audience, head = version, replication/custody = who holds bytes. Three separate planes; ◐ my paraphrase of the gloss, the one-liner is verbatim.
10. What it must never become (manifesto.md:150): "Capture-resistance must be structural, not aspirational ... architectures whose deployment geometry makes capture cost more than it returns cannot be acquired into hostility." ✅ For storage this reads as: no single host, forge, registry, or doorway may become the authority for an artifact.
11. Operator intent for brit storage (genesis/data/timeline/backlog/brit-daily-driver-handoff-2026-10-27.md, "Operator intent 2026-11-02"): blobs "are expected to be stored on the P2P datastore and cached locally in the EPRFS filesystem projection. This is core product intent, including large-file support, not an optional future LFS storage bridge ... Its pointer format and content hashes must not become a second native storage authority. Reuse the existing EPRFS addressing, storage and projection interfaces." ✅

## 3. eprfs: derivation, closure, FlowEvent, principal accounting

Layering (elohim/eprfs/README.md:5-18 ✅): `elohim-storage` owns EPR records, blobs, custody, replication, DHT/notary, transport. `eprfs` owns local projection (paths, sparse materialization, byte presence, writeback/attestation seams). Consumers such as brit own domain interpretation. Stack: brit, then eprfs projection contract, then elohim-storage data plane, then iroh/libp2p/Holochain.

- **EprfsStorage trait** (eprfs-core/src/storage.rs:97-125 ✅): `resolve_epr`, `has_blob`, `fetch_blob(cid, FetchPolicy)`, `put_blob`, `put_blob_verified(cid, bytes)` ("adapters must verify the CID before persisting. This is byte custody only"), `publish_attestation`, `tier_id`, `fetch_at(root, path, via)`.
- **FetchPolicy** (storage.rs:41-45 ✅): `LocalOnly` (default) or `FetchIfMissing`. Default local-only so a read pattern never leaves the device.
- **Verify-on-read**: a store "does not promise that bytes verify against cid"; the caller checks `h.cid == item.cid && item.cid.verifies(&h.bytes)` (storage.rs:47-49). `BlobCid::verifies` dispatches on the CID's declared codec and fails closed on an unknown codec (eprfs-core/src/address.rs:125; tests 365-414). Tree walks verify each node (tree.rs:266,320). ✅
- **Tree v1 closure**: `load_verified_tree` verifies an entire tree closure; `ProjectedRoot::verify_whole_tree` refuses narrow views and external boundaries ("cannot form a complete native input") and "establishes byte identity only; it neither authorizes execution nor creates reusable derivation evidence" (derive.rs:~88-110). ✅ So "closure" = the verified set of nodes and leaves under a root CID, plus, for gates, the declared input view.
- **MethodDescriptor** (derive.rs:30-70 ✅): name (e.g. `brit-gate`), tools, flags, params, exec, step, embedder, declared `view` paths, non-path inputs, `deterministic`. Its dag-cbor CID is the method CID.
- **DerivationKey** = (method CID, input) where input is a `ProjectedRoot` or a `Raw` CID; "the storage key, never the reach key: reach is judged on the input root" (derive.rs:~130). ✅
- **DerivationRecord** (derive.rs:~178-190 ✅): key, output (BlobCid), computed_by (agent key), basis (Computed / UnderGrant / Recomputed{checked, against, matched}), deterministic, witness (Signature{sig} or Attestation{epr}). Header: "The record is evidence, never authority (spec 12.5): the reader judges it through its own TrustPolicy, and an accepted record whose output bytes cannot be fetched is a miss" (derive.rs:5-7). `to_edge()` projects to a DerivationEdge; `to_attestation(&AdoptedHead)` produces a Notarized (A) draft only for an adopted head, "drafts' derivations stay ephemeral and do not attest".
- **TierBlobManifest** (eprfs-storage/src/tier_blob.rs:27-39 ✅): canonical dag-cbor `{epoch, record: DerivationRecord, byte_len, chunks[{cid, byte_len}]}`; chunks are raw CIDs of up to 1 MiB (line 14); `record.output` is the raw CID of the whole artifact; a reader verifies every chunk, then the full CID, before returning bytes. "The record names the closure and signer claim; accepting its witness or widening its reach remains the caller's decision." Unknown epoch is retained but never a hit. This is the closest existing thing to "artifact storage" and is local-only: "creates no network head, publication, or daemon dependency" (tier_blob.rs:3-4).
- **Local floor** (eprfs-peer/src/local_blob.rs ✅): `put_local_blob(store, flow, record, bytes, ctx)` puts the chunked artifact and appends one FlowEvent: `action: Produce`, `provider: AgentRef(record.computed_by)`, `receiver: ctx.custodian`, `resource: record.output`, `quantity: Count{byte_len,"bytes"}`, `in_scope_of: ctx.in_scope_of`, `occurred_at` (caller-supplied), `classified_as: ["custody-blob", <output cid>, "manifest:<cid>", "copy:self"]` (lines 47-62). Reads (`stream_local_blob`) append one `Use` event with `draw:hit | draw:miss | draw:unavailable` (lines 70-130). If custody succeeds but the flow append fails you get `LocalBlobUnrecorded` carrying the manifest CID; "success is never reported without the event" (line 30-32). Fully offline: "No endpoint, daemon, peer, credential, or publication head participates."
- **Head plane**: `ChannelHead` (eprfs-peer/src/head.rs:28-41) is signed by a device did:key over a domain tag plus canonical dag-cbor of all other fields, `folded` included; `seq` strictly increases; the clock `at` is display-only ("no decision compares two devices' clocks"). announce.rs (header 1-9) debounces, re-announces periodically, and avoids ack-of-ack loops; head_table.rs persists `heads.json`; gossip.rs: the gossip ALPN sits behind admission, "only the person's admitted devices are taken as gossip neighbours", and heads are still judged one by one. ✅
- **Principal accounting**: steward-space spec A5 (genesis/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-steward-space-landing-plane-design.md:234-238 ✅): "Agent output ... records PRINCIPAL `human:matthew` as provider, with persona `agent:<role>@<model>`, session and device recorded as PROVENANCE. 'Vendor is provenance not payee.'" Capability is the intersection of the human's grant and the role's capability, "not a second principal". Caveat in the same section: the hook that stamps human presence is still proposed, so human-only acts are cooperative at S1. In `put_local_blob`, accounting is whatever `AgentRef` the caller puts in `computed_by` and `custodian` (◐: the code does not itself resolve human vs agent; the context "does not claim signer standing", local_blob.rs:16-17 ✅).

## 5. brit attestation: signed, verified, key custody, spec-only

- **What brit is**: gitoxide fork; every commit carries three-pillar EPR trailers; git artifacts project to content-addressed ContentNodes (elohim/brit/CLAUDE.md:7 ✅). Identity is CIDv1/0x71/sha2-256 via the published `elohim-epr` crate; raw blobs 0x55; BLAKE3 only for non-address fingerprints (CLAUDE.md:19 ✅). Engine must stay codec-agnostic.
- **What is signed** (brit-build-ref/src/build_cmd.rs:89-103 ✅): `BuildAttestationContentNode {manifest_cid, step_name, inputs_hash, output_cid, agent_id, hardware_profile, build_duration_ms, built_at, success, signature}`; built with empty signature, canonical bytes computed, ed25519 signature hex set, node put in the local object store, then `put_build_ref(step, commit, node)` writes it under `refs/notes/brit/build/<step>`. `--output-json` stores a `brit.gate-verdict` node (`GateVerdictContentNode {step_name, verdict}`, lines 25-36) so a step with no artifact still has an output CID that resolves. A gate verdict is therefore a brit build attestation whose `output_cid` names a stored verdict; `success` carries pass/fail. `inputs_hash` is the closure key. `--manifest-path` takes the raw CID of the manifest file.
- **Verified on read: yes** (brit-build-ref/tests/verified_reads.rs:1-5, 176-330 ✅). A stored record is returned only when it decodes strictly, its signature verifies against its own `agentId`, and it binds to the ref it was found under. Tests drop a note with flipped `success`, a note copied under another step, an appended field, a non-hex signature. `build get` prints the miss line for an unverifiable record; `build find --step --inputs-hash` prints verified rows as JSON `[commit, attestation]` and names dropped ones on stderr as `unverifiable: <commit> <reason>` (build_cmd.rs:113-140). **Critical limit**: `find_returns_a_foreign_key_note_under_its_own_agent_id_and_no_standing` (verified_reads.rs:224) means "verified" = self-consistent signature, not trusted signer. Standing must be decided by the reader's roster/policy (the handoff notes decision D5 and a `binds-signer` roster row, handoff:259-260). 
- **CAS writes: yes** (brit-epr/tests/cas_writes.rs:1-4, 187, 212, 380, 640 ✅): "Writes publish only against the prior they read, and are Ok only once what they published reads back verified"; races are forced through `BritRefManager::before_publish`; a stale ref is refused and kept; concurrent updates surface as `REFUSED concurrent update`. ## 7. Five constraints any artifact-storage design must satisfy

1. **Bytes by CID, meaning by record.** Blobs are raw (0x55) chunks under a dag-cbor manifest (the `TierBlobManifest` shape); every read verifies per chunk then whole (`BlobCid::verifies`, codec-dispatched, fail-closed). Reach, governance and steward live in the EPR/head, never inferred from the CID alone (protocol-spec:25). A design with a second hash authority (LFS oid, Radicle OID, a registry digest) as the identity violates handoff intent line 11 above.
2. **Three separate planes, one truth.** Control plane (Holochain commitments and attestations) holds the claim that someone should/does hold the artifact; data plane (iroh/libp2p blobs, reconcile pass) moves bytes; projection (SQLite, doorway, forge-like web view) is read-only and derived. Reach, head and replication stay independent (a declared head moves only by canonical channels; heal is fill-only; announcements are doorbells; the receiving peer's conductor verifies).
3. **Custody and use are REA, accounted to the true principal.** Storing is a `Produce` FlowEvent plus a `custody-blob` commitment (provider = custodian, receiver = content steward, `placement-gap` on shortfall); reading is a `Use` event with typed outcome. Provider is the principal (`human:matthew` on the real network); agent persona, session and device are provenance. The event is never missing: success is not reported without its record (local_blob.rs:30-32). Observations graduate to notarized events by recipe policy, not by default.
4. **Attestations are signed, verified on read, and carry no standing by themselves.** Use the brit build/gate attestation pattern (closure-keyed `inputs_hash`, `output_cid`, signature over canonical bytes, writes CAS'd against their prior). A verified signature proves integrity under that agent id only; admitting the signer is a separate roster/policy decision, and a DerivationRecord is "evidence, never authority" judged by the reader's TrustPolicy. Do not copy forge-style "trusted because it is in the repo".
5. **Local-first floor, additive network.** The one-device path (`put_local_blob`, `FetchPolicy::LocalOnly`, no daemon, no network head) must work with nobody else present; replication, heads and gossip are additive, gated by device admission, and bounded (1 MiB chunks, 4096B heads, 64 MiB/1 GiB caps). Governance is read, not owned, by the storage layer (brit reads consent requirements, never owns governance); no single host, doorway or forge becomes the authority (manifesto:150).
