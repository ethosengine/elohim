It's the difference between surveillance (which stores and exploits) and witness (which sees and forgets).

The Observer isn't a product—it's a protocol. A way of seeing that generates stories without storing surveillance.

### **The Ephemeral Architecture**

**What happens to visual data:**
1. Camera captures frame (encrypted at sensor)
2. Streams to local node (never leaves your network)
3. Elohim processes for patterns
4. Generates REA story elements
5. Visual data cryptographically destroyed after proccessing.
6. Only structured story remains

Video data exists long enough for Elohim to understand the context, too short for others to extract and surveil.

### **The Privacy Physics**

Next to every light switch, a matching privacy switch:
- **Up (Eye Open)**: Observer active, stories being written
- **Down (Eye Closed)**: Observer blind, no processing
- **Middle (Eye Half)**: Audio only, no visual

These switches are physical, mechanical—no software can override them. When you flip the switch down, the camera physically disconnects from power. The Elohim cannot see what you've chosen to keep private.

This isn't policy or promise—it's physics.

## **Part III: Solving Fundamental Problems**

### **The Sybil Attack Solution**

One of every open network's oldest challenges is the Sybil attack—one entity pretending to be many. Every prior answer has been a filter: a document, a scan, a test that compares a person to a template of "human" and admits whoever matches. Filters fail the people the template never anticipated, and they are what an adversarial AI learns to pass. The Elohim Protocol answers instead with witness: humanity attested by those who know you, staked on the witness's own standing (the trust others have given them through what they have done in shared space), contestable, never inferred by the underlying network and never reduced to a score. The [manifesto's *Witnessed Humanity* section](./manifesto.md) lays out the digital half of that answer, where the witness is an agent attuned to a person through what they state and reveal. The Observer is where that same answer gains a body.

**Physical Presence**: Hardware the household itself controls, under a mechanical privacy switch, witnesses that a person was here, breathing and moving in a real place. It writes a story of presence and then forgets the frames. It does not verify against a template of what a human looks like. The camera never identifies anyone on its own: a presence story is tied to a person by the people who were there and by the person's own device, the household's elohim signs the story and stakes its own standing on it, and the person can contest it.

**Consistency With Yourself**: Real people are consistent with themselves over time, in their own shape, which may be nothing like anyone else's. The Observer's patterns are attunement, a model of this one person's own habits, not a population profile. A neurodivergent life has its own shape; the Observer compares a person only against that shape.

**Correlation, Not Biometrics**: No biometric identity is produced or stored. What the Observer contributes is correlation: the person the household's agents are attuned to in the digital record and the person its own eyes have witnessed at home are, over a witnessed life, one person. A counterfeit that can fake a stream of text cannot fake a witnessed home life. Having no physical witness is never evidence against anyone's humanity; it only adds when present.

**Social Verification**: The people who share the space attest to who you are, and each attestation carries the attester's own standing. A ring of invented identities that only vouch for one another has no one outside the ring to vouch for it, and no witnessed home life behind it.

This happens continuously, but a presence story counts toward a person's witness only if they have opted in, whatever room the switch is open in; a guest or a child has not chosen to be witnessed because someone else set the room. Your humanity is attested by your witnessed life rather than a government ID; the physical witness is an addition you choose, never a requirement.

## **Part IV: The Graduated Harm Protocol**

### **When Preservation Happens**

The Observer only preserves visual data when specific harm patterns are detected. This isn't surveillance looking for crimes—it's witness ready to protect.

### **The Four-Level Response**

**Level 1 - Personal Record** (Most common):
- Encrypted footage offered to affected party
- They choose: delete, save, report
- No automatic authorities
- Example: Minor workplace injury, interpersonal conflict

**Level 2 - Mediation** (Community level):
- Pattern offered to mediator Elohim
- Both parties must consent
- Focus on restoration not punishment
- Example: Repeated conflicts, boundary violations

**Level 3 - Institutional** (Serious harm):
- Evidence prepared for authorities
- Victim controls disclosure
- Legal chain of custody maintained
- Example: Assault, systematic discrimination

**Level 4 - Emergency** (Imminent danger):
- Immediate alert to emergency services
- Temporary preservation for investigation
- Constitutional protection activated
- Example: Medical emergency, active violence

The key: The victim maintains control except in Level 4 emergencies. The Observer witnesses but doesn't judge, protects but doesn't prosecute.
