## The Course at a Glance

Fourteen days, fifteen modules, five movements — walking the shape of the gospel story from lament to commissioning. Each module pairs one Foundations of Humane Technology concept with its biblical counterpart and lands in practice for three audiences: tech workers, households, and church leaders.

**I. Waking Up — Lament & Sight** (Days 0–2): 1 The Church Dilemma · 2 Systems Thinking · 3 A Problem Well Stated · 4 The Wisdom Gap & the Conditions for Trust

**II. Turning — Repentance & Repair** (Days 3–5): 5 Our Attention is Sacred · 6 Externalities & the Commons · 7 Regeneration & Reconciliation

**III. Reordering Loves** (Days 6–7): 8 Centering Love · 9 Fruit as Metric

**IV. Rebuilding Common Life** (Days 8–10): 10 Recognizing Distortions · 11 Collaboration & Sensemaking · 12 Fairness & Justice

**V. Abundant Life & Sending** (Days 11–13): 13 Helping People Thrive · 14 Ready to Act · 15 Digital Discipleship Retrospective

Three rhythms run the whole course: the Micah 6:8 refrain (walk humbly → love mercy → act justly, resolving at commissioning); the Acts 2:42-47 picture of regenerative community; and the simulation lab thread (The Social Dilemma, Evolution of Trust, Parable of the Polygons). Every module ends with exactly one assignment — the next FHT module or a named practice.
