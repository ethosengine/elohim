  - **(b) partially bound**: 305 (63%) — some steps have no matching definition.
  - **(c) no steps bound at all**: 76 (15%) — story-first placeholder, needs a step file from scratch.
  - **(d) held-by-form** (already in `tag-plan.json`'s `heldByFormNowEnabled`, form-blocked only on shared alpha — Act I on a mesh the suite owns): **35** — included above by their a/b/c bind status but flagged separately; the household mesh is exactly the owned-substrate these need.
