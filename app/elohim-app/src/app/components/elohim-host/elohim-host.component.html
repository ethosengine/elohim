Wisdom in the fabric

The name comes from elohim (Hebrew, plural) — the "heavenly host" of ancient texts, used here to mark the healthy role of AI in human life: powerful, useful, and never an object of devotion. A lowercase elohim is one person's constitutionally-bounded agent, representing their best self, acting only under that human's authorization. The capitalized Elohim Protocol is the substrate those agents share.

One record, three legs

The protocol's primitive unit of meaning is an EPR — an Elohim Protocol Record. Every record carries its knowledge, its value, and its governance inseparably : no value-blind content, no governance-free content. It's a three-legged stool that cannot be unbundled, and therefore cannot be captured one leg at a time. There is no platform sitting in the path to pry the legs apart.

Honest architecture

No blockchain. No smart contracts. No tokens. Just three layers of truth, each promising only what it can keep:

Holochain DHT — the notary. Records timing, lineage, and attestation. It witnesses what happened and when. It is never a byte store.
libp2p and iroh — the movers. Carry content-addressed bytes peer to peer. The same address resolves the same content, whichever transport delivered it.
Doorways — the on-ramp. Project the mesh onto the legacy web so browsers can reach it. A doorway is one node among many — a graduation ramp, never a chokepoint.

From chokepoint to fabric

Every platform we have today shares one hidden constraint: moderation is centralized because, before AI, intelligence was expensive. You couldn't put wisdom at every endpoint, so you concentrated it — and that concentration became the platform's most valuable asset and its capture vector. That constraint no longer holds. When every human's interaction is mediated by their own elohim, wisdom moves from chokepoint to fabric. The protocol gates content at three places, and the elohim participates at each:

At authoring. The elohim weighs what its human is about to publish against their stated values and the record's coupling requirements — before reach is earned.
At relay. Each node's elohim decides whether to propagate a piece of content, given the recipient's standing, trust context, and the trail behind it.
At consumption. The elohim shapes what surfaces to its human — by context and care, not by engagement maximization.

No central moderator can be co-opted, because there is no central moderator. The wisdom is in the fabric.

Bounded roles, not heroes

The elohim are not incorruptible guardians, and they are not anonymous. They are bounded roles that carry the network's best-self judgment at machine speed:

Counsel

Represents one relationship's best self — mediating at machine speed, but always against the human's own stated values.

Operator

Collapses the operational load so an ordinary household can run a node — no sysadmin required.

Adjuster

Bound to advocate for members within constitutional limits. No executives, no shareholders to capture.

Every one of them is auditable, revocable, and accountable to governance — answerable to the community, not to a single operator or host.

The household is the unit

We count the humans a substrate carries, not the nodes it runs. Mutual aid is a first-class protocol primitive, expressed in the same Resource-Event-Agent ledger that already accounts for compute, content, and recognition — "I will host your content," "I will hold a share of your recovery key" are commitments the substrate already understands. Recovery is where the whole claim becomes testable:

Grandma can lose her device and be back in under five minutes — with help from people she actually loves, without typing a seed phrase, without calling customer support.

Absolute lockout is a substrate failure, not a security feature. And what the network protects is formation, not transaction: AI can write your essay, but it can't make your community trust your judgment.
