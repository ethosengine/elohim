What's already here

This isn't a promise deck. The protocol is a working monorepo — Rust, Angular, Holochain — and its vocabulary names the human practices it serves, not the engineering it requires. Seven pillars, each a lived concern:

imagodei — Latin, "image of God." Identity, presence, and the stewardship of self; recovery through the people who actually know you.
lamad — Hebrew, "to learn." Knowledge as territory, journeys, and travelers; learning offered as an invitation, never a certification.
avodah — Hebrew, "work as service." Work items are EPRs; collaboration gated by demonstrated mastery, not by credentials.
qahal — Hebrew, "assembly." Community, consent, and decisions made at the lowest competent level.
shefa — Hebrew, "abundance." Mutual credit and value flows; wealth as circulation rather than accumulation.
mishpat — Hebrew, "justice." Bounded commitments and restored capability — never punishment.
doorway — the web2 on-ramp. A graduation ramp onto the peer-to-peer mesh, owned by no one.
