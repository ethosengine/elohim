Design Principles

Graduated intimacy

Spaces for personal exploration sit alongside protected commons. Consent boundaries keep individual extremes from corrupting shared spaces.

Transparency as immune system

Provenance and open governance make manipulation visible. Sunlight is the immune response, not a surveillance apparatus.

Stewardship over ownership

Nobody owns the commons; contributors steward it. Custody is a committed responsibility you hold, never a claim you assert.

Hub-optional floor

One device is full participation. Hubs graduate convenience and scale — they never gate who gets to belong.

Formation over transaction

Understanding is measured by social reach and stewardship, not grades or engagement. Your community attesting to your judgment is the credential.

Wealth as circulation

Value creates more value by flowing, not by pooling. The ledger rewards care and contribution, and resists the concentration that threatens coordination.
