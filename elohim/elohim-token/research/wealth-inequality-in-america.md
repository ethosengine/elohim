## Why This Is Here

This is a transcript from the viral 2012 video "Wealth Inequality in America" (6.4M+ views), which visualizes the findings of Dan Ariely and Michael Norton's 2011 study "Building a Better America — One Wealth Quintile at a Time."

This video matters to the elohim-token design because it provides the **empirical initialization** for the ResponsibilityDemandParam curve. The study showed that 92% of Americans — Republicans and Democrats alike — agree on an ideal wealth distribution where:

- The wealthiest are 10-20x better off than the poorest (incentive exists)
- A healthy middle class transitions smoothly into wealth
- Nobody is destitute
- Concentration is bounded

The gap between what people *think* the distribution is and what it *actually* is — let alone the gap between reality and the ideal — demonstrates that the problem isn't disagreement about values. It's the absence of structural mechanisms to express values that 92% of people already share.

The elohim-token's responsibility demand curve is that structural mechanism.
