The only in-tree lever today = cgroup crash-floor + restart cadence.

## VERDICT: KNOWN-OPEN, no clean pin-to-fix. Best mechanistic match = **networking-buffer accumulation on the tx5/holochain_p2p SEND path** (`BytesMut`/`Vec<u8>` retained per send) — this is the partial overlap with holochain issue **#5664** (its 65%-of-real component is `BytesMut::put_slice → RawVec::grow_amortized` "networking buffer accumulation", which is NOT iroh-specific) — unfixed in any release. The "could not find url for peer" spam is the TRIGGER that re-drives that send path ~16/sec; it is mitigated upstream by holochain PR **#5718/#5719** (merged AFTER our pinned rev; not in a release we run) — but #5718 is a trigger mitigation, NOT a leak fix (its author: "there's another problem at play that will also need addressing later"). There is NO upstream commit/version we can pin to that demonstrably fixes the leak. Recommendation: pin past #5718 only to cut the trigger spam and re-measure; FILE a kitsune2/holochain issue framing it as **per-send buffer retention re-driven by the validation-receipt peer-URL miss**, with our discrete-≥128KB-mmap telemetry.

### Why the buffer path, not the peer store (mechanism, corrected)
Our signature is a growing COUNT of discrete anonymous mmaps each **>128KB**, with a FLAT `[heap]`. The >128KB figure is a malloc fingerprint: glibc mmaps allocations individually only at/above `M_MMAP_THRESHOLD` (128KB default); smaller allocations come from the heap/arena (jemalloc slab-packs small records even more aggressively — same conclusion, stronger). So the signature means *thousands of individually-large (≥128KB) allocations retained.*
- **Rules OUT small-record HashMap growth.** A kitsune2 `AgentInfo` record (url, agent key, space, storage arcs, timestamps, signature — source-checked at v0.3.2, sub-1KB) accumulating in the peer store would grow the heap/arena (observed FLAT) and realloc one bucket array — NOT spawn thousands of discrete >128KB mmaps. So **kitsune2 CRIT-1 "unbounded peer store growth" cannot produce our signature** (demoted below). Same for HIGH-1 op store unless individual ops exceed 128KB (content blobs only).
- **A peer-URL lookup is a read that MISSES — it inserts nothing, so it can't grow the store.** What the retry storm actually re-drives is repeated SEND attempts, each allocating a transport buffer (`transport_tx5::send` does `data.to_vec()` per send @ v0.3.2 lib.rs:340; recv does `BytesMut::from(...).freeze()` @ :409). A retained population of such buffers IS a growing count of discrete ≥128KB mmaps. That is the fingerprint match.

### #5664 is a PARTIAL match (not a clean disqualifier, not a full match)
- NOT ours: its `iroh::magicsock` VecDeque (159MB, the accelerating part) and `kitsune2_transport_iroh` accept tasks — those crates are absent from our tx5-only conductor (Cargo.lock @ a6d4e805, point 2).
- IS plausibly ours: its largest real component — `BytesMut::put_slice → RawVec::grow_amortized` networking-buffer accumulation (368→431MB) — is generic to holochain_p2p / the transport send path, not iroh-bound. This is the component that matches our >128KB-mmap fingerprint.

## Point 1 — holochain/holochain: validation_receipt / peer-URL / memory

- **#5664 [OPEN] "[BUG] memory leak in Holochain 0.6"** — https://github.com/holochain/holochain/issues/5664. Reported on Holochain 0.6.1-rc.1 (Moss). Conductor child OOM'd at ~34 GB RSS. jemalloc heap diffs (zippy) attribute real growth to: `BytesMut::put_slice` networking buffers (368→431 MB), **`iroh::magicsock::Actor::run` VecDeque (3.8 MB → 159 MB, accelerating 42×)**, `poll_updated` watch channels (96 MB), plus smaller `kitsune2_transport_iroh::spawn_accept_task` and `iroh_quinn ConnectionSet` items. State: open, "awaiting clarification," not reproduced in Wind Tunnel. **Caveat: this profile is the iroh transport; we run tx5 (point 2). And the headline mechanism is a single growing VecDeque, which would grow ONE mapping — our signature is a growing COUNT of discrete mappings. So #5664 only partially overlaps (the BytesMut-per-something component).**

- **#5718 [MERGED to `develop` 2026-04-10] / backport #5719 [MERGED to `develop-0.6` 2026-04-17] "fix: Clear validation receipt requirement for agents who have gone offline"** — https://github.com/holochain/holochain/pull/5718. This is the DIRECT fix for our "could not find url for peer" spam: when an author has no peer URL it now treats them as offline and clears the receipt requirement instead of retrying forever. Author's note (verbatim): the real bug is that the receipt flag is set for any op received (incl. via gossip while author offline); "*the fix is valid, even if there's another problem at play that will also need addressing later.*" **This mitigates the TRIGGER, not the leak. Merged AFTER our pinned rev (a6d4e805), so it is NOT in our 0.6.0-dev.28 conductor.**

- **#5780 [OPEN] "Source-validated Holochain 0.6.1 audit tracking issue"** (45 findings) and child **#5781 (W2-VR-01)** touch the validation-receipt path (`cell.rs`, `validation_receipts.rs`, `validation_receipt.rs`) — these are correctness/security findings, not the memory leak. https://github.com/holochain/holochain/issues/5780

- **#5813 [OPEN] "Host-function panic leaks a CONTEXT_MAP entry (pinned Arc<CallContext>)"** — https://github.com/holochain/holochain/issues/5813. A genuine unbounded per-item leak (one leaked `Arc<CallContext>` per host-fn panic; no scopeguard/catch_unwind). Confirmed present on develop. **Probably NOT ours: triggered by guest WASM host-fn panics, not by the validation-receipt/peer-URL path, and we don't see a host-fn-panic log storm.** Worth noting as a second known unbounded-growth class in 0.6.

Source of the log line (point 3 below) is `validation_receipt_workflow.rs:87`.

**BLOCKING transport fact (resolved):** holochain `Cargo.lock` @ rev `a6d4e805` wires **`kitsune2_transport_tx5` 0.3.2** (tx5 0.8.1 / tx5-go-pion WebRTC + sbd signaling). Our elohim-storage *client* uses kitsune2 0.4.1 (which has iroh); the leaking *conductor* uses 0.3.2 (tx5 only). → #5664's iroh profile is structurally impossible in our conductor.

**Lead candidate — per-send buffer retention on the tx5/holochain_p2p path (matches the >128KB-discrete-mmap fingerprint):** `transport_tx5/src/lib.rs` @ v0.3.2 allocates a fresh buffer per operation — `send` does `self.ep.send(peer_url, data.to_vec())` (line 340), recv does `bytes::BytesMut::from(...).freeze()` (line 409). The validation-receipt retry storm (point 3) re-drives `send_validation_receipts` continuously, so this allocation path is exercised ~16/sec. #5664's jemalloc profile shows the corresponding `BytesMut::put_slice → reserve_inner → RawVec::grow_amortized` "networking buffer accumulation" growing 368→431MB and RETAINED — a population of large buffers, each individually >128KB, is exactly a growing count of discrete anon mmaps with a flat heap. This is the mechanism that fits our telemetry. (Whether the retention is in tx5-connection, holochain_p2p's send queue, or a watch/notify channel — #5664 also fingers `poll_updated` at 96MB — is the open question to pin in a filed issue; the FINGERPRINT says buffers, not records.)

## Point 3 — what `validation_receipt_workflow.rs:87` actually does @ rev a6d4e805

Source (fetched at the pinned rev): the `info!(failed_to_sign_and_send_receipt = ?e)` at line 87 is in the `Err(e)` arm of the per-author send loop in `validation_receipt_workflow`. Behavior:

- It groups pending receipts by author and calls `sign_and_send_receipts_to_author` → `HolochainP2pDnaT::send_validation_receipts(...)`. The "could not find url for peer" error is raised INSIDE that p2p call (kitsune2/holochain_p2p), not in this file.
- **On success** it calls `set_require_receipt(txn, &op_hash, false)` to mark ops sent so they won't be retried.
- **On error (line 86-89)** it ONLY logs and `continue`s — it does **NOT** clear `require_receipt`. So those ops stay `require_receipt = true` and are re-fetched by `pending_receipts` on the NEXT workflow tick and retried. This is a DB-backed **re-enqueue/retry loop**, repeating every workflow run until the peer URL resolves. It is bounded by DB content (not an unbounded in-memory queue in THIS workflow), but it drives the p2p send path continuously → ~16/sec spam.
- The leak accumulates DOWNSTREAM of line 87 — in the holochain_p2p / tx5 send path that keeps being re-driven, specifically the per-send buffer allocations (point 2 lead candidate), NOT in this workflow's own allocations and NOT in the peer store (the URL lookup is a read-miss that inserts nothing). This is exactly why #5718 (clear the requirement when the author has no URL) stops the retry storm: it breaks the re-drive loop, so the per-send buffers stop being allocated ~16/sec. But #5718's author explicitly says the underlying problem (and thus any retention bug in that send path) remains.

## Point 4 — Bottom line / pin path

- **Is it a known upstream class?** Partially. The TRIGGER (validation-receipt sends to authors with no peer URL → retry storm) is known and mitigated by holochain #5718/#5719. The LEAK that best fits our telemetry (growing COUNT of discrete >128KB anon mappings = retained large buffers, flat heap) is the **networking-buffer-accumulation component of holochain #5664** (`BytesMut::put_slice → RawVec::grow_amortized`, the non-iroh 65% of its profile) on the tx5/holochain_p2p send path our retry storm re-drives — OPEN, unfixed in any release. The peer-store/op-store/fetch-queue unbounded classes (#408) are real but their small item sizes don't match our >128KB-discrete-mmap fingerprint.
- **Pin to fix?** There is NO clean "pin → fixed" target. #5718 is post-our-rev but is a trigger mitigation, not a leak fix, and lives on `develop`/`develop-0.6` (next 0.6.x release, not a tagged kitsune2 bump). #5664's iroh-VecDeque fixes don't apply to our tx5 transport, and its BytesMut component has no upstream fix. Pinning forward to a 0.6.x that contains #5719 would quiet the 16/sec spam and almost certainly slow the climb (it stops re-driving the leaking send path), but the buffer-retention bug itself is unaddressed.
- **Recommended action:** (a) pin/upgrade to the first 0.6.x release containing #5719 to cut the trigger spam and re-measure — if the climb stops, that strongly corroborates the per-send-buffer hypothesis (the leak only advances while sends are re-driven); (b) FILE one upstream issue (holochain/holochain, cross-linking #5664 and kitsune2) framed as **per-send networking-buffer retention on the tx5/holochain_p2p path, re-driven by the validation-receipt "could not find url for peer" loop**, with our smaps evidence: anon_mapping_count 4,300→5,500/cycle, multi-GB other_anon_bytes, FLAT [heap] and FLAT largest mapping, FLAT thread count = a growing population of distinct >128KB mmap'd retentions. Emphasize the >128KB fingerprint so maintainers look at per-buffer retention, NOT small-record (peer-store/op-store) growth — that distinction is the whole point of the filing. #5664 is the closest existing issue to attach this to (its BytesMut component); #408's CRIT-1 is the wrong allocation-size profile and should NOT be the headline of the filing.
