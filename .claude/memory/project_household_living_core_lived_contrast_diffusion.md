# Household as living core + value-scanner as substrate mechanism + lived contrast as diffusion

## The chain

1. **The household is the living core.** Not one of multiple worked examples — the foundation, seed, and driver. The protocol becomes embodied at the dwelling, where care is given and received, where presence is shown up, where children learn the discipline by participating in it.
2. **The value-scanner is the substrate primitive that makes care-economy REA visible at the individual + household level.** The household's commons-elohim co-steward observes patterns of care (Tommy making breakfast; Sarah's invisible 11 hours/week of household management; Matthew's afternoon shift with a sick child) and emits these as REA events into the household's care-economy ledger.
3. **Care becomes computable, valuable, exchangeable — not through gamification but through honest observation** of what the 20th-century economic substrate cannot see. The real costs traditional economics treats as zero-value because it has no instrument to measure them.
4. **The household is the epistemic seed.** Living in the substrate at home reforms common sense — the lived experience of dignity, presence, attestation, and reflection becomes the new baseline against which everything else is measured.
5. **Lived contrast is the diffusion mechanism.** The participant encounters work, school, civic engagement, healthcare, finance — every institution from Sections 5-6 — and asks, in honest bewilderment: *"why isn't this like home?"* The asymmetry becomes intolerable not by ideology but by embodied common sense. The participant brings the substrate INTO every other institution.

## Why this matters

This is the answer to *how does the multi-decade arc actually happen?*

Not by:
- Ideological persuasion
- Top-down deployment
- Regulatory mandate
- Venture-funded rollout
- Market mechanism (requires existing demand)
- Political mechanism (requires existing authority)

But by:
- **Embodied experience** that reforms common sense
- **Lived contrast** that makes legacy institutional shapes intolerable
- **Participants bringing the substrate INTO** every other surface they encounter

The substrate spreads by being lived. Once the seed is real, the catalog dissolves itself.

## Why MVP focuses so narrowly

This is the architectural justification for MVP scope. The four Tier-0 worked examples — and especially the Dowell household — are everything. Get the seed real. The rest follows from participants bringing the substrate INTO every other institution because lived contrast makes the absence intolerable.

This is why the spec's Tier 1+2 catalog (Section 5) and Tier 3 catalog (Section 6) are post-MVP horizon, not parallel MVP tracks: they will dissolve themselves once the seed at the household is real.
