The reality: there is a real population of humans who, through nature or nurture, are structurally unable to take accountability. Not bad-faith in the moment-to-moment sense — *patterned*. The protocol's accountability mechanics (consolidation events, restitution obligations, reach-drop on bad-propagation, reconciliation requirements, quarantine of known-harm) will feel oppressive to this population.

**Disclosure must be crystal-clear, values-forward, never hidden.** This is the consent. Hiding it (softening, deferring, equivocating) would be dishonest about what the protocol *is*. The protocol takes a side. Pretending it doesn't is the failure mode.

**The frame for affected users**:
- Yes, this will feel bad to you. We're not pretending it won't.
- AND: you and everyone in your network are better off because the structure exists.
- The structure is a *safety and security measure for you* as much as for everyone else — Icarus would have flown himself into the sun if the structure of his wings hadn't constrained him; flying into the sun is what unrestrained accountability-resistance produces, and the protocol prevents that for people who can't self-correct.
- Many of the people whose well-being your patterns affect are the same people whose unrecognized contributions make your life functional. The protocol doesn't ask you to acknowledge them; it just refuses to let your refusal harm them.

**Connections to existing principles:**
- *Ungrudging service* — the protocol's gifts flow even to those who hate the structure. Resentment doesn't disqualify someone from receiving the protection.
- *Elohim as counsel* — even when a human is the source of harm, their elohim still represents them, while the structure still constrains the harm. Defense and accountability coexist.
- *No sovereignty, no ownership — stewardship instead* — accountability-resistance often masquerades as sovereignty claim ("nobody can tell me what to do with what's mine"); the protocol's no-sovereignty stance directly addresses this masquerade.
- *Stewardship philosophy — six principles* — accountability-resistant adults are another station on the cradle-to-grave stewardship lifecycle (alongside child / custody / probation / disability / elder); not failed humans, just patterns the structure must hold around.
- *Mission — subsume G/F/A via "it just works"* — credible to a grandmother whose family photos are on the line includes credible against the dangerous patterns *within* the network, not only against external attack.
- *Elohim Protocol vision — fruit back on the tree* — protocol restores flourishing; defends against harmful dynamics. Accountability-resistance is one of the dynamics the protocol is designed *against*, not in service of.

**Adversarial-by-design (load-bearing):**

The protocol is not values-neutral with strong defaults. It is *adversarial to specific participation patterns by design*. Pretending it's neutral lets harmful patterns claim the protocol owes them accommodation; being explicit about the adversarial stance strengthens the consent and frees the rest of the network from the burden of negotiating that accommodation case by case.

The values-forward disclosure makes the friction *legible*: the user can see what they're agreeing to, can decline if it's not for them, and cannot later claim the protocol misled them about what it would do when their patterns produced harm.

**Where this lives long-term:**

This belongs eventually in manifesto-tier content under `genesis/docs/content/elohim-protocol/` — likely a "what this protocol asks of you / what it refuses to negotiate" section as part of consent-to-participate language. The consolidation-events spec should reference it but the value statement is upstream of any spec.

**How to apply:**
- Onboarding flows must surface this disclosure, not bury it.
- Consolidation/restitution/reach-drop mechanisms in the substrate must include user-facing transparency about *why* this is happening — never silent enforcement.
- Resist softening language during implementation (e.g., calling restitution "reconciliation suggestions" or framing reach-drops as "discoverability adjustments"). Accuracy is the kindness.
- The Icarus framing is the recommended onboarding metaphor: the structure constrains because the consequence of unconstraint is fatal to the unconstrained.
