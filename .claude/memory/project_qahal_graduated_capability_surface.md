# Qahal as graduated capability surface

Qahal is **one primitive** with a **continuous capability curve** from commons-reach surface outward (anyone in your peer graph encounters it) to steward-tier authority inward (full governance, custody, Agreement cascading). The gating mechanism is **standing**, computed at request-time from:

- lamad attestations (Bloom-graded quizzes authored by the Qahal's stewards as governable, versionable EPRs)
- affinity signals (you keep showing up, the social nervous system metabolizes you as present)
- FeedbackSignal debits (have eaten into your standing here)
- the current rubric version (itself a Qahal-governed EPR; rubric evolution is recursive — high-Bloom-Create stewards design tomorrow's rubric)

## The political move

**No invite-only binaries.** Binary gates can be hijacked by one bad-faith insider; mastery-attested gates cannot, because the gate criteria *themselves* are governed by the collective. An outsider can only get past the gate by becoming legible to the collective's values — which is the opposite of colonization.

## Two axes, not one

- **Reach** gates content visibility/distribution outward
- **Standing** gates capability/authority inward

Both are earned, computed, revocable. Reach is per-audience; standing is per-Qahal.

## Standing decay is innate

Like memory ceremony, groups have a natural lifecycle. Standing decays via demurrage / stepping-back / abandoning unless tended as a garden. Cadence-archetype-tunable.
