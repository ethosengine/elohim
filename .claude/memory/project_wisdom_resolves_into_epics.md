Memory should resolve INTO stories — not as imagination but as compaction of detail into meaning. The Lord of the Rings doesn't expose its subtext; the richness becomes the meaning-shape that carries forward. Oral tradition of rich storytelling IS compaction. Wisdom is what's essential to the details.

**Implication for our system:** `genesis/docs/content/elohim-protocol/` (epics, manifesto, living memory narrative) is the *destination* of memory's lifecycle, not just documentation. The comet-tail of `[Memory lifecycle = comet shape]` isn't `forget` — it's `distilled-into-epic`. The memorialized core IS story. This is class #7 (wisdom) in `[Memory classes]`, but more precisely: wisdom is the asymptote the other classes reach toward via story-compaction.

**Why:** It names the missing direction in the lifecycle spec. The existing primitives (`promote`, `compact`, `merge`, `submerge/surface`, `close-interval`, `memorialize`, `forget`, `quarantine`) handle horizontal flow between memory tiers but don't capture *vertical* flow into wisdom. The user's framing supplies that vertical direction: memory → story → epic → manifesto. Compaction is the operative word.

**How to apply:**
- When designing memory tools, ask "does this serve hygiene (lateral) or compaction (vertical)?" The kit currently only does lateral.
- A future `promote-to-epic` motion is distinct from `cleanup`'s archive — it should distill cluster-of-entries into a paragraph-of-epic, not just move them
- Converge's "memorial-tier check" exists to *prevent* damage to compacted wisdom — the inverse motion (promotion into wisdom) is its complement
- The historian role (separate memory: project_historian_pattern_surface_agent.md) operates on the archive AND on the wisdom layer — it surfaces both past patterns and the compacted essence
