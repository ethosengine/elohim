The qahal collective view's layout and feature set is anchored on slide 45 of the "After the Feed: Trust, Connection, and the Next Era of Social Technology" deck (New_ Public, May 2026). The slide shows a "Bay Area Dawn Runners" mock and names five design moves we want our collective surface to embody.

**Persistent asset paths:**
- Image: `/projects/research/after-the-feed-2026-05/slide-45-bay-area-dawn-runners.png`
- Full deck: `/projects/research/after-the-feed-2026-05/after-the-feed-full-deck.pdf` (82 pages)

**Layout — composite single screen:**

- **Top:** group identity strip (community name + member avatars + role badges)
- **Right rail:** member list (~6 visible) with avatars and status dots
- **Center stage, top-to-bottom:**
  - AI-coordinator notification card with a concrete recommendation and a match score (e.g. "92% match" with a peer who fits your goal)
  - Stats row: three counters — **active members · active activity-instances · stewards**
  - Live "happening now" surface (in the mock, a map of three runners currently out; in qahal terms, the live-co-presence pane)
  - Upcoming-activity cards stacked vertically
- **Left margin:** five salmon-tinted callouts naming the design moves

**The five named features (memorize these):**

1. **Community norms are visible and persistent** — reminders of what the group has agreed to live on the surface, not buried in a settings menu
2. **AI coordinator knows your goals** — surfaces specific, contextual recommendations; not a feed
3. **Live co-presence is prioritized over metrics or a feed** — "see who's here right now" beats "see what people posted"
4. **Reputation is earned through contribution** — concrete acts ("organized 43 runs"), never follower counts
5. **Smart matching** notifies you only of members who match pace, schedule, and interests — pull, not push to all

**Why:** The deck synthesizes a thesis (trust economy + cozy-web retreat + software abundance + decentralized protocols) into one product mock that is precisely the design space [[qahal-collective-governance]], [[reach-gate-is-elohim-mediated-matchmaking]], and [[recovery-grandma-standard]] are pointing at. The five features map almost 1:1 onto qahal substrate we already have or have promised — making this slide a load-bearing reference image for the qahal pillar UX, not a generic mood board.

**How to apply:** When designing or auditing any qahal collective view (collective dashboard, household-as-collective view, congregation surface, learning circle), check that the five features are present and identifiable. Use the layout (identity strip → AI coordinator card → stats row → live-presence → upcoming) as the default composition. Slide 50 of the same deck supplies the charter quote ("intimacy of a group chat + openness of a public square + connect when it matters"). Slide 49 supplies the prosocial-principles → product-guidance crosswalk that maps directly to acceptance criteria for the surface.
