A doorway projecting EPRs in classic client→server shape for public consumption is a
**commons privilege and right afforded by the network**, not something the doorway owns.
It is earned and held under the EPR's declared reach (validated by the protocol), and the
public has **feedback available to challenge and refine that standing** — the witnessed
interaction / earned-reach machinery, not an operator switch.

**Why:** the federated doorway design (2026-09-12 ruling: doorways are the federated load
balancer; a public name is a DHT fold of projection contracts × reach × requester standing ×
liveness; multiple projections across doorways are the CDN tier) only stays coherent if the
serve itself is a governed standing. Otherwise a cache or a warm shell keeps "surfacing
through elohim protocol without its proper governance" — a projection kept alive by bytes
after the reach that permitted it narrowed.

**How to apply:** frame every doorway serving feature as a standing the doorway holds on
behalf of the commons: eligibility is derived from the DHT (contract + reach + standing +
liveness), checked at serve time, never a doorway-local allowlist or a cache of permission;
a community holon's ruling on reach must propagate to every doorway's fold; and the
public-facing feedback path (challenge/refine standing) is part of the feature, not an
afterthought. See [[project_earned_reach_governance_pr_ceremony_vision]],
[[project-eprfs-witnessed-interaction-primitive]], [[project_head_reach_freshness_semantics]],
[[feedback_framing_guards]].
