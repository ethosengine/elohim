**Operator 2026-09-25**, on the compute-agreement sprint: "I know it's not a 'real' market — this is play, and if we play, learn, and master, we will build something that holds up when this becomes the foundation for that realized market."

**Why:** the marketplace primitive (`delegates-compute` grant → `work` commitment → accept/complete events → receipt) is one shape instantiated per scope; mastering it between two agents the operator owns is the training loop for every automated and human market built later. A shortcut that makes play cheaper by skipping a primitive teaches the wrong lesson.

**How to apply:** keep the network stage at **Bootstrap** (never a declared `Simulacra`) so verification costs are paid and measured now; refuse bearer tokens standing in for grants, file receipts standing in for DHT completions, key-identity checks standing in for grant checks; label reciprocity between operator-owned agents as *training evidence*, never market evidence. Cautions about "not a real market" are not objections the operator needs raised again.
