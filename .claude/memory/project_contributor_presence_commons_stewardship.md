# Contributor-presence re-grounding — commons stewardship (design agreed 2026-07-21)

Conductor-less substrate residents (the 11 fixture humans the resilience-identity-coherence
scenario reds on) are mis-modeled as `humans` rows claiming presence. Agreed re-grounding
(gate-passed, spec pending):

- **Reuse `ContributorPresence`** (`unclaimed → stewarded → claimed`) — a substrate resident IS
  "a person not yet in the network." Slug-addressed (legitimate pre-existence identifier).
- **WitnessedAscription** = existing imagodei `Attestation` reuse (no new entry type): a real
  agent authors *about* the presence, signed with their own key — `affirm_membership`'s
  "identity is theirs from day one" discipline intact; mediated-agency (guardian co-authors)
  generalized.
- **Holder relation splits verified vs witnessed**; a2o invariant becomes
  `household_id ⇒ (agent_pub_key ∨ witnessed-presence)` — orphaned state becomes a detectable
  defect class, not the fixture default.
- **Claim = Intent opening a negotiation, NEVER an atomic transfer (2026-07-21 refinement).**
  Claiming ("I step into my stewardship of all this") convenes a wide review + negotiated
  settlement with the tending stewards — e.g. a critical open-source contribution (git → brit
  content-addressed attribution) the commons relies on: backfill negotiates against the human's
  needs/desires/limits, bounded by the constitutional frame (dignity floor, accumulation
  ceiling, graduated stewardship) — those are boundary conditions, not the answer. Settlements
  are appealable/renegotiable by construction (append-only events; corrections are new events),
  and stewardship claims are continually re-evaluated within the steward web the claimant
  exits INTO (Mishpat negotiated-consequences frame). At claim the system CONVENES and
  WITNESSES the settlement — and then execution DOES happen, in the standing shape:
  **deterministic floor** (mechanical legs execute mechanically — identity binding, membership
  stamp, event emission) · **elohim ceiling** (judgment/ceremony — agentic inference calls) ·
  **execution into the flow** (settlement lands as REA events). Never a computed payout at the
  ceiling, never judgment at the floor.

**Bootstrap mandate (2026-07-21): model the lifecycle as a2o forcing functions, within genesis.**
The 11 bench presences resolve via the presence model itself (no conductor needed — that IS
their resolution given the test bench cannot host their conductors). Plus **+1 live human
lifecycle on shem (in resource budget): gertrude joins / lives / dies** — Phase 0 presence
(witnessed, zero orphans) · Phase 1 claim (intent → convening [ceremony-stub] → floor stamps
identity, ceiling negotiates backfill [stub] → claimed, card verified) · Phase 2 lives (accrual +
one appeal-by-correcting-events scenario [stub]) · Phase 3 dies (key retirement via rotation
lineage; standing returns to commons-stewarded witnessed posture; legacy negotiated [stub]) —
presence is the symmetric cradle-AND-grave posture, per the cradle-to-grave canon.
**Ceremony-stub convention:** scenarios assert the ceremony was convened/witnessed/produced
recorded events — never outcome content; deterministic assertions around stubs are
e2e-checkable now. Story told and modeled within genesis (story-first).

**Operator steering (load-bearing, 2026-07-21):** three roles, not two — the WITNESS attests
and custodies shards; the STEWARD of the data and value flowing through presence nodes is the
**elohim-commons** (a commons pool holding standing in trust for the eventual claimant), never
the individual witness. Witnesses MAY earn incentive for holding commons shards, but
**settlement semantics are deliberately uninterpreted** — they resolve through emergent liquid
consensus(es) in the elohim constitutional values system. Do NOT define the constitution,
settlement agents, rates, or distribution rules now.

**Design constraint the spec must carry:** record facts, defer valuation — full provenance
(witness, pool, custody events, flows) as immutable REA events such that ANY future
constitutional settlement story is retroactively computable. A schema choice that forecloses a
settlement interpretation is wrong; one that merely doesn't implement one is right. Spec needs
an explicit non-goals section stating this.

**Horizon marker (operator, 2026-07-21 — noted, NOT designed):** the imagined absolute end
state is a memorial compaction — the protocol compacting the witness of a whole life from the
commons her data returned to; a "crystal" snapshot in memoriam, replayable to see her impact
on the network of the present, refreshed if humanity deems it worth holding. The
record-facts/defer-interpretation constraint + content addressing + commons-return-at-death
already point here; foreclose nothing that prevents it. Do not build toward it.
