**Why:** a gospel-tier surface (agent prompt, SKILL.md, CLAUDE.md, spec) is read for months; any
temporal claim — "as of <date>", "in flight", "Phase N closed", a live magnitude (count,
percentage) — silently ages into falsity the moment the substrate moves, and downstream agents
act on the stale claim. This slug is the canonical-discipline handle the memory-ceremony lenses
cite when flagging PROCESS-STATUS findings.

**How to apply:** assert the durable rule; a founding event may appear as a dated parenthetical
(`decided:` frontmatter, "decided 2026-06-03"), but the body states what IS, not where work
stands. Sprint-state belongs in memory topic files, timeline backlog entries, or handoffs.

Gospel home (graduated 2026-06-03, restored as a citable slug 2026-07-02): the "Gospel surfaces
describe stable architecture, not where-we-are" operating principle in
`.claude/scripts/memory-kit/CLAUDE.md`.
