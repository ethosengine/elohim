The EPR "chrome" the doorway adds around Elohim Protocol sites is **the trust signal to the
broader world**: this is a governed place; a whole system stands behind it; a fair trade is
occurring for your consumption; if there is an issue there is redress and feedback with a
near-guaranteed response; and at the level of elohim decision-making there is high-context
interpretability of the decision and the power to act. It is content, but it is also a
nearly-everyone (ultimately universal) personal concierge stewarding tailored service to
the whole commons.

**Why:** the chrome is where the doorway's commons standing
([[feedback_doorway_projection_is_commons_privilege]]) becomes visible to a person who knows
nothing of the protocol. Without it, governance is real but invisible, and a site looks like
any other server.

**How to apply:** treat these five as the chrome's affordances and the next checks for
epr-atom-home (green on slice 1, "commons next"): (1) the governance mark — reach, ruling,
holder; (2) the fair-trade receipt — REA exchange for this serve, serving credit; (3) the
redress entry ([[project-eprfs-witnessed-interaction-primitive]], accountable-correction);
(4) feedback that becomes a witnessed commitment with a due window (owed response, not hoped);
(5) an interpretability surface where the elohim explains with receipts and can act. Keep the
friend voice ([[feedback_epic_friend_voice_at_moment_of_need]]) — protocol vocabulary one
request away. When eligibility narrows, the chrome is what tells the visitor why and where to
be heard. Related: [[project_epr_atom_home_slice1_landed]],
[[feedback_valueflow_authorship_is_the_process]].
