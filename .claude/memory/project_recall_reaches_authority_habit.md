**Declared 2026-09-11** at `.epr-meta/recall-reaches-authority.habit.md`, born red, by the sprint plan
`genesis/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-11-bounded-recall-mastery-sprint.md`. It takes over the
verdict rule the memory-kit replacement had grafted onto `dev-system-equilibrium` (a stock-rate habit
that memory work could never flip): a context-reset agent with a concrete question reaches the
current authoritative source through `epr flow memory recall open --need …` alone, its question is the
intent, `.claude/` is inside the entry's scope, bytes are metered, no mistaken assertion.

**Middot / mishpat:** `recall-metered-bytes@1`, `recall-unmetered-bytes@1`, `recall-mistaken-assertions@1`,
`recall-screens-to-shape@1` and their `-ceiling` rows in `.claude/epr-meta/measures.yaml`;
`recall-unmetered-bytes-ceiling@1` renders into the `recall` SessionStart headline slot (the retired
`memkit` slot's successor). First sample folded: 14 screens, ~100 KB unmetered, 1 mistaken assertion.

**Graduation (operator ruling on the plan, 2026-09-11):** this concept graduates from eprfs to p2p when
humans search, surface and consume content and discover meaning in their communities through the same
kind of governable artifact, its mishpat and middot inspectable and interpretable, judged against human
flourishing for humans and agents of the larger network. Hence the invariants now: the algorithm is a
content-addressed EPRFS artifact (`.epr-meta/elohim/algorithms/recall-contract.json`) with declared
authorship and change authority; bounds are declared rows, never code constants; measures fold as
re-derivable observations; every first screen prints its selection rule and omissions.
