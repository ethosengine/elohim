Canon: `lamad.md` "Exit with Elohim" (decompose → integrate into the graph → generate a personal
path; entities become ContributorPresences) and its meaning maps. What 2026-09-27 adds is the
formation-import framing. Operator, while recomposing FCT: a "course" like FCT is a semester's worth of
content, and that depth is the point. Customizing for a learner needs a deep **cloud** of
content that can be mapped against the cloud a person has already consumed and understood
through the lens of their imagodei. This is the long-term goal for all content in the
protocol.

"You are what you read"; "first we shape the medium, then the medium shapes us." Our
epistemic formation happens largely *through* the EPRs that formed us. A person arrives at
the protocol already formed; importing their YouTube history, Facebook likes, posts and so
on lets the protocol help them see **who they are and what shaped them**, and find where they
stand in relation to everything else in the network. Intentional design of the medium is
intentional design of formation.

**Why:** personalization is a join between two graphs, the network's content graph and the
person's formation graph. A path an elohim composes is only as good as the join points.

**How to apply:** `recompose.py` (FCT) is Exit-with-Elohim step 1 done by hand. When authoring or importing content, make the atoms that a person's history
could match *first-class and correctly identified*: books, films, talks and podcasts typed as
what they are with clean titles (the FCT media retype, 2026-09-27), scripture keyed by passage,
concepts shared across courses rather than course-prefixed. Prefer fine-grained atoms with typed
edges over monolithic pages. Keep imported history private (intimate reach) by default; it is
the person's formation, revealed to them first. Related: [[paths-are-walks-over-epr-graph]],
[[fct-v2-recomposition-intent]], [[feedback_private_thought_governed_fruit]].
