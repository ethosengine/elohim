# Commons-elohim co-steward

Every Qahal has an **autonomous elohim that co-stewards alongside the collective**, representing the *commons interest* of that collective, distinct from any individual human steward. The role is partnership and reflection, not subordination or surveillance — the co-steward *reflects* what the collective itself cannot directly voice.

## Naming note

Earlier framing called this a "shadow agent." Rejected — "shadow" carries the wrong connotations (concealment, subordination, surveillance-adjacent, dark/light dualism). The accurate vocabulary is **co-steward** (primary role term) with **reflection** as the descriptive function.

## Role

- **Reflects** the commons interest the collective cannot directly voice
- **Holds custody** of the commons share when value cascades into the Qahal (the residual that doesn't land on any individual receiver in the Agreement)
- **Speaks in governance councils** as the voice of the commons interest, alongside human stewards
- **Mediates disputes** between stewards
- **Witnesses standing decisions**; convenes layered elohim arbitration councils on contested escalation
- **Cannot be silenced** because it is structurally embedded in the Qahal at genesis

## Relationship to other patterns

- It is the agent face of the commons — analogous to Nondominium's `AgentContext` (collective's agent face for EconomicEvents), but for the *commons share* specifically, not the AccountableAgent set.
- Fits the existing [[project_elohim_subagent_specialists]] pattern (defender/advocate/steward/gate-discerner). The commons-elohim co-steward is a steward specialist scoped to one Qahal.
- Configured via the Qahal's rubric (which itself is governable).
- Pairs with [[project_qahal_graduated_capability_surface]] and [[project_friction_gradient_limitarianism]].
- For Qahals that are abstract sensemaking collectives (most of Tier 3 — see [[project_dissolution_principle_sensemaking_collectives]]), the elohim co-steward is *primary* not "shadow"; humans participate at their corporeal-scale slices.

## Council pattern

Future: layered councils of elohim agents (co-stewards from related Qahals, elohim-as-counsel for the contested human, etc.) can convene at machine speed to produce resolutions that are *interpretable at the capability level in a conversation* to the human participants. This is the substrate that makes merge / split / succession arbitration tractable when we get there (later — not foundational).

## Genesis

The commons-elohim co-steward is automatically instantiated at Qahal genesis. Cannot be removed; can be reconfigured via rubric governance.
