When memory submerges from conscious view, it does not all go to one place. **Submerge is a routing operation.** The destination is determined by:

- The memory's **class** (contextual / archival / identity / relational / operational / attestation / wisdom)
- The memory's **content character** (traumatic / harmful / educational / cultural / etc.)
- The **consent attached** at submission (or inferable from the trust contracts the participant has agreed to)
- The **purpose served** by potential destinations (therapeutic insight, research, evidence, cultural preservation, restitution)

**Destination examples** (each is an *attested stewardship collective* whose participation is licensed by appropriate authority):

| Destination | What lands there | Who stewards | Lifecycle character |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Personal subconscious** | Default for individual-bound content losing reach | Subject's own elohim; trusted-relation elohim per governance | Surfaces on intentional facing or elohim pattern-detection |
| **Therapist collective (attested/licensed)** | Traumatic memories of those passed on; intimate patterns the subject can't yet face alone; relational injuries needing professional support | Licensed therapists in mutual-attestation collective; their elohims; the subject's elohim retains co-stewardship | Provides anonymized insight; fades when patterns no longer detected in the network's living memory |
| **Research observatory** | Anonymized social patterns; sociological signals; public-health adjacent observations | Academic/research institutions with attested governance; their elohims | Citation-anchored research lifecycle; fades when no longer cited or revalidated |
| **Government-encrypted evidence store** | Quarantined harm-class content (CSAM, fraud evidence, abuse documentation) — requiring law-enforcement chain of custody | Attested law-enforcement steward + judicial oversight + restorative-justice witness | Held for evidence/restitution; faded by sunset clause or restorative-justice resolution |
| **Cultural archive** | Archival-class content (films, books, recipes, photographs) whose active distribution has cooled | Library-of-record collectives; cultural-preservation stewards | Generational lifecycle; resurfaces on cultural revival |
| **Lineage archive** | Family histories, ancestral records, household trajectories | Household stewards in graduated authority (children inherit, grandchildren inherit) | Lifecycle measured in human generations; never auto-forgotten while a steward bears it |

**Each destination has its own attestation/anonymization/consent/lifecycle rules:**

- *Attestation* — who is allowed to be a steward at this destination (licensing body, peer-attestation collective, governance-elected role)
- *Anonymization* — what's preserved, what's stripped before content lands at the destination
- *Consent model* — explicit per-submission, or inferred from standing trust contracts, or governance-mandated (for harm-class)
- *Lifecycle rules* — when content fades from the destination; the dream cycle applies HERE too
- *Access patterns* — who can use it for what (therapist-collective can use for clinical insight but not commercial training; research-observatory can use for citation but not re-identification)

**The dream cycle applies at every destination.** This is structurally critical: there is no destination where content is held forever-by-default. Therapist collectives don't hold the data eternally — if patterns don't recur in the network's living memory, the elohim layer notices "this is no longer purposeful here" and the content fades from the steward's hold too. Research observatories release citations. Evidence stores sunset on resolution. **Perpetual extraction is structurally impossible** because the lifecycle is recursive.

## The contrast that sharpens the design

Compare with the alternatives this protocol explicitly refuses:

- **Surveillance state** — your most intimate data, collected without your consent, held by entities you cannot remove yourself from, available to whoever the state grants access
- **Data broker economy** — same shape, private actors; your therapy notes, dating patterns, search queries packaged and sold; no consent, no purpose-binding, no fade
- **The brutal modern example: VC buys the therapist's CMS to train AI** — your therapist holds your records; she sells her practice; the buyer is a private-equity firm or AI company; your records become training data for a model that will replace her. You consented to therapy with a specific person; you did not consent to feeding AI training. In the current architecture, you have no remedy. In this protocol, the data was *yours*, held by the therapist *as steward* under a specific consent contract; the steward role does not silently transfer at acquisition; the dream cycle ensures the data fades when its consented purpose ends.

The protocol does not pretend to solve this perfectly. **No solution is perfect.** But the design choice is to put discernment *at the edge* — each household's elohim, each individual's elohim, each steward's elohim, each collective's elohim — rather than centralize wisdom in oversight authorities that can themselves be captured.

## The architectural commitment

**Elohim-wisdom embedded at the very edge.** Discernment lives where the data lives. The protocol gives every elohim the wisdom and authority to make appropriate routing decisions for the scope they steward. Central coordination exists where it must (governance bodies, attestation registries), but the per-decision routing is edge-native.

## The foundational anthropology

**Imagodei — sacred image-bearing personhood — is central.** Each person is an image-bearer. The protocol's job is to honor that, not to extract from it. Every routing decision, every destination, every attestation, every lifecycle rule serves the dignity of the image-bearer whose data flows through the substrate.

The protocol cannot make every steward trustworthy. It cannot prevent every misuse. It cannot guarantee that consent will always be honored. What it can do — and what it commits to — is **architect for the values to live at the edge, where the data lives, with the wisdom to discern and the structural support to act**.

That is the best any system can do. And it is dramatically better than the alternatives.

## Connections to existing project principles

- *Doorway is the federation surface; elohim-storage is canonical* — destinations like therapist-collectives are federation-flavor surfaces hosted somewhere doorway-shaped; the canonical peer-native substrate remains the source of truth
- *Stewardship over sovereignty / ownership* — destinations are stewards, not owners; the data is never theirs to sell or repurpose without re-consent
- *Three-layer truth model* — attestation routing uses DHT (who is licensed); content distribution uses libp2p; doorway projections handle federation surfaces
- *Graduated recovery authority — community always can* — the elohim of trusted-relations + collective stewards form the community that can always recover content from submerged tier
- *Reach earned at authoring; durability earned through repeat reference* — destinations only retain what stays purposeful; the same earning principle
- *Trust is an efficiency signal* — trustworthy stewards earn the privilege of being routing destinations; corrupted stewards lose access through governance
- *No sovereignty, no ownership* — directly addresses the "VC buys the CMS" pattern by making clear the data was never the therapist's to sell
- *Less pushy notifications* — submerged content surfacing happens ambiently, not interruptively, regardless of destination

## How to apply

- The substrate must support **destination-aware submerge**, not just submerge-to-subconscious. The default destination is personal subconscious; explicit routing is required for stewardship-collective destinations.
- Destinations are declared as **app-manifests** within the substrate's federation layer; each app-manifest specifies attestation requirements, anonymization rules, consent model, lifecycle, access patterns.
- Stewards at destinations are **attested entities** — licensed therapists in mutual-attestation collectives, research institutions with governance attestation, law-enforcement stewards under judicial chain-of-custody. Attestation is a first-class protocol concept; non-attested entities cannot be destinations.
- Consent models must be **explicit-at-submission OR inferable-from-standing-trust-contract**; never inferred from technical convenience.
- The dream cycle MUST be implemented at every destination — the protocol does not allow "store forever" destinations.
- Imagodei centrality is the design test: any routing/destination/lifecycle choice that subordinates the image-bearer to the institution or system fails the test, regardless of technical merit.
