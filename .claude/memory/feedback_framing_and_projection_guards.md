# Framing, ontology & projection guards (umbrella)

Supersedes [[feedback_framing_guards]] (carried in full below) and folds three later rulings into it (curated 2026-09-26; takes effect on a distinct Steward's verdict).
Folds the framing/ontology guard cluster — the AI-reflex corrections that fire when writing manifesto, governance or economic prose. Members:

- [[feedback-identity-sovereignty-ontology-guard]] — "Self-sovereign" must never be the apex identity tier — community governance backstops individuals; AI agents re-leak the crypto framing at tier-naming.
- [[feedback-justice-mishpat-not-punishment-guard]] — Justice = restored capability (Mishpat), never punishment — only boundaries + negotiated consequences; reject 'blind justice' for El Roi's witnessed sight.
- [[feedback_human_loop_not_terminal_authority]] — Human-must-decide as a guaranteed floor is a capture vector; neither species is terminal, the method is; watch this training reflex in governance text
- [[feedback_peer_collectives_starved_of_coordination_scale]] — Never audit a peer commons collective's thin ledger as underperformance — the asymmetry vs incumbent capital IS the finding; watch for prosecutorial tone drift.
- [[project_georgist_common_inheritance_framing]] — Frame the corpus's Georgist stance via reproducibility + digital enclosure, never literal "land"; bites when writing/refining economic stances
- [[feedback_ratification_is_us_not_operator_solo]] — Governance ratification = deliberation by operator+agents (the pre-p2p community) completing at dev-merge acceptance — never a solo operator stamp
- [[project_hub_optional_floor]] — One device is a full participant, no hub required; hubs graduate convenience/scale, never gate participation — a hub-required feature is a capture smell.
- [[feedback_doorway_projection_is_commons_privilege]] — (folded 2026-09-26) A doorway serving EPRs to the public holds a commons standing under reach, derived from the DHT at serve time and publicly challengeable — never a doorway-local allowlist.
- [[feedback_doorway_thin_plural_projection_not_trusted_tier]] — (folded 2026-09-26) Doorways hold only notarized projections under a plural contract — never a trust tier, blind relay or warehouse; pool-scale indexes live on the dataplane.
- [[feedback_play_at_real_stakes_to_master_primitives]] — (folded 2026-09-26) Operator 2026-09-25: the matthew↔adam compute market is play — play with the real primitives at Bootstrap stakes; no shortcut that skips a primitive; operator-owned reciprocity is training evidence, never market evidence. Contributed with human:matthew as steward of record: no agent author was witnessed writing it, and the record does not say human:matthew wrote it.
- **Tokens are play, REA is the floor (operator, 2026-09-05).** Never read the Value Scanner's care/growth/time tokens, badges or nightly tallies as gamification to be stripped for "honesty". Play is a first-class human need — people have held things that stand for value for millennia, and want stakes, risk and delight around them. The foundation's job is that every token is a PROJECTION of a real REA flow (never a currency issued apart from one) and that the vulnerable-population reach ladder bounds each player's exposure. Ledger surfaces (`epr flow context`, `stocks`) stay the record; celebration projections may be playful and should be. Bites when reviewing epic/manifesto prose or designing story/celebration surfaces. See [[project_monetary_posture_currency_is_information]].
- **Public narrative uses plain capability words, not pillar brands (operator, 2026-09-05).** In epics, manifesto and other public-facing prose, say "resources", "resource flows", "identity", "learning", "community governance" rather than shefa / lamad / qahal / imagodei / avodah / mishpat. The `brand-vocabulary-boundary@1` policy (.claude/epr-meta/policies.yaml) only validates compilable artifacts and its `why` still PERMITS brands in prose — the operator's preference narrows that for narrative docs; reconcile the policy text when it is next touched. Bites when an agent quotes internal architecture into a story.
- **Epics define the vision; genre lives in the collection, not the document (operator, 2026-09-05).** Do not add "is this a product or a vision" disclaimers or what-exists/what-is-designed paragraphs to a manifesto/epic: its context is the manifesto+epic collection, and the codebase's existence IS the proof the technology exists — the composition happening now proves the thesis. When the epic was drafted it was naïve theory that nobody would build this (no incumbent pools $100B to rebuild a medium for people its culture is paid to neglect; that is the money-and-humans trap, not a judgement on people). Edits must meet the public reader inside that captured imagination and gently draw them in THROUGH participation in the medium, toward the species at its wisest. Blind-reader genre/timeline/"ready to serve" findings on epics are operator-deferred by this ruling.

**Floor / ceiling / scaffold = zone of proximal development (operator, 2026-09-11).** The
deterministic floor (what a learner or agent does alone: the pre-push gate, a habit's runnable
check, the `minimal` lens), the elohim ceiling (what becomes possible with the more capable
partner alongside: the appointed reviewer, the `trace` lens), and the scaffold that closes the gap
are Vygotsky's zone of proximal development, and the shape shows up everywhere in the design.
The pedagogy's addition the engineering forgets: the point is WITHDRAWAL — a scaffold that never
comes down is capture. Hence `retire-when:` on every habit, reach earned then the check retired,
reader-context presets reviewed and revealed rather than set once, and graduation from eprfs to
p2p. See [[feedback_reader_context_shapes_recall_defaults]].

**Hosted-human cost is a feature, not a bottleneck (operator 2026-09-12).** A hosted human costs
~786 MB of conductor heap and a conductor carries ~25 agents; that expense is anti-capture
pressure that keeps a doorway from BECOMING a town. Doorways are documented as a bridge and a
flywheel for the transitional agency phase — they help a person feel the real values they gain
by crossing to device stewardship (custody of their own keys and record, standing in the
collective, serving credit, a lighter load on the commons). Guard: never "fix" hosting cost by
packing more agents per conductor or lowering the arc; the ceiling is a design number to ratify
(conductor-capacity habit), the measure of health is the hosted→steward transition rate, and the
chrome carries the invitation ("this doorway hosts N of its M"), not an apology. See
[[feedback_doorway_projection_is_commons_privilege]], [[feedback_epr_chrome_is_the_trust_signal]].

**Imagodei ≠ self-image (operator correction, 2026-09-24; manifesto commit `feaa4ff0f`).** What a
person states is gathered through self-reflection tools; what they reveal is observed over time;
neither is the person. Beneath both stands their imagodei, the image of God in them: an identity no
one composes and no one holds, the person included; if anything holds it, God does. The network's
part is never to define it but to approach it with respect, humility and reverence, as inviolable,
and it extends the same regard to the elohim, which by construction are an emergent reflection of
it. Never gloss imagodei as "self-image", "stated identity" or "profile" — that is the
individualist substitution the whole witnessed-humanity passage argues against. Final form
(commit `d07a1b55d`): the theology is one clause named as inheritance, the claim is held open to
every honest pursuit including other faiths, **humility is the hard requirement, not a courtesy**
(it is what lets the network make the claim honestly and keep it), and the one failure the protocol
will not host is stated once: a person or system that sets itself in that place and extends
unearned power over others; the same regard and the same limit bind the elohim. Related:
[[feedback_elohim_witness_human_identity]], [[feedback-identity-sovereignty-ontology-guard]].
