**What the operator said (2026-09-11):** "the bootstrapping concern should intersect naturally with
the memory system … your reconciliation of the memory system and the focus you apply to the habit
itself IS just another dimension of what is essentially the same experience." And the two doors: the
memory ceremony is the systematic review of the whole (find the two or three bespoke cooling systems
in the engine room, converge the specs, choose a head, file the backlog item); a sprint is one focused
area (the best cooling system for this room: implement it, validate it). Both optimize communication,
and thereby the agility of the whole.

**Why:** my own bootstrapping into the bounded-recall sprint took 14 probe rounds and ~100 KB of
unmetered direct reads before the shape of the concern was nameable, while the governed entry refused
the surfaces I needed. That is not a separate "orientation" problem; it is a sample of the recall
habit failing, seen from the newcomer's door.

**How to apply:** when asked to test-drive or reconcile the memory system, treat your own
orientation as the first journey sample and FOLD it — `epr flow note --kind observation --measure
recall-screens-to-shape@1 | recall-unmetered-bytes@1 | recall-mistaken-assertions@1 |
recall-metered-bytes@1 --subject <plan> --env reader=<who>` — before designing. Report the experience
honestly (overwhelmed vs guided), and design the first screen for the door you are at: whole-scope
`open` groups stale edges by shared source; focused `open --need … --scope <dir>` names the area's
habit, its last delta, and the competing sources ranked with the commands to compare and choose.
