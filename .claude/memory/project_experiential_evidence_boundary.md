# Prove the experience across runtime boundaries

A passing command repeatedly concealed an unobserved boundary: the binary tested was not the binary running; a listed peer could not read; a ready service served stale application bytes; a release was verified but nobody adopted it. The durable lesson is to identify the intended experience, then account for each boundary between the proof and the person relying on it. Do not promote a narrower success into delivery.

These are historical witnesses and diagnostic routes, not declarations of today's fleet state. Before carrying a dated conclusion forward, consult the owning scenario, habit, current configuration and a fresh scoped observation. Missing or held observations leave acceptance unestablished.

## Follow the boundary that the evidence has not crossed

**Build → running process.** Inspect the executable, its feature set and the process that actually owns the port. A green branch gate can build a different cargo slot; a restart can preserve captured environment or target a stale pid. [[project_local_mesh_binary_slot_and_restart]] preserves procfs capture, slot, watcher, fixture and restart failures, including the September 8 branch-slot mismatch. Its dated command recipes require checking against today's launcher.

**Ready/listed → participating and reading.** Check the declared roster before counting peers; service readiness does not establish gossip. [[project_alpha_topology_bootstrap_pair]] preserves the six-versus-seven census error and bootstrap responsibilities. [[project_sovereign_peer_t3_rung_traps]] distinguishes transport-lineage incompatibility from absent authorities and preserves the September 6 fork extraction recipe. [[project_household_space_partition_blocks_and_round_deadline]] shows why increasing a round timeout did not repair a cell blocked after post-close writes: causal diagnosis had two layers.

**One endpoint → the claimed scope.** [[project_alpha_substrate_probe_rails]] records doorway-specific backend routing and why failed log queries are not zero observations. Read both sides when making a cross-doorway claim; name the endpoint, peer and space each observation covers.

**Passing harness → exercised product.** [[project_app_pipeline_e2e_ghost_and_apex_seed_gate]] preserves a swallowed Cypress failure, held scenarios and a stale-but-200 host. [[project_alpha_auth_portal_baseline_2026_09_04]] records actual registration returning an existing human under DEV_MODE. Scenario counts, browser requests and observed identity matter; a pipeline's success caption cannot supply missing experience.

**Transport success → complete recovery.** [[project_pure_iroh_parity_and_mesh_traps]] preserves the sequence from document sync without row projection to peer-neutral reconciliation, including 258-second and later roughly 60-second warm-recovery observations. These witnesses do not establish cold recovery, all population sizes or every custody path; the original note retains the residuals and instrumentation limits.

**Published/verified → adopted and accepted.** [[project_adoption_ceremony_mesh_traps]] preserves local release prerequisites, lineage refusals, baseline rollback hazards and why evidence belongs to the release actually resolved. [[project_workspace_to_fleet_first_crossing_2026_09_06]] preserves the later transition from fleet verification to canary application, the wire-address mismatch, target-relative appliesTo and single-role candidate discipline. Observe, canary and apply are different behaviors; one canary's receipt does not speak for its observers.

## Reconcile without erasing the experiment

Record what was actually attempted, on which bytes and environment, the resulting observation, and the claim it supports. Retain contrary evidence and remaining conditions. The ten linked notes stay intact as forensic records; this umbrella replaces their competing presence in the context head, not their content or discoverability. Use the relevant diagnostic route above to retrieve the full witness before acting. Independent experiential acceptance remains a separate judgment from verification.
