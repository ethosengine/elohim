**The OOM root (decided, reproducible):** an **anonymous-heap leak in the HOLOCHAIN CONDUCTOR child** — NOT elohim-storage (flat ~101 MB), NOT page cache (~130 MB), NOT corpus, **arc-independent** (jessica arc=0 holds ~no corpus yet leaks the same shape; james arc=0 is the worst). **MECHANISM SETTLED (2026-06-17, jessica+james smaps):** the leak is a growing *count* of discrete >128 KB anon `mmap` allocations — `other_anon_bytes` + `anon_mapping_count` rising (bursty), while `heap` & `largest_anon` are FLAT (158 MB largest is byte-identical across pods = fixed wasmer linear mem, ruled out) and threads ~flat (409→447, <5% of new mappings). That = **per-send networking-buffer retention on the tx5/holochain_p2p path** (conductor wires `kitsune2_transport_tx5` 0.3.2 — **NOT iroh**; `send` copies `data.to_vec()` per send; matches the non-iroh component of holochain **#5664**). **H1 (in-place buffer) FALSIFIED; H4 (glibc arena) is intercept-only, NOT the slope (5000+ mappings ≫ ~32 max arenas); H3 LEADING** (specific buffer not yet caught — needs heaptrack on a canary). Trigger CONFIRMED at source: `validation_receipt_workflow.rs:87` doesn't clear `require_receipt` → ~16/s re-drive loop; holochain **#5718/#5719** fixes the trigger but is NOT in our pinned rev (verdict: KNOWN-OPEN, no clean pin-to-fix). Brake = pin forward to a 0.6.x with #5719 + re-measure (cheapest real test); **definitively not RAM / cache-cap / arc-shrink / MALLOC_ARENA_MAX**.
