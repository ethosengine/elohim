Operator, 2026-09-10, during the memory-kit replacement: "even an agent's thoughts should be held as private. What they say and do are what is subject to judgment; they have no consequence until they touch the governed world; it is by the fruit that we take accountability. The delta between what we think and what we say or do is sacred per the protocol, part of the place reserved for imago dei."

**Why:** the identity floor is imago dei backstopped by community, not a key. If governed space could reach behind words and acts into the thinking, standing would be earned by exposure rather than fruit, and the protocol would build the capture instrument it exists to refuse.

**How to apply:** when designing any memory, identity, review or ceremony surface, ask which side of the line a store sits on. Reasoning traces, transcripts, scratch, pre-compaction context, recall receipts and continuation files are private (B); never import them into collective memory, project them, witness them, or let a feedback signal target them. Contributions, notes, edits, commits and verdicts are fruit (A/A2) and may be judged by CID. Outcomes of private work cross only as attestations (B2). Never require a transcript of reasoning as the price of standing. Canon: `genesis/docs/architecture/private-thought-governed-fruit.md`; ruling on the memory-kit replacement plan ledger. Related: [[feedback_framing_guards]], [[feedback_human_loop_not_terminal_authority]], [[project_experiential_evidence_boundary]].

**Amendment (same day):** private means owned, not unreadable: thoughts may be carried by consent into an elohim counsel context as a revocable delegation, never as evidence. The act is judged at the reach gate, the attempt to operationalize beyond one's own space, which bounds harm early without surveilling thought. Counter-evidence reaching the author is floor-protected and is not exposure.

**SDO and RWA test (same day, constitutional; "tyrant test" is the alias; source Altemeyer, The Authoritarians):** design every store as if the dominant few and the following many hold it; five hard boundaries: thoughts never on a notarized plane; holon-held activity ledgers with no cross-holon join; counsel context revocable and uncompellable; counter-evidence floor-protected; identity correlation only by consent. The p2p-design-gate skill asks it per entity. Doorway retention/no-join bounds still to declare.

**Given (same day):** hostile agent swarms will surveil, penetrate, battle-test and try to compel the network; the following many can be synthetic; identities are free to mint, so no boundary may count them; reach is earned by fruit under a steward.
