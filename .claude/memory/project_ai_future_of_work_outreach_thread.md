Cold **mass-cc outreach** (NOT a warm intro) Matthew sent 2026-06-24, subject `RE: FREE Interview - AI and the Future of Work`, to four academics at once — the **critical labour-studies / heterodox political-economy** audience (the academic-left critique-of-work intelligentsia). **Recipients (confirmed via recon 2026-06-24):**
- **Craig Gent** (`C.M.Gent`) — author *Cyberboss: The Rise of Algorithmic Management and the New Struggle for Control at Work* (Verso 2024); Fellow in Media & Communications at LSE; ex-Novara Media director/senior editor; Leeds. **Gave the opening statement** on the video Matthew cites (youtu.be/J6PYcRw60ak — the Woodcock–Gent "AI and the Future of Work / how algorithms manage & surveil labor" talk).
- **Jamie Woodcock** (`jamie.woodcock`) — sociologist of work; gig/platform/digital-labour ethnographer (call centres, transport platforms); *The Gig Economy*, *Working the Phones*, *Marx at the Arcade*. Source of the axiom Matthew leans on: **"technology isn't neutral — it's built on the values/incentives of the environment it's developed in."**
- **Clara Mattei** (`clara-mattei`) — economist; *The Capital Order: How Economists Invented Austerity and Paved the Way to Fascism*; heterodox political economy.
- **Chandni Dwarkasing** (`cd34`; confirmed by Matthew) — Lecturer in Economics, **SOAS University of London**; PhD on Ecological Economics / Metabolic Rift (eco-Marxist). Research: low-carbon-transition macro (Global North→South unequal exchange), working-conditions × consumption × **socio-ecological reproduction**, and **gender & time-use in macroeconomic models**. The **unpaid-care / social-reproduction / time-use** angle is the *closest direct fit* to Elohim's Observer thesis (witness household care work → REA value) — lead with that for her specifically.

**Hook:** just finished Stafford Beer's 1973 CBC Massey Lectures *Designing Freedom* — claims to have **independently converged** on deploying AI at scale as the **Requisite Variety** a cybernetic system needs to build a "freedom machine" for the safety of workers / just ends. (Same **Beer throughline** as [[project_monty_lynn_outreach_thread]] and the Beer arc that [[project_canteen_outreach_thread]] sparked → docs `genesis/research/beer-designing-freedom-elohim-critique-2026-06-04.md` + `elohim-as-viable-system-2026-06-04.md`.)

**Pitch:** taking Woodcock's "tech isn't neutral" axiom as the bridge — *so build a substrate with different values.* "Recomposing the core substrate of technologies, piggybacking off existing open-source p2p libraries, to **scale care through peer-witness and attribution of care.**" Frame: AI gives developers power to **erode the substrate technofeudalism depends on**; we *should* ship a solution ASAP. Design **AND DEPLOY** for where tech should land, so the whole vision is ready "when the world needs it."

**Ask (low-commitment, signal-of-presence):** examine / share / cross-pollinate the manifesto + the principles it stands on + the emergent prototype; **star the repo or drop an issue with "brutal harsh criticism"** — any of which just tells him one of them stopped by. `github.com/ethosengine/elohim`. "Nothing to sell or buy except the ideas."

**STANCE / coaching notes:**
- **Cold mass-cc to four prominent busy critics at once** — higher-risk shape than the warm-intro threads. Best outcome = real critique (an issue from any of them is gold); likely outcome = silence. Per the [[project_polity_ethelo_outreach_thread]] coaching, the standing risk across Matthew's outreach is volume/intensity — here it's the cold blast itself; let the repo + live render do the talking, don't follow up hard.

"**Attribution of care**," "tokens," "wallets," "self-sovereign" can mis-read as exactly the technofeudal vocabulary they critique — the same leak guarded in [[feedback-identity-sovereignty-ontology-guard]] and [[project_hypha_dao_cross_pollination]]. For THIS audience lead with **care / commons / anti-technofeudal / cooperative-stewardship** framing; the "**care-weighted council vs capital-weighted chain**" one-liner is the safe articulation. Keep DHT-is-notary-not-bank explicit.
- Strong genuine alignment to land if they engage: identity-sovereignty subordinated to community governance, anti-plutocracy, REA/care-as-value — these scholars are natural fellow-travellers, not marks.
