[steward of record: human:matthew] Operator, 2026-10-27 (steward-space design session):

> *"H7 attributed to matthew (me) is correct.. everyone else here is part of our genesis test-fixtures.. We need to be able to test everything in a simulacra as if it were the mature network, but respect the boundary that it's me.. and a bunch of agents and this network right now."*

**The fact.** On the REAL network the only human is matthew.
- Claude and Codex sessions are his delegates acting under his device grant.
- Attributing their acts to `human:matthew` as principal is correct. The agent persona, session and device are provenance, not separate economic parties.
- Every other human (Jessica, James, the household cast, collectives, council members) is a genesis test fixture.

**Why:** a design review (the challenger's H7) read principal-attribution as capture and proposed stripping the human from agent records. On the real network that would erase the one true party and make the agents look like independent actors with standing they have not earned.

**How to apply:**
- **Real network:** keep `human:matthew` as provider or principal on agent records. Record the agent as `agent:<role>@<model>` provenance plus session.
- **Simulacra:** the mature network is exercised here, at the declared network stakes stage (`elohim-storage/src/trust/stage.rs`), and must never leak into real device state. That covers many humans, persona-level attribution, collectives, councils and k-of-n. The first-class seam is backlog `device-plane-fixture-vs-real-stage-seam`.
- **What changes this:** persona-level economic attribution becomes real only when a second real human enrolls, or an agent persona earns standing (operator decision: delegates now, standing earned later).
- **Every design and review checks both sides:** does it hold on the real network of one, and is it testable in Simulacra as if mature?

**Guidestar, still TBD (same session):** *"we still want to be accountable.. even to ourselves. If someone donated $100 to our efforts (I want this to go to the developers/dependencies/and inspiration of this work.).. those contributions should honestly init the pools and attribute to the nascent contributor presences.. that honesty is our network attractor, and is a part of our epic."*
- Attribution has two axes:
  - who ACTED: the principal (matthew), with agents as provenance;
  - whose work it BUILDS ON: dependencies, upstream projects and inspirations, as contributor presences that are unclaimed or stewarded until claimed.
- Inbound value initializes the commons pools and flows along the contribution edges. The steward never encloses it.
- The only near-term requirement: artifacts and attestations carry their upstream contribution edges (depends-on, cites), so value is traceable later. Do not design the flow yet.
