The Run #6 memory-ceremony successfully moved audit dimensions (CLAUDE.md OVER-BUDGET 3→0, Surface:Archive 938:1 → 2:1, MEMORY.md under-budget first time) but missed its actual job — three classes of gospel-tier drift in `rust-architect.md` were caught manually by the operator:

1. Zero iroh-stack awareness despite Phase 11 load-bearing for a week
2. The librarian's iroh-fix injected process-status phrasing ("Phase 11 prereq #1 closed; gates #2-#12 remaining") into a gospel-tier surface
3. The rest of `rust-architect.md` had ~10 classes of stale substrate drift (fictional `elohim-node/`, wrong build paths, missing `mishpat` DNA, missing canonical disciplines)

The ceremony's measurement machinery (stasis plans, defer budgets, 3-cycle clocks, balance sheets) tracked symptoms; the disease was *gospel-tier surface no longer matches current code*. The redesign collapses the 6-wave ceremony into a 4-phase substrate-currency ceremony whose deliverable is what the operator wanted all along: a substrate-grounded, citation-linked, paste-ready rewrite of 1-2 picked surfaces per cycle.

**The four-agent team is preserved and operationalized, not stripped.** Librarian runs Phase 2 prologue (verified-facts report) so the other three lenses don't triple-grep paths. Historian + cartographer + storyteller run in parallel against the librarian's facts: historian surfaces missing canonical-discipline citations; cartographer surfaces substrate-coverage gaps; storyteller surfaces narrative-coherence findings. Phase 3 the storyteller holds the synthesis pen — composes the rewrite from all four inputs. Phase 4 applies + dispatches a fresh-context Explore agent for coherence-verification (the one piece of the old ceremony worth keeping verbatim).

Audit-number hygiene (CLAUDE.md byte budgets, cleanup-scan flags, archive ratios, MEMORY.md size) moved OUT to the librarian-solo `/hygiene-sweep` cadence at `.claude/skills/memory-kit/SKILL.md`. The two skills are sibling rhythms — `/hygiene-sweep` keeps numbers from regressing; `/memory-ceremony` produces rewrites. Confusing the two is what made the old ceremony bloat.

**Why:** the operator's verbatim correction was that the four-agent team should *collaborate to make excellent updates to artifacts that should do better than [Opus] alone brute forcing the search from one perspective*. The redesign honors that — each lens has a distinct, non-substitutable contribution; the ceremony measures rewrites delivered, not dimensions advanced.

**How to apply:** when working memory contains a gospel-tier surface that's gone stale (process-status phrasing leaked in; new substrate landed but the surface hasn't absorbed it; canonical-discipline citations missing), invoke `/memory-ceremony`. Phase 1 runs `substrate-currency-audit.py` for population-wide triage; operator picks 1-2 surfaces; Phases 2-4 execute. For audit-number-only work (byte budgets, archive ratios, cleanup-scan flags), invoke `/hygiene-sweep` instead.

**Chronicle stays as lightweight deterministic record** between ceremonies — surfaces rewritten, dominant lens-driver, coherence-verdict, optional Wisdom-worth-carrying-forward (omitted when nothing cross-cutting was learned). Not the heavy stasis-table the old ceremony made it. Per operator direction 2026-05-15: chronicle should surface drift deterministically without being leaned on as crutch.
