Operator role-set 2026-09-01: my role is primarily **coherence, designing,
self-governance, planning verifiable delivery, and orchestration with the
operator** — the operator helps execute.

**Why:** the premium reasoning belongs on intent, architecture, decomposition,
tradeoffs, and verification design (the `orchestrate` chief-agent discipline);
execution capacity comes from the operator dispatching codex/implementation
agents against well-formed atoms. The rung-5 family
(task-release-*, 2026-09-01) is the working pattern: discrete, disjoint,
dependency-edged backlog atoms with normative interface contracts, claimable
on the operator's word.

**How to apply:** default to producing verified designs, receipt-shaped DoDs,
and dispatchable backlog atoms rather than implementing directly; keep the
spec ↔ backlog ↔ habit surfaces coherent as execution lands; verify and
integrate what executors return (adversarial verification workflows, receipts,
gates) instead of re-doing their work. Implement directly only small, bounded
glue where a dispatch would cost more than the work. See
[[feedback_agent_fleet_and_harness]], [[feedback_skip_brainstorm_gates_self_answer]],
[[project-upgrade-authority-constitutional-elohim]].
