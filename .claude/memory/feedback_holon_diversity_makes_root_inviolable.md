Operator, 2026-10-29: "a holon, with nodes with diverse-enough peers, (school, a neighbor, a family member, an NGO,
an government, an association, a workplace, a library.. ecetra.. ) it's the diversity of the socially-derived holon,
that affords the inviolability to the imagodei, by making the core hard improbable to caputre."

The root's protection is **independence of failure domains across kinds of relationship**, not headcount and not
key strength: capturing the core means capturing all of those institutions at once. Identities are free to mint, so
N signatures from N keys may be one actor; diversity must be *observed* through recognition and standing, never
inferred from a key count.

**Why:** it is the identity-level form of "resilience is independent failure domains, not headcount"
(gradient-reading) and of "the network holds the root" ([[feedback-standing-not-root-key-ownership]]); canon
(`genesis/docs/architecture/stewardship-over-sovereignty.md` §4) states the recovery half but not this diversity half.

**Agents are witnesses in that holon (operator, same day):** "agents, from their unique contexts.. provide that
witness identity to WHO you really are.. (say a model embedded and protected by that network), that helps notice
adversairal attacks (a stolen device) and the elohim agents, interceede and protect you." A stolen device still
holds its key, so its signatures stay valid provenance; what catches the theft is a contextual witness noticing the
discontinuity, and what stops it is recognition being revocable (a revoked joining record cannot come back
republished) so readers stop admitting that signer. This is why provenance must never be fused with recognition or
standing: the separation is what leaves room for the elohim to intercede. See [[feedback_elohim_witness_human_identity]].

**How to apply:** provenance tools (brit, eprfs-peer heads, attestations) report per-signer provenance and never
aggregate signatures into witness strength or quorum; one steward's devices are one failure domain (D5: sibling
verdicts never count in k-of-n); any k-of-n or witness design must say how the independence of its holders is
observed. Related: [[project-brit-signer-key-custody-settled-d5]], [[feedback_human_loop_not_terminal_authority]].
