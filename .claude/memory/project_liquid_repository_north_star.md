Operator, 2026-11-04, on what the native dev plane is for: "imagine a liquid repository.. while
you're walking through the tree of code.. every epr-meta root module COULD come with a changing
social-context as to who cares for that component... a button to launch a lvi workspace, where the
repo just reveals and knows exactly what the compute requirements are... and just start deving on
that space at your own private reach.. where you can be invited almost anywhere in source code to
participate in the evolution of code. Stuff doesn't collapse to a repository address anymore."

**Why:** it is the experience that the derivation plane, reach graduation and REA/mishpat-derived
stewardship exist to serve. It is the onboarding moment of [[feedback-master-peer-compute-before-hardware]],
seen from inside the code tree.

**How to apply:** no new primitive is needed. Each part is a read:
- the component is an `.epr-meta` root;
- who cares for it is projected from mishpat commitments;
- the compute needs come from method descriptors plus the projected view (the sparse checkout) plus
  envelope demand;
- the button is lvi's seed materializing that view at `self` reach;
- an invitation is a reach grant scoped to one subtree.

Design consequence: key view declarations by `.epr-meta` root, not only by gate step (spec §1a,
track A). Related: [[project_earned_reach_governance_pr_ceremony_vision]], [[project_elohim_native_devspace_runtime]].
