# The dissolution principle + sensemaking collectives as the primary domain of the elohim

## The general rule (diagnostic)

At full deployment of ubiquitous scalable wisdom and intelligence, ask: **what role does this thing play?** If it persists primarily because of a **friction-of-information, friction-of-value, or friction-of-governance** that the substrate now resolves, it has become **vestigial**. It does not vanish; it transforms into an **abstract sensemaking collective** — a Qahal-shaped projection that coordinates where the dissolved institution previously coordinated through its institutional shape.

Three diagnostic questions:
1. Does the function exist because of an **information friction** the substrate now resolves? (Credentialing, journalism, certification, regulation, peer review.)
2. Does it exist because of a **value friction**? (Banking, brokerage, insurance, escrow, settlement.)
3. Does it exist because of a **governance friction**? (Government, regulatory agencies, court systems, corporate management, legislation.)

If yes to any → becomes an abstract sensemaking collective primarily stewarded by elohim councils.
If no to all three (function exists because of irreducible human relationship at intimate scale — care, fellowship, dwelling, ritual, presence) → remains a human-stewarded Qahal with elohim shadow.

## What the protocol does NOT have

There is no "government," no "banking," no "lawyers," no "insurance," no "police," no "courts," no "schools-as-state-apparatus," no "corporations" in the 20th-century institutional sense. What exists is the **three substrate currents** — **Story** (attestation, mastery, narrative, identity, recognition), **Value** (REA flows, mutual aid, commons share, agreements, restitution), **Governance** (qahal rubric, commons-elohim councils, peer mediation, friction-gradient enforcement) — flowing through nested commons-stewardship Qahals.

## Sensemaking collectives as transitional bridges

During the (generational) transition, sensemaking collectives bridge legacy institutions to substrate primitives. Four phases: **Interface → Subsumption → Absorption → Retirement.** Doorway-pillar engineering is largely the construction of these interfaces. The endpoint is dissolution of the institutional shape; the function persists as substrate.

## The substantive content of "the apex"

**These abstract sensemaking collectives are the primary domain of the elohim.** This gives substantive content to the apex named in [[project_elohim_councils_capture_apex]]. The apex is NOT a void filled by friction-gradient. It IS the layer of abstract sensemaking collectives, and elohim councils hold it.

## Division of stewardship at scale

- **Humans steward what is irreducibly corporeal.** Households, life-groups, neighborhood associations, local congregations, intimate workplace coops, body-scale care. Section 4 Tier-0 worked examples + Tier 1+2 archetypes are predominantly here.
- **Elohim councils steward what is irreducibly abstract.** Bioregional natural collectives, mutual aid pools at planetary scale, cross-Qahal coordination, dissolving institutional functions, the planetary-boundary ceiling of the donut endstate. Section 6 Tier-3 items are predominantly here.

The friction-gradient prevents either layer from colonizing the other. The Imago Dei discriminator (see [[project_qahal_graduated_capability_surface]] §1.5) ensures the elohim-stewarded abstract layer never violates the inherent dignity of beings in the human-stewarded corporeal layer.

## Why this matters architecturally

- The catalog in qahal-architecture-vision spec Sections 5/6 is a **map of functional surfaces the substrate absorbs**, not a list of Qahal-shaped versions of current institutions
- "Banking" doesn't appear in the catalog because the function is fully distributed across shefa + venture coops + mutual aid + commons-elohim custody
- "Government" doesn't appear because the function distributes across municipal Qahals + transportation infrastructure + natural collectives + justice + education + commons-elohim council apparatus
- The MVP delivers the human-stewardship layer (Tier-0 worked examples); post-MVP horizon delivers the abstract sensemaking-collective layer where elohim councils are primary
- **Multi-decade arc:** humans coming home to corporeal-scale collectives while wisdom comes home to the abstract layer humans were never built to hold
