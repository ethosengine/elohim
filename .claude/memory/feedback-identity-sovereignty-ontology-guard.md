The Elohim identity ontology (imagodei pillar) treats a human's identity as imago dei — an inviolable right **backstopped by institutional/social expression**, not a self-asserted crypto primitive. The architect has repeatedly had to fight "self-sovereign / true data sovereignty" framing from leaking into the substrate vocabulary. AI coding agents default to the silicon-crypto sovereignty ontology (overrepresented in training data) and it slips past the design gates specifically at **tier-naming**: an apex tier called "self-sovereign" reads as a neutral capability level ("more keys → more autonomy → higher") rather than the ontological claim it actually is, so it sails through review that would catch a load-bearing assertion. As of 2026-06-22 the drift is live in the repo: the imagodei agency ladder tops out at "self-sovereign (keys on device, full autonomy)" and `hardware-spec.md` celebrates "true data sovereignty" — both contradict the intended ontology.

**Why:** Sovereignty-without-community is illusory; real freedom/agency/security come from community and institutions backstopping the individual. Unqualified self-sovereignty also can't model the whole human life — children, IDD, seniors, legal wards — where the right is exercised *with/through others* (the repo's own IDD design says "supported decision-making, NOT substituted judgment"). Hardened cryptographic sovereignty is extended only in rare, justified cases (e.g. targeted dissidents under state duress) — the exception, never the apex default. This is the architect's core differentiator vs. the crypto/"sovereignty" space, and the pitch to governance-minded collaborators (e.g. Ethelo) leans on it.

**How to apply:** Never introduce or celebrate "self-sovereign" / "sovereignty" as the positive top of an identity/agency gradient. Frame the high-autonomy tier as community-grounded (e.g. node-stewardship standing) and keep sovereignty subordinated to community-adjudicated governance. Quoting sovereignty as an *adversary frame* (the thing being resisted) is fine; asserting it as the protocol's own apex value is the drift to catch. The corrected apex lexicon is the architect's call — confirm before bulk-renaming.
