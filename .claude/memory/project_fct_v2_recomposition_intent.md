On 2026-09-23 the FCT course v2 (fifteen modules, five movements, Micah 6:8 refrain) landed
in `genesis/docs/content/fct/fct-course/` with the built site + `.docx` packed as the
`fct-course` html5-app node. The operator calls that zip **the teacher's curriculum
workbook: the source material in which all the other EPRs compose.** The `fct-module-*`,
`fct-bible-*`, `fct-media-*` nodes are still the 2025 v1 decomposition.

**Why:** The course pre-dates the AI turn; the Elohim Protocol IS the author's answer to the
course's open question (Modules 14–15 "build the lab"). But the answer must be *revealed, not
given* — that is why lamad was built first. The medium is the message: a learner meets
"Christ knocks and does not autoplay" inside a medium that does not autoplay, Joash's
transparent chest inside a notarized ledger, the Body-as-complex-system inside a graph with
no center. A zip in an iframe is the course *wrapped* in the protocol; the v1-style
decomposition is the course *spoken by* it. So re-decomposition is the point, not a chore.

**How to apply:** When the operator says "do FCT right", the shape they named is:
multi-media EPR recomposition — images and figures; epr-links to references (videos via
`video-embed`/iframe); a Sophia exercise per piece of content (learning objectives →
mastery; discussion rounds → reflection; Mind the Gap → discovery). v2 structure that
becomes edges rather than prose: movement → chapter; anchor vs supporting verse
relationships; story → narrative node; three lanes → practice nodes keyed to audience
(tech worker / household / church leader); cross-module callbacks ("bring your Module 1
note to 6, 8, 9") as real links; the Micah refrain and Acts 2 "home" as concept nodes
threaded across modules. The workbook node stays as the printable/facilitator form every
atom cites as source, never the thing the path leads with. Open question the operator
hasn't answered: whether lamad ever points at its own floor (a Module 14 moment) or stays
silent and lets it be recognized. Constraint: seed-slug epr-links drift silently and the
seal tool is DEFERRED ([[project_epr_link_first_class_seed_authoring]]); FCT stewardship
routes with the church collective ([[project_collective_topology_author_stewards]]).
