The corpus's monetary position (2026-08-24, `genesis/research/monetary-posture-internal-currencies-external-fiat-2026-08-23.md` §2.9 + `succession.md`):

**Currency is the information that moves in an exchange, not the token carrying it** (*Rethinking Money*). So **REA is already full of currency, held in abstract** — recorded before anything is priced. Capture resistance is therefore **layering, not absence**: every denomination is a revocable reading of an un-denominated record that outlives it. Composes with Beer, already the paper's frame — money "attenuates the full state space of human contribution down to a few priced channels."

**"The protocol issues nothing / no currency and will not have one / empty on purpose" was WRONG and is retired. Do not re-assert it.** It contradicted Stance I.4, which names *"the **private** issuance of money"* as unearned increment graduating to a commons owned by no one — canon **socialises** issuance rather than abolishing it, so permanent refusal forfeits what canon vested in the commons. Also acquitted by §2.2 (a mandate-bound instrument reabsorbed on completion never accretes, has no holder, is not a store of value) and forbidden by §2.4's own register rule (prohibition is right *only* for substrate invariants).

Current shape: **N1 refuses only a unit held as property.** Commons issuance is **precondition-gated (P1–P6, five unmet)**, formed as **policy lanes over commons pools**, with councils deliberating **at arm's length from the human valueflows** — no beneficiary on the authorising body, which is the whole anti-enclosure argument. It mints nothing; minting would convert a refusal-to-foreclose into a roadmap item.

**Known honest limit, conceded in-argument:** the architecture prevents **memory capture** (the record being erased) but *not* **functional capture** (one denomination becoming the one everyone actually uses). A railway track outlives any train; a railway monopoly is still a monopoly. That detector does not exist.

Hayek is met, not waved past: the price's compression *is* its power, and that argument is untouched because **no calculation is attempted** (`succession.md` §5.4). Prices coordinate; records remember.
