**Decision (operator, 2026-09-23).** The eprfs actor plane admits the human as a participant kind
(`human:<handle>`, claimed by the human like an agent claims; never derived from git, never an email).
Acts on collective-memory contributions (author/edit/review/retract/graduate) become append-only
events projected into the tracked store (`<id>.acts.jsonl`); `author` is derived from the first act
and frozen; reviewer independence reads the set of everyone who shaped the body. The substrate never
copies the operator's email into fruit (250/251 tracked contributions did on 2026-09-23).

**Attribution bounds declared (development-stage, revisable when a second human witnesses):**
rent vs labor computed from the cite graph with retention factor `r = 1/2` (rest flows upstream to
the `.epr-meta` anchor as sink); within retained, witnessed acts only, judgment verbs weighted equal
to production verbs; agents' share flows to the collective's pool, the model vendor is provenance
never a payee (already paid at market out of the operator's pocket). No share is realised.

**Why:** operator saw `author` flip between agents on every re-import ("burying stewardship and
affinity to objects in git history") and named the human "a load-bearing imagodei". A discipline
rehearsed on a substrate that misreports provenance graduates with the misreport in it.

**How to apply:** spec `genesis/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-human-participant-actor-plane-design.md`
(gate output, 6 stations); habit `acts-attributed-to-participants` (`.epr-meta/`, red, 3 grep
checks). Station 1 claimed by orchestrator@fable-5-1 with a bootstrap-honesty note. Never propose a
fixed human/agent ratio — propose a bound and a derivation. See [[feedback_private_thought_governed_fruit]],
