Operator 2026-09-26, after one session asked for a tier registration, command permissions, an
offer verdict, a steward-of-record import and three memory-fold verdicts: "these approvals are too
tedious … we can't be asking for approvals constantly … humans don't want to be reading the TOS."

**Why:** consent asked constantly degrades into rubber-stamping, and a rubber stamp is capture
wearing governance. The distinct-Steward primitives stay; what must change is how often a human
is asked.

**How to apply:**
- Rely on the harness permission classifier for command-level gating. Don't pre-ask for what it
  would allow.
- When a governance primitive needs the human's verdict, gather every same-kind pending verdict
  and ask once with a recommendation, never item by item.
- Surface a single verdict on its own only when it crosses the network (settlement, offers to
  consumers we don't own), touches identity or keys, or can't be reversed.
- The protocol-level fix is standing delegations as governed Agreements: the backlog item
  `affirmative-consent-classifier`.
